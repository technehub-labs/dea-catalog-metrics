## 2. Definition

Meeting Hours per Employee per Week (MHPE) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking digital workplace & collaboration. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Meeting Hours / Employee Weeks; the result is stated in hours. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures time spent in meetings, typically from calendar data. It is the bluntest instrument against the coordination-cost epidemic, and often the first metric leaders act on because the number shocks. Interpret carefully: meeting hours are inputs, not outcomes, and some meetings are the work. Slice by role and meeting type, and pair with decision quality evidence. Read with time loss to bureaucracy and decision cycle time.

## Formula

```
Meeting Hours / Employee Weeks
```
