## 2. Definition

Within Semiconductor Manufacturing (Deep Dive), Equipment Effective Uptime - SEMI E10 (EEU) is used to gauge the utilization or availability of capacity. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Productive Time / Total Time, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Semiconductors.

## Formula

```
Productive Time / Total Time
```
