## 2. Definition

Within Entertainment Venues & Attractions, Local Resident Discount Uptake (LRDU) is used to gauge growth over time. It is computed as Discount Tickets Sold to Locals / Local Capacity, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## Formula

```
Discount Tickets Sold to Locals / Local Capacity
```
