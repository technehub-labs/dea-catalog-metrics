## 2. Definition

Partner-Initiated Pipeline Ratio (PIPR) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of alliances & partnerships management reporting. The formula is Qualified Pipeline Originated by Partners / Pipeline - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how much of the sales pipeline originates with partners. Partner-originated pipeline is the leading indicator of ecosystem value. Pipeline inflated by double-registered self-sourced deals is theater. Read with channel partner productivity and pipeline coverage ratio.

## Formula

```
PIPR = Qualified Pipeline Originated by Partners / Pipeline
```
