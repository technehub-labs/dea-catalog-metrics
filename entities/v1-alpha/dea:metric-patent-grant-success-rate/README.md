## 2. Definition

Patent Grant Success Rate (PGSR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of intellectual property & licensing reporting. Its calculation is Patents Granted / Applications Prosecuted, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of applications that mature into granted patents. Grant rate reveals both filing quality and prosecution discipline. Success on narrow, easily-cleared claims can be defensive theater. Read with patent portfolio growth rate and patent citation impact.

## Formula

```
Patents Granted / Applications Prosecuted
```
