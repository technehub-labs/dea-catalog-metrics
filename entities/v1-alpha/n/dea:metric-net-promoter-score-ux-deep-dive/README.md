## 2. Definition

Net Promoter Score (UX) - Deep Dive (NPSUXD) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of ux & design systems reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Promoters minus Detractors among Product Users; the result is stated in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures user willingness to recommend based on experience quality specifically. UX-attributed NPS separates product experience effects from brand effects. Low-response-rate surveys over-represent the delighted and the furious. Read with usability test success rate and customer effort score.

## Formula

```
NPSUXD = Promoters minus Detractors among Product Users
```
