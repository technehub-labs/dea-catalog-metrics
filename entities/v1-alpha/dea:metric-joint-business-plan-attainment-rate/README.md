## 2. Definition

A Alliances & Partnerships Management indicator, Joint Business Plan Attainment Rate (JBPAR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Partner Plans Meeting Agreed Targets / Plans; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures achievement of the co-authored annual plan with each strategic partner. Joint plans are how alliances convert goodwill into commitments. Attainment measured on activity (meetings held) rather than revenue is nominal. Read with partnership revenue contribution and alliance health score.

## Formula

```
Partner Plans Meeting Agreed Targets / Plans
```
