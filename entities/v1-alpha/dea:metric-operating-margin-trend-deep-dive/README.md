## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard - Financial Perspective indicator, Operating Margin Trend (Deep Dive) (OMT-D) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Change in Operating Margin over Periods - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the direction of operating profitability over time. Margin trend reveals whether growth is scaling efficiently or being consumed by cost growth. Mix shifts toward low-margin business can hide within a stable average. Read with revenue growth rate and gross margin stability index.

## Formula

```
Change in Operating Margin over Periods
```
