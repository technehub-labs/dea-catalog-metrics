## 2. Definition

Key Reporting Timeliness (Investor) (KRTI) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of investment risk & middle office operations reporting. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Its calculation is Client Reports Delivered by Deadline / Reports, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Investment management practice.

## Formula

```
Client Reports Delivered by Deadline / Reports
```
