## 2. Definition

Bias Remediation Closure Rate (BRCR-AI) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai reporting. The formula is Bias Findings Remediated by Deadline / Findings - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of identified bias findings actually remediated within target timelines. Remediation is where fairness programs succeed or stall: findings without closure accumulate into documented knowledge of unfairness, which is the worst position for any organization - aware and unresponsive. Root-cause remediation (data, features, thresholds) differs from cosmetic patching. Read with algorithmic bias audit coverage (Deep Dive) and post-remediation fairness re-measurement.

## Formula

```
Bias Findings Remediated by Deadline / Findings
```
