## 2. Definition

Employee Training on ESG (ETSG) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Sustainability & ESG domain. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It is defined as Employees Completing ESG Training / Required; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Employees Completing ESG Training / Required
```
