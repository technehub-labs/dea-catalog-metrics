## 2. Definition

Pre-Open Readiness Completion Rate (PORCR-T) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of investment research & trading operations reporting. It is computed as Trading Systems Ready by Pre-Open Deadline / Systems, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Trading Systems Ready by Pre-Open Deadline / Systems
```
