## 2. Definition

A International Trade & Customs indicator, Documentary Compliance Rate (Trade) (DCRT) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Shipments with Complete Trade Documentation / Shipments - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Logistics & Trade sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Shipments with Complete Trade Documentation / Shipments
```
