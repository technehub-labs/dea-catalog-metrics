## 2. Definition

Franchisee Satisfaction Index (Deep Dive) (FSI-D) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of franchise operations (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Franchisee Survey Composite Score - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures franchisee sentiment on support, economics, and system direction. Franchisee satisfaction is the leading indicator of royalty performance, litigation, and system growth - dissatisfied operators under-report, churn, and dissuade candidates, and field support quality drives it. Anonymous surveys with adequate response rates are the only credible measure, since named surveys suppress candor. Read with franchise agreement renewal rate (Deep Dive) and field-visit program quality.

Franchisee Survey Composite Score
