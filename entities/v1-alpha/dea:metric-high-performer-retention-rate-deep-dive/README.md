## 2. Definition

A Enterprise OKR & Performance Management indicator, High Performer Retention Rate (Deep Dive) (HPRR-D) quantifies the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. The formula is Top-Rated Employees Retained / Top-Rated - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures retention of top-rated talent specifically. Losing high performers at a higher rate than the average signals a value problem in the core population. Ratings inflation narrows the gap the metric should reveal. Read with critical talent retention rate and regretted attrition rate.

## Formula

```
Top-Rated Employees Retained / Top-Rated
```
