## 2. Definition

Employment Rate of Graduates (ERG) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Education domain. The formula is Graduates Employed within Period / Graduates - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Education sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

The share of graduates in employment, conventionally measured at a fixed window after graduation and increasingly at the center of government-led employability metrics and ranking systems that made it the outcome accountability metric of higher education. Its strategic significance is the market test: it is the metric that connects the institution's promise to observable post-graduation reality, and in several regimes it now carries funding consequence, which has reshaped curriculum design toward employability data. The honest measurement is far harder than the headline: destination-response bias (employed graduates respond more), the quality-of-employment question (a rate that includes any employment says nothing about skill match), and further-study counting conventions all move the number materially. Read with time-to-first-employment and salary data, which convert the binary rate into the value question.

## Formula

```
Graduates Employed within Period / Graduates
```
