## 2. Definition

Citizen Trust Index (CTI-PS) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of public sector & citizen service standard measures (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Survey-Based Composite of Institutional Trust, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Public sector.

## 3. Context

Measures public trust in the institution's services and fairness. Trust is the asset every public mandate depends on. Index scores from own surveys drift toward politeness; independent polling is more honest. Read with citizen satisfaction and complaint resolution timeliness.

## Formula

Survey-Based Composite of Institutional Trust
