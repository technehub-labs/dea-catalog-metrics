## 2. Definition

Strategic Goal Attainment (SGA) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking strategy & portfolio. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Goals Achieved / Strategic Goals Set; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

The share of stated strategic goals achieved by their target dates - the accountability metric of strategy itself, closing the loop between the strategy document and observed reality. Its strategic weight is the mirror it holds up: consistently high attainment suggests goals were set to be attainable rather than ambitious, consistently low attainment suggests either execution failure or goals-as-theater, and the diagnostic work is deciding which. The measurement discipline that makes the metric honest is goal operationalization - goals stated as measurable outcomes with owners, baselines, and dates at the time they are set, not reverse-engineered at review time. Read with the goal-mix analysis: firms reliably over-attain financial goals and under-attain capability and culture goals, which reveals where measurement infrastructure exists and where aspiration goes unaudited.

## Formula

```
Goals Achieved / Strategic Goals Set
```
