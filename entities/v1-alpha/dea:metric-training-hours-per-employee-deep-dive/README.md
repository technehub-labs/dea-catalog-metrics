## 2. Definition

Training Hours per Employee (Deep Dive) (THPE-D) measures capability maturity or development in the Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective domain. Its calculation is Total Training Hours / Employees, measured in hours. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
THPE-D = Total Training Hours / Employees
```
