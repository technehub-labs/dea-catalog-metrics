## 2. Definition

A Compliance Monitoring & Testing indicator, Regulatory Penalty Exposure Ratio (Deep Dive) (RPER) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is computed as Penalties Paid and Accrued / Revenue, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the scale of potential penalties relative to capacity to absorb them. It converts scattered compliance weaknesses into one board-level risk number. Ratios depend on penalty models that are inherently speculative. Read with compliance risk assessment coverage (Deep Dive) and risk appetite threshold utilization.

## Formula

```
Penalties Paid and Accrued / Revenue
```
