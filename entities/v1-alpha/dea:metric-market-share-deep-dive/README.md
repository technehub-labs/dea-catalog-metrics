## 2. Definition

Market Share (Deep Dive) (MSDD) captures growth over time for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Entity Sales / Total Addressable Market Sales; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the company's share of the addressable market. Share is the scoreboard of competitive position, and trend matters more than level. Market definitions that redraw the denominator flatter the number. Read with market share trend (Deep Dive) and relative market share.

## Formula

```
Entity Sales / Total Addressable Market Sales
```
