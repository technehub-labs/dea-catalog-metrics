## 2. Definition

Within Financial Planning & Analysis, Long-Range Plan Revision Frequency (LRPRF) is used to gauge governance. It is computed as Major LRP Revisions per Year, expressed in times per period. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how often the long-range plan is materially revised. Both extremes are diagnostic: never-revised plans are fiction, constantly-revised ones are weather-vanes. Revisions triggered by genuine strategy shifts differ from forecast-chasing. Read with scenario analysis coverage and forecast accuracy (Revenue).
