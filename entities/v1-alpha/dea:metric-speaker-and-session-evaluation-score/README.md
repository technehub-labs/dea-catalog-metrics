## 2. Definition

Within Events & Exhibitions, Speaker and Session Evaluation Score (SSES) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Average Session Rating, expressed in score (points). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment.

## Formula

```
SSES = Average Session Rating
```
