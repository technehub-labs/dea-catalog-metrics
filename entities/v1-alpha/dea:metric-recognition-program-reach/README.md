## 2. Definition

Recognition Program Reach (RPR-EE) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking employee engagement & culture (deep dive). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Employees Recognized in Period / Employees, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Employees Recognized in Period / Employees
```
