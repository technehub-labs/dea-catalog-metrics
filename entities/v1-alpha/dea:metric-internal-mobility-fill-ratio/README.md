## 2. Definition

Internal Mobility Fill Ratio (IMFR) addresses the retention of customers, users, or assets over time and is a fixture of talent acquisition deep dive reporting. It is defined as Roles Filled Internally / Roles Filled; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of open roles filled by internal candidates. Internal fills preserve institutional knowledge and signal credible career paths. Ratios kept high by blocking external competition stagnate capability. Read with internal mobility rate (Deep Dive) and succession ready-now ratio (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Roles Filled Internally / Roles Filled
```
