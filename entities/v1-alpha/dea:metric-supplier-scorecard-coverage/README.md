## 2. Definition

Supplier Scorecard Coverage (SSC-SS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking strategic sourcing & supplier performance (deep dive). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Strategic Suppliers under Scorecard / Strategic Suppliers, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Strategic Suppliers under Scorecard / Strategic Suppliers
```
