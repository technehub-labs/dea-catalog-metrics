## 2. Definition

Order Accuracy Rate (Deep Dive) (OAR-D) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking restaurant operations (deep dive). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Orders Correct on Delivery / Orders, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Restaurants.

## Formula

```
Orders Correct on Delivery / Orders
```
