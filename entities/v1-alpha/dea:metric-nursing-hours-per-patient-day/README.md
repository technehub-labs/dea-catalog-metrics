## 2. Definition

Nursing Hours per Patient Day (NHPPD) addresses the utilization or availability of capacity and is a fixture of medical practice & clinical operations reporting. The formula is Nursing Care Hours / Patient Days - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector.

## Formula

```
Nursing Care Hours / Patient Days
```
