## 2. Definition

Brand Lift (Ad Recall) (BLAR) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. The formula is Exposed Recall minus Control Recall - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the increase in ad recall or brand awareness attributable to campaigns. Lift isolates the brand effect from baseline awareness. Lift surveys among exposed-but-unconverted audiences understate full effect. Read with brand preference score and share of search (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Exposed Recall minus Control Recall
```
