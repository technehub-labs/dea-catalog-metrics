## 2. Definition

Gig Worker Satisfaction (GWS) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Gig & Contingent Workforce domain. The formula is Satisfied Gig Worker Survey Responses / Responses - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures satisfaction among platform gig workers. It matters because gig satisfaction drives supply reliability on the platform. Surveying only active workers during high earnings periods skews scores high. Read with Gig Worker Retention (Repeat Providers) and supply coverage to confirm satisfaction translates into supply.

## Formula

```
Satisfied Gig Worker Survey Responses / Responses
```
