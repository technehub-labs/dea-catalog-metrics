## 2. Definition

A Corporate Communications & PR (Deep Dive) indicator, Reputation Risk Radar Coverage (RRRC) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Risks Monitored / Identified Risk Landscape; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of material reputation risks under active monitoring. It extends risk management beyond financial risks into the sentiment and social-media layer where reputations now form and break in hours, and coverage gaps are where surprises originate. Coverage must include stakeholder-specific listening (regulators, employees, key accounts), not just news media. Read with issue-escalation drill performance and share of positive coverage trends.

## Formula

```
Risks Monitored / Identified Risk Landscape
```
