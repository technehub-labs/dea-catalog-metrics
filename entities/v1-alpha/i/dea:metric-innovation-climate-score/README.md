## 2. Definition

Innovation Climate Score (ICS-BSC) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Survey-Based Innovation Culture Score; the result is stated in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether employees believe ideas are welcomed, resourced, and safely pursued. Climate is the cultural soil: no innovation process outperforms its climate. Safe-survey conditions matter; scores under identifiable conditions drift optimistic. Read with continuous improvement idea implementation rate and psychological safety score.

## Formula

```
ICS-BSC = Survey-Based Innovation Culture Score
```
