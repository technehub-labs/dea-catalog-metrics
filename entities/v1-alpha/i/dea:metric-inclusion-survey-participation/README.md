## 2. Definition

Inclusion Survey Participation (ISP) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion domain. The formula is Survey Responses / Survey Invitations - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
ISP = Survey Responses / Survey Invitations
```
