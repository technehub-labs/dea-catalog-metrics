## 2. Definition

IT Spend as Percent of Revenue (Deep Dive) (ITSPR-D) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of it financial management & technology budgeting reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Total IT Spend / Revenue, expressed in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures total technology spend relative to revenue, benchmarked against industry peers. It frames technology as an investment envelope rather than a cost to be minimized. Low spend can mean efficiency or decayed infrastructure; the metric cannot tell you which. Read with run versus change spend ratio and technology debt remediation progress.

## Formula

```
ITSPR-D = Total IT Spend / Revenue
```
