## 2. Definition

A Knowledge Management (Deep Dive) indicator, Duplicate Content Ratio (DCR-KM) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Redundant Articles / Total Articles - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Redundant Articles / Total Articles
```
