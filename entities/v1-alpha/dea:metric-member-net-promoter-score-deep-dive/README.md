## 2. Definition

Member Net Promoter Score (Deep Dive) (MNPS-D) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of fitness & wellness (deep dive) reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Member NPS Survey Result, expressed in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Health & Fitness.

## 3. Context

Measures member willingness to recommend the facility. In a market with low switching costs, NPS is the leading indicator of both retention and referral-led acquisition, which is the cheapest growth channel in fitness. Location-based clubs should read NPS against cleanliness, equipment, and staff-connection drivers, not the aggregate score. Read with member retention rate (Deep Dive) and review-platform ratings.
