## 2. Definition

Graduate Satisfaction Score (Deep Dive) (GSSDD) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking education & training outcome standard measures. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Exit Survey Composite of Graduates; the result is stated in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Education, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures graduate-rated satisfaction with their education retrospectively. Graduate satisfaction predicts alumni engagement, referrals, and giving. Surveys at graduation capture relief, not long-term judgment. Read with graduate employment rate and alumni giving participation rate.

Exit Survey Composite of Graduates
