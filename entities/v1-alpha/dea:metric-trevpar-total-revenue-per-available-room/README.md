## 2. Definition

TRevPAR - Total Revenue per Available Room (TRevPAR) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of hotel & resort operations (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Total Revenue / Available Room Nights, expressed in currency per unit. Its use is concentrated in Hospitality. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

TRevPAR extends RevPAR beyond rooms: total revenue (rooms, F&B, spa, events) divided by available rooms. It matters because hotel profitability is increasingly ancillary, and a property can have mediocre room revenue but outstanding TRevPAR from its other outlets. Unlike RevPAR it captures the whole asset's earning power, which is why operators of full-service and resort properties run the business on TRevPAR while using RevPAR for the rooms division alone.

## Formula

```
Total Revenue / Available Room Nights
```
