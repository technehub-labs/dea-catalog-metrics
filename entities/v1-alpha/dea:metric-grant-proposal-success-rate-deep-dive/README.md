## 2. Definition

Grant Proposal Success Rate (Deep Dive) (GPSR-D) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of nonprofit & donor operations (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Grants Awarded / Proposals Submitted; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Nonprofit, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of submitted proposals funded. It is the yield metric of institutional fundraising, and it signals both pipeline fit (applying to the right funders) and proposal quality, which need different fixes. Win-rate optimization through avoiding stretch applications can quietly narrow funding diversity - concentration in few funders is the risk. Read with funder concentration risk and pipeline coverage against funding needs.

## Formula

```
Grants Awarded / Proposals Submitted
```
