## 2. Definition

Within Fitness & Wellness, Personal Training Attach Rate (PTAR) is used to gauge growth over time. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Members with Active PT Contracts / Members, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Fitness & wellness practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of members using paid personal training. It matters because training attach is the primary margin-expansion lever beyond dues. Aggressive sales pushes can attach members who churn quickly after one package. Read with Member Retention by Cohort and Training Session Utilization to confirm attach economics are durable.

## Formula

```
Members with Active PT Contracts / Members
```
