## 2. Definition

Share of Voice (Deep Dive) (SOV-D) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of advertising & media buying reporting. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Brand Impressions / Total Category Impressions - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Media & Entertainment sector.

## 3. Context

Measures a brand's proportion of the category's total advertising or media exposure. It is the input metric of competitive advertising strategy, often paired with market share under the rule that sustained excess share of voice drives share growth. Exposure measures without attention weighting overstate voice in cluttered environments, and digital SOV definitions are notoriously elastic. Read with share of search, brand lift, and market share for the causal chain.

## Formula

```
Brand Impressions / Total Category Impressions
```
