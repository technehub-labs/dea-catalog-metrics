## 2. Definition

A Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures indicator, Market Penetration Rate (Deep Dive) (MPRDD) quantifies growth over time. It is computed as Users of Product / Total Potential Users, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of the addressable population that are active customers. Penetration frames the remaining headroom for the category strategy. Addressable-population definitions drive the number more than sales do. Read with market share (Deep Dive) and customer base growth rate.

## Formula

```
Users of Product / Total Potential Users
```
