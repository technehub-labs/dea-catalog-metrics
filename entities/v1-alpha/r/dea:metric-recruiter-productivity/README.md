## 2. Definition

Recruiter Productivity (RPR-S) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Staffing & Recruiting domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Placements per Recruiter per Period - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Staffing & recruiting sector.

## 3. Context

Measures placements or revenue per recruiter. It matters because it benchmarks the staffing firm's core production economics. High productivity can reflect cherry-picked easy requisitions rather than skill. Read with Time to Placement and Repeat Client Business to confirm productivity is sustainable and quality-backed.

## Formula

```
RPR-S = Placements per Recruiter per Period
```
