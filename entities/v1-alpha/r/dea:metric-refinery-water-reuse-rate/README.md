## 2. Definition

Within Oil & Gas Downstream & Refining, Refinery-Water Reuse Rate (RWRR) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. It is computed as Reused Water / Total Water Withdrawn, expressed in percent (%). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Oil & Gas.

## Formula

```
RWRR = Reused Water / Total Water Withdrawn
```
