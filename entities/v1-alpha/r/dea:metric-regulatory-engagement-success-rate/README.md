## 2. Definition

Regulatory Engagement Success Rate (RESR) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of regulatory affairs & compliance operations reporting. It is computed as Favorable or Neutral Outcomes / Engagements with Regulator, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures outcomes of formal engagement with regulators - applications, approvals, interpretive relief, license changes - against attempts. In regulated industries, regulatory engagement is a production function, and this is its yield metric. Success driven by over-defensive positioning (never asking, never refused) is fake perfection, so weight by materiality of requests. Read with time to regulatory decision and repeat findings rates.

## Formula

```
RESR = Favorable or Neutral Outcomes / Engagements with Regulator
```
