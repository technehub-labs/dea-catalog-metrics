## 2. Definition

A Pulp, Paper & Forest Products indicator, Reforestation Completion Rate (RCR-F) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Areas Replanted per License Requirement / Required, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice.

## 3. Context

Measures delivery of replanting obligations - area, seedling survival, species mix - after harvest. Completion must be survival-adjusted, since planted seedlings are headline, and surviving stands are the obligation. Be alert to species simplification that meets area targets while degrading forest resilience. Read with third-party silviculture audits and harvest-area reconciliations.

## Formula

```
RCR-F = Areas Replanted per License Requirement / Required
```
