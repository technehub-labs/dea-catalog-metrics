## 2. Definition

Rehire Rate (Boomerang Employees) (RRE) measures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time in the Culture & Employee Experience domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Former Employees Rehired within Period / Hires - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of new hires who are returning former employees. It matters because boomerangs are a strong signal of employer brand and a cheap talent source. Rehire rates can rise for wrong reasons, such as alumni returning only because market conditions worsened. Read with Rehire Retention and Alumni Network engagement to confirm boomerangs are healthy re-recruitment.

## Formula

```
RRE = Former Employees Rehired within Period / Hires
```
