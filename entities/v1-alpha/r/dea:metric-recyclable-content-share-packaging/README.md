## 2. Definition

Recyclable Content Share (Packaging) (RCS-P) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking packaging & plastics. It is computed as Recycled Content Mass / Total Packaging Mass, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Manufacturing. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of recycled or recyclable material in packaging by weight or volume. It is the principal lever on packaging circularity commitments, and both regulation and brand positioning increasingly demand verified content. Distinguish recyclable-in-theory from recyclable-in-practice, since local collection infrastructure decides actual recycling rates. Read with lifecycle analysis results and supplier verification of recycled content claims.

## Formula

```
RCS-P = Recycled Content Mass / Total Packaging Mass
```
