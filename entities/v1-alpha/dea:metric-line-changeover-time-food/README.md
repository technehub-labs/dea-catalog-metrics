## 2. Definition

Within Food & Beverage Manufacturing, Line Changeover Time (Food) (LCT-F) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Minutes from Last Good Product to Next Good Product, expressed in minutes. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Its use is concentrated in Food & Beverage.
