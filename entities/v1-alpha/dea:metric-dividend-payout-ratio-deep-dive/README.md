## 2. Definition

Dividend Payout Ratio (Deep Dive) (DPRDD) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Valuation & Corporate Finance Standard Measures domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Dividends / Net Income - reported in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of earnings distributed as dividends. Payout balances shareholder income against reinvestment capacity. Payouts sustained above earnings are borrowing against the future. Read with dividend payout consistency and retention ratio (Earnings).

## Formula

```
Dividends / Net Income
```
