## 2. Definition

Sanding Rework Rate (SRR-F) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Furniture & Wood Products domain. It is computed as Items Requiring Sanding Rework / Items, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Manufacturing. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Items Requiring Sanding Rework / Items
```
