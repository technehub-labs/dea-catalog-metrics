## 2. Definition

Pre-Delinquency Treatment Uptake (PDTU) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking banking retail lending (deep dive). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is At-Risk Customers Accepting Treatment / Offered, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Banking practice.

## Formula

```
At-Risk Customers Accepting Treatment / Offered
```
