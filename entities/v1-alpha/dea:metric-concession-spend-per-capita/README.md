## 2. Definition

Within Sports & Events, Concession Spend per Capita (CSPC) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Concession Revenue / Attendance, measured in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Sports & events practice.

## 3. Context

Measures concession revenue per attendee at venues. It matters because concessions are among the highest-margin revenue streams in events. Per-caps can rise with pricing that degrades guest experience and future attendance. Read with Attendance trends and Guest Satisfaction to confirm per-capita gains are sustainable.

## Formula

```
Concession Revenue / Attendance
```
