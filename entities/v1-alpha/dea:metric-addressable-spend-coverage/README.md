## 2. Definition

Within Strategic Sourcing & Supplier Performance (Deep Dive), Addressable Spend Coverage (ASC-SS) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Spend under Sourcing Management / Total Third-Party Spend; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of total spend that falls within sourcing management's addressable scope. Coverage defines the ceiling on all sourcing value. Scope exclusions negotiated by exception become permanent leakage. Read with managed spend under contract and maverick spend rate (Sourcing).

## Formula

```
Spend under Sourcing Management / Total Third-Party Spend
```
