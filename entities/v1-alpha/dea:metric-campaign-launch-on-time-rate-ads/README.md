## 2. Definition

Campaign Launch On-Time Rate (Ads) (CLOT) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of advertising & media buying reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Campaigns Live on Scheduled Date / Campaigns - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Media & Entertainment sector.

## Formula

```
Campaigns Live on Scheduled Date / Campaigns
```
