## 2. Definition

Within Government & Public Administration, Procurement Cycle Time (Government) (PCT-G) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its calculation is Days from Requisition to Contract Award, measured in days. It originates from and is most used in Public Sector practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
