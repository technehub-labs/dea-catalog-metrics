## 2. Definition

Cleaning-Related Complaint Rate (CRCR-C) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Cleaning & Environmental Services domain. It is computed as Complaints / 1,000 Cleaning Visits, expressed in events per thousand or million (scaled). Its use is concentrated in Facility Services. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Complaints / 1,000 Cleaning Visits
```
