## 2. Definition

Ethics Incident Mean Time to Resolve (EIMTTR) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai reporting. Its calculation is Days from Ethics Incident to Closure, measured in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures elapsed time from ethics incident detection to resolution (remediation, communication, and closure). Resolution time is the accountability metric for AI ethics programs: fast resolution demonstrates seriousness to regulators and affected parties, while slow resolution compounds harm and reputational damage. Resolution quality must accompany speed, since fast superficial closure is the failure pattern. Read with incident root-cause recurrence and remediation verification.
