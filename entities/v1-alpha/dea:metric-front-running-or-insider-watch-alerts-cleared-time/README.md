## 2. Definition

A Investment Research & Trading Operations indicator, Front Running or Insider Watch Alerts Cleared Time (FRWACT) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is computed as Hours to Clear Surveillance Alerts, expressed in hours. Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula
