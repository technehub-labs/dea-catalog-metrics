## 2. Definition

Event Net Promoter Score (Attendees) (ENPS-A) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking events & exhibitions. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Attendee NPS Survey Result, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## 3. Context

Measures attendee willingness to recommend the event. For trade events, attendee satisfaction is the product quality that exhibitors actually buy, so this score anchors both renewal conversations and pricing power. Sample across attendee segments (buyer versus browser), since the buyers matter disproportionately. Read with exhibitor net promoter score and attendance-segment trends.
