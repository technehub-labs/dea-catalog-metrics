## 2. Definition

A Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures indicator, Price Cost Margin Squeeze Ratio (PCMSR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Margin Change / Cost Change - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the compression between achievable prices and input costs. Squeeze is the profitability killer in commodity-like markets. Averages across product lines hide the SKUs already underwater. Read with gross margin stability index and price realization rate (Market).

## Formula

```
Margin Change / Cost Change
```
