## 2. Definition

Quality Monitoring Score (Deep Dive) (QMS-D) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of contact center operations (deep dive) reporting. It is defined as Average QA Evaluation Score; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
