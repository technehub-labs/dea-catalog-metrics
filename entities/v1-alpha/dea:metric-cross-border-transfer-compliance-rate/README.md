## 2. Definition

Within Data Privacy & Protection, Cross-Border Transfer Compliance Rate (CBTCR) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Transfers under Valid Mechanism / Transfers Identified - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether international personal data transfers follow required mechanisms. Transfer compliance is the most technical and fastest-moving area of privacy law. Reliance on outdated transfer mappings is silent non-compliance. Read with privacy program maturity score and data residency compliance rate.

## Formula

```
Transfers under Valid Mechanism / Transfers Identified
```
