## 2. Definition

Monthly Viewing Hours per Subscriber (MVHS) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking broadcasting & streaming media. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Hours Streamed / Subscribers, expressed in hours. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment.

## 3. Context

Measures engagement depth per account. It is the leading indicator of churn and of long-term pricing power: engaged households cancel rarely and tolerate price increases, while dormant accounts are future cancels parked in the base. Household-level measurement beats account-level when profiles share plans. Read with streaming churn rate (Deep Dive) cohort analysis and content-genre engagement.

## Formula

```
Hours Streamed / Subscribers
```
