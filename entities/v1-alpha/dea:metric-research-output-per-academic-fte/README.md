## 2. Definition

A Education & Training Outcome Standard Measures indicator, Research Output per Academic FTE (ROAF) quantifies capability maturity or development. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Peer-Reviewed Outputs / Academic FTE - reported in events per thousand or million (scaled). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. It is a standard measure in the Education sector.

## 3. Context

Measures publications, grants, or citations per academic staff member. It is the standard research productivity comparator. Volume-weighted measures reward low-impact publishing over genuine contribution. Read with citation impact (field-normalized) and grant capture rate.

## Formula

```
Peer-Reviewed Outputs / Academic FTE
```
