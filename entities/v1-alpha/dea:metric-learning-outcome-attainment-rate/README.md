## 2. Definition

A Education & Training Outcome Standard Measures indicator, Learning Outcome Attainment Rate (LOATR) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Students Meeting Course Learning Outcomes / Enrolled Students, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Education practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of students meeting defined learning outcomes. Outcomes are the education equivalent of quality: enrollment without attainment is credentialing. Outcomes assessed by course instructors rather than standardized measures inflate. Read with accreditation exam pass rate and graduate satisfaction score (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Students Meeting Course Learning Outcomes / Enrolled Students
```
