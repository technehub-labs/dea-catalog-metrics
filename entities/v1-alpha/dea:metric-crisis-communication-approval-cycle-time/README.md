## 2. Definition

Crisis Communication Approval Cycle Time (CCACT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Crisis Management & Emergency Response domain. Its calculation is Minutes to Draft, Approve and Issue Public Statement, measured in minutes. It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures how long a crisis statement takes from drafting to approved release. Slow approvals in crises are how organizations go silent while rumors fill the void. Pre-approved templates and authorities are what compress this cycle. Read with stakeholder notification coverage and crisis response team activation time.

Minutes to Draft, Approve and Issue Public Statement
