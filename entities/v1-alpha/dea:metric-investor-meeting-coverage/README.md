## 2. Definition

Within Investor Relations & Capital Markets, Investor Meeting Coverage (IMC) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its calculation is Top Shareholders Engaged in Period / Top Shareholders, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Listed companies practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of target institutional investors engaged over a period. It matters because coverage drives valuation understanding and shareholder stability. Coverage can be gamed by counting brief conference touches as meaningful engagement. Read with Analyst Rating trends and institutional ownership churn to confirm breadth of engagement reflects influence.

## Formula

```
Top Shareholders Engaged in Period / Top Shareholders
```
