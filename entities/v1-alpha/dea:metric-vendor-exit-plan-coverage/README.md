## 2. Definition

Vendor Exit Plan Coverage (VEPC) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Third-Party Risk Management domain. Its calculation is Critical Vendors with Tested Exit Plan / Critical Vendors, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical vendors with tested exit or contingency plans. Exit plans are what make concentration a managed risk instead of a hostage situation. Plans without data portability verification are unexecutable. Read with concentration risk ratio and vendor management plan effectiveness.

## Formula

```
Critical Vendors with Tested Exit Plan / Critical Vendors
```
