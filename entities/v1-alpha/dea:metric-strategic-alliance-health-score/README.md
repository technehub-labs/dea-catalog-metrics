## 2. Definition

Strategic Alliance Health Score (SAHS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking corporate venturing & partnerships. The formula is Weighted Index of Joint Plan Attainment and Satisfaction - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

A composite of relationship quality measures for a strategic alliance - governance cadence, joint plan delivery, executive engagement, trust - typically from structured reviews with both partners. Alliances fail quietly through erosion rather than rupture, so health scores are the early warning system. Beware single-source scoring (only your side's view) and scores that are generous in years with renewals pending. Read with partnership revenue share and joint initiative delivery rates.
