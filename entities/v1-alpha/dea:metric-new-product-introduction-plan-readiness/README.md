## 2. Definition

A Sales & Operations Planning indicator, New Product Introduction Plan Readiness (NPIR) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as NPIs with Supply Plan at Launch / NPIs Launched; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Assesses whether launch-critical elements - engineering release, supply commitments, channel training, pricing, support content - are complete at each gate before a new product ships. It matters because launch failures are rarely heroic single-point misses; they are accumulated readiness debt. Watch for gate reviews that wave items through with open actions, and readiness scores that weight checklist completion over evidence of readiness. Read with launch phase quality returns and time to launch metrics.

## Formula

```
NPIs with Supply Plan at Launch / NPIs Launched
```
