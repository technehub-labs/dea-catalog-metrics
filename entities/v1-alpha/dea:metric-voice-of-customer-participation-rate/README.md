## 2. Definition

Voice of Customer Participation Rate (VoCPR) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Customer domain. It is computed as Survey Responses / Survey Invitations, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Survey Responses / Survey Invitations
```
