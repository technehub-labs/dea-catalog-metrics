## 2. Definition

Treatment Room Turnover Time (TRTT) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking beauty & personal care services. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Minutes between Appointments in Room - reported in minutes. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Personal Services sector.

## Formula

```
Minutes between Appointments in Room
```
