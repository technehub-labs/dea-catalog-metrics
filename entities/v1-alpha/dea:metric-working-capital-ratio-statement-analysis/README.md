## 2. Definition

A Financial Statement Analysis Ratios (Deep Dive) indicator, Working Capital Ratio (Statement Analysis) (WCRDD) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Net Working Capital / Total Assets; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the current-assets-to-current-liabilities relationship in ratio terms for analytical comparison. It frames short-term financial structure at a glance. Optimal values are industry-specific; high is not virtuous. Read with current ratio (Deep Dive) and working capital productivity (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Net Working Capital / Total Assets
```
