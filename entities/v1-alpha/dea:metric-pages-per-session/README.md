## 2. Definition

Pages per Session (PPS-WDA) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of web & digital analytics standard measures reporting. The formula is Pageviews / Sessions - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is a standard measure in the Digital product sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Pageviews / Sessions
```
