## 2. Definition

A Threat Intelligence & Red Team indicator, Adversary Emulation Fidelity Score (AEFS) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Exercise Fidelity to Real Adversary TTPs per Rubric, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
AEFS = Exercise Fidelity to Real Adversary TTPs per Rubric
```
