## 2. Definition

Visual Management Board Effectiveness (VMBE) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Process Excellence (Lean Six Sigma) domain. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Boards Reviewed at Required Cadence / Boards Deployed - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Boards Reviewed at Required Cadence / Boards Deployed
```
