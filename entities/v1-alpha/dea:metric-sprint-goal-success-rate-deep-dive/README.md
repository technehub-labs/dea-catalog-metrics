## 2. Definition

Sprint Goal Success Rate (Deep Dive) (SGSR-AG) measures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome in the Agile Delivery & Team Effectiveness domain. Its calculation is Sprint Goals Achieved / Sprints, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Sprint Goals Achieved / Sprints
```
