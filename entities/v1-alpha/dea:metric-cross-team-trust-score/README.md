## 2. Definition

A Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive) indicator, Cross-Team Trust Score (CTTS) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Survey Score of Inter-Team Trust; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the level of confidence and cooperation between teams rather than within them. Cross-team friction is where matrixed organizations actually fail. Trust scored by the teams themselves anchors low; third-party observation helps. Read with cross-functional OKR alignment ratio and collaboration network density.

## Formula

Survey Score of Inter-Team Trust
