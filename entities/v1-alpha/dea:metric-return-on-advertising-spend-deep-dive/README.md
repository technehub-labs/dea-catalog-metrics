## 2. Definition

Return on Advertising Spend (Deep Dive) (ROASDD) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of marketing & advertising standard measures (deep dive) reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue attributed per advertising dollar. ROAS is the workhorse of performance marketing accountability. Attribution models that credit the last click systematically overstate paid media. Read with marketing efficiency ratio (Deep Dive) and attribution model coverage (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Attributed Revenue / Advertising Spend
```
