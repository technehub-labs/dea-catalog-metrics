## 2. Definition

Takeout and Delivery Mix (Deep Dive) (TDM-D) captures growth over time for organizations tracking restaurant operations (deep dive). The formula is Off-Premise Revenue / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Restaurants sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue from off-premise channels. The mix shift reshapes restaurant economics - off-premise carries different margin structures, packaging costs, and marketplace commissions - and it stress-tests the dine-in cost base built for a mix that no longer exists. Mix read without channel-level profitability is strategy blindness. Read with channel contribution margins and third-party commission exposure.

## Formula

```
Off-Premise Revenue / Total Revenue
```
