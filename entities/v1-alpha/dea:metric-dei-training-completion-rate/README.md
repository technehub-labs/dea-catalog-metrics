## 2. Definition

A Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) indicator, DEI Training Completion Rate (DEITC) quantifies capability maturity or development. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Staff Completing Required DEI Learning / Staff, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
DEITC = Staff Completing Required DEI Learning / Staff
```
