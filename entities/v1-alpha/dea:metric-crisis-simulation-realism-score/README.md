## 2. Definition

A Crisis Management & Emergency Response indicator, Crisis Simulation Realism Score (CSRS) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Rubric-Based Realism and Challenge of Simulations - reported in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

Rubric-Based Realism and Challenge of Simulations
