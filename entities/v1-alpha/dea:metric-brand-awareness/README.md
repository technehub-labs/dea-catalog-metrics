## 2. Definition

Brand Awareness (BRA) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of marketing reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Respondents Recognizing Brand / Surveyed Population; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

The share of a target audience able to recognize or recall the brand - the top of the brand funnel, measured through prompted and unprompted survey methods that produce systematically different numbers and must never be mixed in a trend line. Strategic weight comes from what it predicts: awareness sets the ceiling on consideration, and consideration on purchase, so a brand whose awareness decays is losing future market share that sales metrics will not show for quarters. The honest use is relative - tracking against category competitors over time - because absolute awareness levels depend on methodology more than on brand strength. Unprompted recall is the purer signal, since prompted recognition credits brands the consumer would never summon themselves.

## Formula

```
Respondents Recognizing Brand / Surveyed Population
```
