## 2. Definition

Escalation Rate (Contact Center) (Deep Dive) (ESCDD) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking contact center standard measures (deep dive). It is computed as Contacts Escalated to Tier 2+ / Contacts, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Contacts Escalated to Tier 2+ / Contacts
```
