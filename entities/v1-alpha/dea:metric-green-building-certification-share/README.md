## 2. Definition

Within Real Estate Portfolio & Property Management, Green Building Certification Share (GBCS) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. It is computed as Certified Floor Area / Total Floor Area, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Real Estate.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of portfolio area holding recognized sustainability certifications. Certifications increasingly gate institutional capital, tenant selection, and regulatory positioning, and they support rental and valuation premiums in many markets. Beware certificate-as-checkbox portfolios: certification without operational performance (measured energy intensity) is marketing. Read with measured energy use intensity and tenant sustainability requirements.

## Formula

```
GBCS = Certified Floor Area / Total Floor Area
```
