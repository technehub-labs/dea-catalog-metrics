## 2. Definition

Water Recycling Rate (WRR) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking sustainability & esg. Its calculation is Recycled or Reused Water / Total Water Withdrawn, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of water use supplied by recycled or reclaimed sources. It matters because reuse directly reduces both withdrawal and discharge exposure. It can be inflated by counting single internal reuse cycles as closed-loop recycling. Read with Water Intensity and discharge quality metrics to confirm recycling reflects genuine circularity.

## Formula

```
Recycled or Reused Water / Total Water Withdrawn
```
