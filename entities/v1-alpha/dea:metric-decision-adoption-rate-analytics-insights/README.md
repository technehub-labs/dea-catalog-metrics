## 2. Definition

Within Data Science & Analytics Operations, Decision Adoption Rate - Analytics Insights (DARAI) is used to gauge how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Recommendations Adopted by Decision Makers / Recommendations; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of analytics recommendations adopted into business decisions. It is the value-conversion metric of the analytics function: insight without decision is expensive journalism, and adoption rates expose whether analytics is embedded in operating cadence or producing shelf-ware. Attribution must be defined at handoff, since adoption claimed post-hoc is unfalsifiable. Read with insight-to-action cycle time (Deep Dive) and decision-quality feedback.

## Formula

```
Recommendations Adopted by Decision Makers / Recommendations
```
