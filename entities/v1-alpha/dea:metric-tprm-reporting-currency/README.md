## 2. Definition

A Third-Party Risk Management indicator, TPRM Reporting Currency (TPRMRC) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Risk Reports Refreshed per Cadence / Reports; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Risk Reports Refreshed per Cadence / Reports
```
