## 2. Definition

Assurance Readiness Score (ESG) (ARSS) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting domain. Its calculation is Readiness Assessment for External Assurance, measured in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures readiness of ESG disclosures for external assurance - evidence trails, controls, data lineage. Assurance is moving from voluntary to regulatory expectation, and readiness gaps discovered by an assurance provider become findings, restatements, and management-letter items. Readiness scoring should follow the assurance standard's evidence requirements, not internal comfort. Read with ESG data completeness rate and internal-control testing results.
