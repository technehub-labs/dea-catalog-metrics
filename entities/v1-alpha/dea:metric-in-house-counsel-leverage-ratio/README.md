## 2. Definition

Within Legal Operations, In-House Counsel Leverage Ratio (IHCL) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. The formula is In-House Legal Hours / Outside Counsel Hours - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of legal work handled in-house versus sent to external firms, or in-house spend as a share of total legal spend. It is the core make-or-buy metric of legal operations, and rising leverage usually cuts cost while deepening institutional knowledge. Watch the hidden costs: leverage achieved by pushing work onto business teams without counsel is risk transfer, not efficiency. Read with legal spend as percent of revenue and outside counsel rate trends.

## Formula

```
In-House Legal Hours / Outside Counsel Hours
```
