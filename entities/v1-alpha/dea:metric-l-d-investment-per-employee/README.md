## 2. Definition

L&D Investment per Employee (LDIPE) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking learning & development effectiveness (deep dive). It is defined as Annual L&D Spend / Employees; the result is stated in currency per unit. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures annual learning investment per head. It frames L&D as capability investment rather than discretionary cost. Spend level alone says nothing about transfer or business impact. Read with training investment per employee and learning ROI ratio.

## Formula

```
LDIPE = Annual L&D Spend / Employees
```
