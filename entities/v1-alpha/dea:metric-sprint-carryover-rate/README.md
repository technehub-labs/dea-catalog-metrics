## 2. Definition

Sprint Carryover Rate (SCR-AG) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Agile Delivery & Team Effectiveness domain. The formula is Stories Carried to Next Sprint / Sprint Stories - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Stories Carried to Next Sprint / Sprint Stories
```
