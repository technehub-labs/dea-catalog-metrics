## 2. Definition

Design-to-Dev Handoff Rework Rate (DHR-UX) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of ux & design systems reporting. It is computed as Handoffs Requiring Redesign / Handoffs, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Handoffs Requiring Redesign / Handoffs
```
