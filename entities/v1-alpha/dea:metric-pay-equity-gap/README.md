## 2. Definition

Pay Equity Gap (PEG-DEI) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of diversity, equity & inclusion (deep dive) reporting. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Adjusted Pay Gap for Equivalent Roles - reported in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the adjusted pay difference between demographic groups in comparable roles. Adjusted gaps isolate potential discrimination from composition effects. Regression-based adjustment quality determines whether the gap is honest. Read with pay equity audit completion rate and compensation equity index.

## Formula

```
Adjusted Pay Gap for Equivalent Roles
```
