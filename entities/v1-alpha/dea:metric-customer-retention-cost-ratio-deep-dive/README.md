## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective, Customer Retention Cost Ratio (Deep Dive) (CRCR-D) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its calculation is Retention Spend / Retained Revenue, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures retention and loyalty spend relative to the revenue base being protected. Retention spending is justified up to the point where it exceeds churn cost. Loyalty spend that rewards would-stay-anyway customers wastes budget. Read with churn rate and net revenue retention.

## Formula

```
Retention Spend / Retained Revenue
```
