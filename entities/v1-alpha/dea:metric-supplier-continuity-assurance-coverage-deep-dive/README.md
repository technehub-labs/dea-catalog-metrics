## 2. Definition

Within Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery, Supplier Continuity Assurance Coverage (Deep Dive) (SCAC) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Critical Suppliers with Verified Continuity Plans / Critical Suppliers; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical suppliers with verified continuity and recovery commitments. Supplier failure is indistinguishable from internal failure in customer impact. Assurances collected as attestation letters rather than tested evidence are thin. Read with continuity attestation coverage (Vendors) and critical vendor assessment coverage.

## Formula

```
Critical Suppliers with Verified Continuity Plans / Critical Suppliers
```
