## 2. Definition

Flaring Intensity (Deep Dive) (FI-OG) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking oil & gas downstream & refining. The formula is Flared Volume / Throughput - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is a standard measure in the Oil & Gas sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures gas flared per unit of production. Flaring is both wasted product and the most publicly visible emissions failure in oil and gas, satellite monitoring has made it undeniable, and intensity normalization separates operational discipline from production declines. Routine versus safety flaring classification is the honesty question. Read with flare-reduction project delivery and regulatory exceedance history.
