## 2. Definition

Crisis Communication Activation Time (CCAT) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of corporate communications & pr (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Hours from Event to First Approved Statement; the result is stated in hours. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures elapsed time from incident to first coordinated public communication. Speed is the strategic variable in crisis response: the first hours set the narrative frame, and silence is filled by others, permanently - classic crisis research shows response delays convert incidents into reputation events. Measure both decision and dissemination clocks, since legal review is the usual bottleneck. Read with crisis simulation performance and media monitoring reaction quality.

Hours from Event to First Approved Statement
