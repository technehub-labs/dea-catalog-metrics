## 2. Definition

Ceremony Attendance Rate (CAR-AG) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of agile delivery & team effectiveness reporting. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Its calculation is Team Member Attendance at Scheduled Ceremonies / Expected, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Team Member Attendance at Scheduled Ceremonies / Expected
```
