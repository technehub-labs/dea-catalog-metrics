## 2. Definition

Risk Survey Completion Rate (RSCR) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Insurance Underwriting domain. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Surveys Completed before Binding / Risks Requiring Survey - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector.

## Formula

```
Surveys Completed before Binding / Risks Requiring Survey
```
