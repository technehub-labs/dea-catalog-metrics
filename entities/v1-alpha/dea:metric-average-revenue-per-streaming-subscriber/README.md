## 2. Definition

A Broadcasting & Streaming Media indicator, Average Revenue per Streaming Subscriber (ARPS) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Subscription Revenue / Subscribers; the result is stated in currency per unit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue per paying account, blended across plans, tiers, and geographies. It is the monetization dial of streaming strategy, and mix effects (international growth at lower price points) depress it without any domestic weakness. Ad-supported tiers complicate the read, so track subscription ARPU and advertising ARPU separately. Read with plan-mix trends and price-change pass-through rates.

## Formula

```
Subscription Revenue / Subscribers
```
