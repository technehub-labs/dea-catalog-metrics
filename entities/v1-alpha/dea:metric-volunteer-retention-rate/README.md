## 2. Definition

Volunteer Retention Rate (VRR-N) addresses the retention of customers, users, or assets over time and is a fixture of nonprofit & ngo reporting. It is defined as Volunteers Returning / Volunteers Engaged Previously; the result is stated in percent (%). In Nonprofit & NGO, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of volunteers returning period over period. It matters because volunteer continuity determines program delivery capacity. Retention can be maintained with declining engagement to warm bodies. Read with Volunteer Hours trend and Program Outcome delivery to confirm retention is active, not nominal.

## Formula

```
Volunteers Returning / Volunteers Engaged Previously
```
