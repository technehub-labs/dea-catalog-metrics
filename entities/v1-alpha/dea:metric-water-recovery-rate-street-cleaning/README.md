## 2. Definition

Water Recovery Rate (Street Cleaning) (WRR-S2) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Cleaning & Environmental Services domain. Its calculation is Recovered Wastewater / Water Used, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Facility Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Recovered Wastewater / Water Used
```
