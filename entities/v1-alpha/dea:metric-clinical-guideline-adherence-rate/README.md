## 2. Definition

A Medical Practice & Clinical Operations indicator, Clinical Guideline Adherence Rate (CGAR) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Care Decisions per Guideline / Care Decisions Sampled; the result is stated in percent (%). In Healthcare, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures conformance of delivered care to evidence-based guidelines for defined conditions. It operationalizes evidence into practice, and unwarranted variation is the classic quality problem it exposes. Adherence is not always right: documented, justified deviation for patient-specific reasons is good medicine, so audit samples for appropriateness, not just conformance. Read with outcome measures for the same conditions, since process adherence without outcomes is incomplete.

## Formula

```
Care Decisions per Guideline / Care Decisions Sampled
```
