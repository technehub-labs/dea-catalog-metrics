## 2. Definition

Regulatory Change Intake Cycle Time (RCICT) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of compliance monitoring & testing reporting. The formula is Days from Regulation Published to Obligation Assessed - reported in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

Days from Regulation Published to Obligation Assessed
