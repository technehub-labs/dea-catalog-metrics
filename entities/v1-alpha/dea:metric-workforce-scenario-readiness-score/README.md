## 2. Definition

Within Workforce Planning & Productivity, Workforce Scenario Readiness Score (WSRS) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Rubric of Modeled Scenarios for Workforce Shifts, expressed in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how well workforce plans cover major demand scenarios (growth, contraction, automation shifts). Scenario-ready workforce plans are the difference between response and improvisation. Scenarios limited to comfortable growth are planning theater. Read with scenario analysis coverage and workforce plan accuracy.
