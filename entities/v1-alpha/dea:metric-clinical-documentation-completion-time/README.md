## 2. Definition

A Medical Practice & Clinical Operations indicator, Clinical Documentation Completion Time (CDCT) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Hours from Encounter to Closed Note, expressed in hours. Its use is concentrated in Healthcare. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

Hours from Encounter to Closed Note
