## 2. Definition

Model Update Cycle Compliance (MUCC-RS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking investment research & trading operations. Its calculation is Coverage Models Refreshed per Schedule / Due, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Coverage Models Refreshed per Schedule / Due
```
