## 2. Definition

Within Fitness & Wellness (Deep Dive), Revenue per Member (Deep Dive) (RPM-D) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. It is computed as Total Revenue / Active Members, expressed in currency per unit. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Health & Fitness.

## 3. Context

Measures total revenue (dues, personal training, retail) per member per period. It is the monetization summary of the fitness model, and its composition matters: revenue growth from personal-training attach is quality growth, while late-fee harvesting is reputational and regulatory risk. Pricing power reads best with tenure mix, since legacy members grandfathered at old rates depress it. Read with retention and secondary-spend attach rates.

## Formula

```
Total Revenue / Active Members
```
