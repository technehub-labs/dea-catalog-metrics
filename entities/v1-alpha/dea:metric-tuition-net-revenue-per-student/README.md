## 2. Definition

Tuition Net Revenue Per Student (TNRS) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking education & training outcome standard measures. Its calculation is Net Tuition Revenue / Enrolled Students, measured in currency per unit. It originates from and is most used in Education practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures realized tuition after discounts and aid per student. It is the honest revenue number beneath sticker prices. Discount rates can rise until net revenue per student falls while enrollment grows. Read with net tuition revenue growth and discount rate (institutional aid).

## Formula

```
Net Tuition Revenue / Enrolled Students
```
