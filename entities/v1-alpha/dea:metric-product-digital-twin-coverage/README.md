## 2. Definition

Product Digital Twin Coverage (PDTC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of digital transformation reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Products with Digital Twin / Products in Scope - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of products or assets represented by maintained digital twins. It matters because twins are the foundation for predictive operations and faster change simulation. Coverage can be claimed with static models that are never updated or used. Read with Twin Synchronization freshness and simulation-informed decisions to confirm twins are live.

## Formula

```
Products with Digital Twin / Products in Scope
```
