## 2. Definition

Psychological Safety Score - Delivery Teams (PSS-DT) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking agile delivery & team effectiveness. It is computed as Survey-Based Psychological Safety Score, expressed in score (points). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether team members feel safe to raise problems, admit mistakes, and challenge decisions. Safety is the leading indicator of honest retrospectives, early risk surfacing, and real innovation. Scores degrade silently when leaders punish messengers. Read with team happiness index and incident post-mortem completion rate.
