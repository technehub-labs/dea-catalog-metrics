## 2. Definition

A Knowledge Management indicator, Knowledge Contribution Rate (KCR-E) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Employees Contributing Knowledge per Period / Employees, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Employees Contributing Knowledge per Period / Employees
```
