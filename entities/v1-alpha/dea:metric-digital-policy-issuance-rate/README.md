## 2. Definition

Digital Policy Issuance Rate (DPIR) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Insurance Distribution - Direct & Digital domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Policies Issued Digitally / Policies Issued - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of policies issued end-to-end digitally without manual touch. It is the unit-economics lever of direct and digital insurance: manual issuance is the cost wall between digital customer acquisition and profitable conversion, and every point of automation moves loss-adjusted economics. Issuance automated at the expense of underwriting controls writes worse business, so read quality alongside. Read with direct channel loss ratio and digital insurance cost per policy.

## Formula

```
Policies Issued Digitally / Policies Issued
```
