## 2. Definition

Distribution-to-Paid-In Ratio (Private) (DPI-IM) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of investment management - portfolio operations reporting. It is computed as Distributions Paid to Investors / Capital Contributed, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Financial Services.

## 3. Context

Measures cumulative cash actually returned to limited partners as a share of capital paid in. DPI is the only purely realized performance figure private markets offer, immune to valuation optimism. A low DPI late in fund life is the uncomfortable truth IRRs can obscure. Read with total value to paid-in ratio and fund-level IRR.

## Formula

```
Distributions Paid to Investors / Capital Contributed
```
