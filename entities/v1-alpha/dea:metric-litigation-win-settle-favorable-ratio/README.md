## 2. Definition

Litigation Win-Settle-Favorable Ratio (LWSF) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking legal operations. It is computed as Favorable Outcomes / Resolved Matters, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of resolved matters with favorable outcomes - wins at trial, favorable settlements, dismissals. It is the outcome metric of legal strategy, but raw win rates mislead without matter selection and risk-adjusted expectations: a department that only fights sure winners is not serving the business. Benchmark matters against early case assessments. Read with litigation cost per matter and cycle times.

## Formula

```
Favorable Outcomes / Resolved Matters
```
