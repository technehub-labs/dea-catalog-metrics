## 2. Definition

Within Textile & Apparel (Deep Dive), Order Fulfilment Rate - Apparel (Deep Dive) (OFR-D2) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Shipped Complete and On Time / Ordered - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Textiles & Apparel sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures complete, on-time fulfilment of wholesale and e-commerce orders. In a fashion calendar industry, fulfilment reliability protects the sell-through window - a late delivery in season is a missed season, with markdown consequences priced into every order. Complete-order measurement beats line-fill alone, since partial shipments split the difference. Read with lead-time adherence and inventory availability by style at cut-off.

## Formula

```
Shipped Complete and On Time / Ordered
```
