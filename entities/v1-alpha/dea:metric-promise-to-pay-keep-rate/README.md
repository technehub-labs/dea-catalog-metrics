## 2. Definition

A Accounts Payable & Receivable Operations (Deep Dive) indicator, Promise-to-Pay Keep Rate (PTPKR) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Kept Payment Promises / Promises Made - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
PTPKR = Kept Payment Promises / Promises Made
```
