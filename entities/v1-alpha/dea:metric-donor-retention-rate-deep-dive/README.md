## 2. Definition

Within Nonprofit & Donor Operations (Deep Dive), Donor Retention Rate (Deep Dive) (DRR-D) is used to gauge the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Donors Giving Again / Prior Donors, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Nonprofit practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of donors giving again in a subsequent period. It is the compounding engine of nonprofit sustainability: donor acquisition costs multiples of retention, and organizations with healthy retention fund mission through relationships rather than perpetual re-acquisition. First-gift conversion to second is the pivotal retention step, since most donors lapse after one gift. Read with recurring donor share of revenue (Deep Dive) and donor lifetime value (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Donors Giving Again / Prior Donors
```
