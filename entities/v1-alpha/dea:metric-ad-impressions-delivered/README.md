## 2. Definition

Ad Impressions Delivered (AID) measures the utilization or availability of capacity in the Media & Entertainment domain. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Its calculation is Count of Ad Impressions per Period, measured in count (items per period). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Media & entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
Count of Ad Impressions per Period
```
