## 2. Definition

Within Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive), Inclusive Leadership Score (ILS-DEI) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. The formula is 360-Degree Rating of Inclusive Behaviors - reported in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether leaders demonstrably create inclusive team conditions. Inclusive behavior is the mechanism through which representation becomes belonging. Scores from direct reports carry the signal; scores from leaders' self-assessments carry noise. Read with belonging index and manager effectiveness index (Deep Dive).

## Formula

360-Degree Rating of Inclusive Behaviors
