## 2. Definition

Time-to-Degree (Median) (TTD) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of education & training outcome standard measures reporting. Its calculation is Median Years from Entry to Award, measured in years. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Education practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the median years students take to complete. Time-to-degree is the hidden cost driver of education: extra years delay earnings and consume aid. Median figures on completers-only exclude the dropouts. Read with graduation rate (Deep Dive) and credit accumulation rate.

Median Years from Entry to Award
