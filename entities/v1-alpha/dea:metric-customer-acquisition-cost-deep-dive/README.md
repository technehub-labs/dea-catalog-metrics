## 2. Definition

Customer Acquisition Cost (Deep Dive) (CACDD) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Unit Economics & Growth (Deep Dive) domain. The formula is Total Acquisition Spend / New Customers - reported in currency per unit. It is a standard measure in the Software sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the fully loaded cost to acquire a customer. CAC is the denominator of growth economics. Blended CAC hides the decaying paid channels inside a healthy average. Read with LTV-to-CAC ratio (Deep Dive) and marginal CAC efficiency.

## Formula

```
Total Acquisition Spend / New Customers
```
