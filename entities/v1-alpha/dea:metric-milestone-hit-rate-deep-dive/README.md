## 2. Definition

A Project Management (Deep Dive) indicator, Milestone Hit Rate (Deep Dive) (MHR-D) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is computed as Milestones Achieved on Time / Milestones Planned, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of committed milestones achieved on schedule. Milestones are the checkpoints where stakeholders calibrate trust. Teams game the rate by declaring early completion or redefining milestones. Read with schedule variance percent and stage gate bypass rate.

## Formula

```
Milestones Achieved on Time / Milestones Planned
```
