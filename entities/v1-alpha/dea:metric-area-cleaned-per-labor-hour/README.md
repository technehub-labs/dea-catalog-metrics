## 2. Definition

A Cleaning & Environmental Services indicator, Area Cleaned per Labor Hour (ACPH) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Square Metres Cleaned / Labor Hours, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Facility Services.

## Formula

```
Square Metres Cleaned / Labor Hours
```
