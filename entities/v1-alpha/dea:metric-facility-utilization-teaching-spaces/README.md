## 2. Definition

Facility Utilization (Teaching Spaces) (FUTS) captures the utilization or availability of capacity for organizations tracking education & training outcome standard measures. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Scheduled Teaching Hours / Available Space Hours, expressed in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Its use is concentrated in Education.

## Formula

```
Scheduled Teaching Hours / Available Space Hours
```
