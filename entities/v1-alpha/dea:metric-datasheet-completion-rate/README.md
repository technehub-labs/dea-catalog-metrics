## 2. Definition

Datasheet Completion Rate (DCR-AI) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai. Its calculation is Datasets with Completed Datasheets / Datasets, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Datasets with Completed Datasheets / Datasets
```
