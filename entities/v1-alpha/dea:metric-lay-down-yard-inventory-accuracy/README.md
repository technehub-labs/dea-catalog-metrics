## 2. Definition

Lay-down Yard Inventory Accuracy (LYIA) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking construction contracting (deep dive). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Materials Tracked Accurately / Audited, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Construction.

## Formula

```
LYIA = Materials Tracked Accurately / Audited
```
