## 2. Definition

A Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive) indicator, Employee Engagement Score (Deep Dive) (EESDD) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Composite Survey Score of Engagement Drivers; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures employees' commitment and connection to their work and organization. Engagement predicts productivity, retention, and quality quarters ahead. Scores from annual surveys arrive too late to manage; pulse cadence matters. Read with employee engagement score trend (Deep Dive) and eNPS (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
EESDD = Composite Survey Score of Engagement Drivers
```
