## 2. Definition

Strategy Communication Reach (SCHR) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of balanced scorecard implementation & review reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Employees Aware of Strategic Objectives / Employees; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of employees reached by strategy communication they can recall and relate to their work. Reach without comprehension is broadcasting. Surveys measuring exposure rather than understanding flatter the effort. Read with strategy refresh cycle time and employee engagement score trend.

## Formula

```
Employees Aware of Strategic Objectives / Employees
```
