## 2. Definition

Within Digital Workplace & Collaboration, Remote Work Enablement Score (RWES) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. It is defined as Weighted Index of Access, Tooling, Experience Items; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the completeness of remote work capability - device standards, secure access, collaboration equity, home-office policy support - across the workforce. It became a resilience and talent-access metric post-2020, and scores unevenly across frontline versus knowledge segments. Self-assessed scores inflate in organizations with strong hybrid culture, so triangulate with access-outage and support-ticket data. Read with productivity stability measures during disruption events.
