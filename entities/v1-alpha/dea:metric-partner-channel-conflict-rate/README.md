## 2. Definition

A Corporate Venturing & Partnerships indicator, Partner Channel Conflict Rate (PCCR) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Disputed Partner Transactions / Partner Transactions, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures frequency of conflicts between direct and partner channels. It matters because conflict burns partner goodwill and distorts customer pricing. Conflicts can be hidden by crediting deals to whichever channel complains loudest. Read with Partner Satisfaction and channel margin trends to confirm conflict management is working.

## Formula

```
PCCR = Disputed Partner Transactions / Partner Transactions
```
