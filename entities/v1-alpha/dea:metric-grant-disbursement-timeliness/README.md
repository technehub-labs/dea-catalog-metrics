## 2. Definition

A Public Sector & Government indicator, Grant Disbursement Timeliness (GDT) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Grants Disbursed by Target Date / Grants Approved; the result is stated in percent (%). In Public sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of grants paid within committed timelines. It matters because delayed funds stall programs and destroy grantee trust. Timeliness can be improved by disbursing before verification, trading speed for fraud and compliance risk. Read with Grant Compliance findings and improper payment rates to confirm fast is also correct.

## Formula

```
GDT = Grants Disbursed by Target Date / Grants Approved
```
