## 2. Definition

A Field Maintenance & Technical Field Service (Deep Dive) indicator, Mean Time between Field Visits - Asset (MTBFV) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Days between Corrective Visits per Asset - reported in days. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Days between Corrective Visits per Asset
```
