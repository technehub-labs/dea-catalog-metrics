## 2. Definition

Initiative-to-Objective Traceability (IOT-BSC) captures governance for organizations tracking balanced scorecard implementation & review. Its calculation is Strategic Initiatives Mapped to Objectives / Initiatives, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether each strategic objective is supported by traceable initiatives with contribution logic. Traceability converts strategy from aspiration into an executable plan. Traces that stop at initiative names without owner accountability are nominal. Read with objective-to-action traceability rate and benefits tracking coverage.

## Formula

```
IOT-BSC = Strategic Initiatives Mapped to Objectives / Initiatives
```
