## 2. Definition

Vendor Dispute Resolution Time (VDRT) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of outsourcing & vendor management reporting. Its calculation is Days from Dispute Raised to Resolution, measured in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures average time to resolve commercial disputes with vendors. It matters because disputes freeze payments and poison the relationship that daily service depends on. Resolution speed can be bought by conceding value to close disputes. Read with Vendor SLA Compliance Rate and dispute recurrence to confirm resolutions are fair and final.

## Formula

```
Days from Dispute Raised to Resolution
```
