## 2. Definition

A Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Marketing Qualified Lead to Sales Accepted Ratio (MQSAR) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its calculation is SALs / MQLs, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
SALs / MQLs
```
