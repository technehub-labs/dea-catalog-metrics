## 2. Definition

Dividend Payout Consistency (DPC-BSC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of balanced scorecard - financial perspective reporting. The formula is Dividends Paid as Planned / Planned - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the reliability of dividend payments over time. Consistency is priced by the market as a signal of earnings quality and management discipline. Maintaining dividends by cutting maintenance capex borrows from the future. Read with free cash flow margin and debt to equity.

## Formula

```
Dividends Paid as Planned / Planned
```
