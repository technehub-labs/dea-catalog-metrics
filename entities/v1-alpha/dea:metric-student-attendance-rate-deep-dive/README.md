## 2. Definition

A Education & Training Outcome Standard Measures indicator, Student Attendance Rate (Deep Dive) (SARDD) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Attended Sessions / Scheduled Sessions - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Education sector.

## Formula

```
Attended Sessions / Scheduled Sessions
```
