## 2. Definition

A Banking Retail Lending (Deep Dive) indicator, Collections Right-Party Contact Rate (RPCR) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Contacts with Customer / Contact Attempts, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Banking.

## Formula

```
RPCR = Contacts with Customer / Contact Attempts
```
