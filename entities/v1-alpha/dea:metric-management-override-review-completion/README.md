## 2. Definition

Within Internal Controls & Financial Reporting Integrity, Management Override Review Completion (MORC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its calculation is Required Override Reviews Performed / Required Reviews, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Required Override Reviews Performed / Required Reviews
```
