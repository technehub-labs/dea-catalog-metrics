## 2. Definition

Tacit-to-Explicit Conversion Rate (TECR) measures capability maturity or development in the Knowledge Management (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Documented Expert Sessions / Sessions Planned; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how much expert tacit knowledge is being captured into usable explicit form. Conversion is the only durable answer to knowledge walking out the door. Conversion counted by documents produced rather than knowledge reused is activity. Read with knowledge base contribution rate and critical knowledge capture rate.

## Formula

```
Documented Expert Sessions / Sessions Planned
```
