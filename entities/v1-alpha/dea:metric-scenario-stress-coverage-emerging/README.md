## 2. Definition

Within Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning, Scenario Stress Coverage (Emerging) (SSCE) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Emerging Risks with Quantified Scenarios / Material Emerging Risks, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how thoroughly emerging risks are stress-tested against plausible scenarios. Stress testing is where vague concerns become quantified exposure. Scenarios constrained to precedent cannot imagine novelty. Read with scenario analysis coverage and stress test scenario coverage.

## Formula

```
Emerging Risks with Quantified Scenarios / Material Emerging Risks
```
