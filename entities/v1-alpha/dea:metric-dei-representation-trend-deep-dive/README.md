## 2. Definition

DEI Representation Trend (Deep Dive) (DEIRT-D) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Change in Representation Metrics between Periods - reported in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the direction of diversity across workforce levels over time. Trend, and its distribution across seniority, reveals whether representation is improving or stalling at the top. Hiring-pipeline-only measurement ignores retention and promotion bottlenecks. Read with promotion equity rate and pay equity gap.
