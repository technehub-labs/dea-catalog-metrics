## 2. Definition

Logistics Cost-to-Serve Ratio (Deep Dive) (LCTSDD) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Supply Chain Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Total Logistics Cost / Revenue; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures logistics cost relative to the revenue or volume served. It is the honest price of the delivery promise made to customers. Costs allocated at network average hide the expensive lanes and customers. Read with cost to serve per customer (Deep Dive) and total landed cost variance.

## Formula

```
Total Logistics Cost / Revenue
```
