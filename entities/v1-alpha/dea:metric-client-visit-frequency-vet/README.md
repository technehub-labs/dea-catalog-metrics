## 2. Definition

Client Visit Frequency (Vet) (CVF) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of veterinary & animal health reporting. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Visits per Patient per Year, expressed in times per period. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Healthcare.

## Formula

```
Visits per Patient per Year
```
