## 2. Definition

A PMO & Portfolio Governance indicator, Strategic Initiative Alignment Score (SIAS) quantifies strategy. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Weighted Alignment of Portfolio to Strategy Objectives, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how well initiatives map to declared strategic priorities. Misaligned spend is strategy on paper only. Self-scored alignment tends toward unanimity. Read with strategic alignment score - initiatives and strategic goal execution rate.

## Formula

```
Weighted Alignment of Portfolio to Strategy Objectives
```
