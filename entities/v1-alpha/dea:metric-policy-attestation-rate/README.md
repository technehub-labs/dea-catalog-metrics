## 2. Definition

A Risk & Compliance indicator, Policy Attestation Rate (PAR) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Employees Attesting to Policies / Employees Required, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Employees Attesting to Policies / Employees Required
```
