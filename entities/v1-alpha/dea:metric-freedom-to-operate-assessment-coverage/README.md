## 2. Definition

Freedom-to-Operate Assessment Coverage (FTOC) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking intellectual property & licensing. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Product Launches with FTO Clearance / Launches - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how thoroughly new products are assessed for third-party IP conflicts before launch. FTO gaps convert into injunctions and redesigns after the money is spent. Assessments limited to headline patents miss the dense secondary claims. Read with IP litigation cost exposure ratio and patent landscape analysis coverage.

## Formula

```
Product Launches with FTO Clearance / Launches
```
