## 2. Definition

A Standard Workforce & Pay Metrics indicator, Promotion Rate (Deep Dive) (PRDD) quantifies capability maturity or development. It is computed as Promotions / Average Headcount, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of employees promoted per period. Healthy promotion rates balance career velocity against title inflation. Rates that vary by demographic are an equity signal. Read with promotion equity ratio and leadership pipeline strength index.

## Formula

```
Promotions / Average Headcount
```
