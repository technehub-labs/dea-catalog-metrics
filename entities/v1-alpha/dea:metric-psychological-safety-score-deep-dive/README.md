## 2. Definition

A Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive) indicator, Psychological Safety Score (Deep Dive) (PSSDD) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Team-Level Psychological Safety Survey Composite; the result is stated in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether employees can speak up, admit error, and challenge without fear. Safety is the leading indicator of learning, innovation, and incident honesty. Scores degrade silently when speaking up gets punished once. Read with psychological safety score - Delivery Teams and burnout risk index.
