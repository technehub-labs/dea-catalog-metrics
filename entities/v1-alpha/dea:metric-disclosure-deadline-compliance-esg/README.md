## 2. Definition

Within Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting, Disclosure Deadline Compliance (ESG) (DDCE) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Disclosures on Time / Required; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures on-time delivery of mandatory ESG disclosures under regulatory regimes. Deadlines are now legal obligations with enforcement attached in major jurisdictions, and late or incomplete filings carry the same weight as financial reporting failures. Compliance achieved through last-minute low-quality submissions satisfies the letter only. Read with assurance readiness score (ESG) and data pipeline timeliness upstream.

## Formula

```
DDCE = Disclosures on Time / Required
```
