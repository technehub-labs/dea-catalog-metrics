## 2. Definition

Counterfeit Intel Rejection Rate (CIRR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking threat intelligence & red team. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Unverified Intel Rejected / Intel Received - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Unverified Intel Rejected / Intel Received
```
