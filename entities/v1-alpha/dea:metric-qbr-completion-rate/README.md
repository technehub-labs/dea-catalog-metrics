## 2. Definition

QBR Completion Rate (QBRC) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of customer success (b2b saas) reporting. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Its calculation is Quarterly Business Reviews Held / Reviews Planned, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Software & SaaS practice.

## Formula

```
Quarterly Business Reviews Held / Reviews Planned
```
