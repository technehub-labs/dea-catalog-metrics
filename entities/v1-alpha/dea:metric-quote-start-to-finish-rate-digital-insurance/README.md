## 2. Definition

A Insurance Distribution - Direct & Digital indicator, Quote Start-to-Finish Rate (Digital Insurance) (QSFR) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Completed Quotes / Started Quotes, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Insurance practice.

## Formula

```
Completed Quotes / Started Quotes
```
