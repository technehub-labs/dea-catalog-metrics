## 2. Definition

Threat Hunting Coverage (THC-SOC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of secops & security operations center reporting. The formula is Attack Hypotheses Hunted per Period vs Plan - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula
