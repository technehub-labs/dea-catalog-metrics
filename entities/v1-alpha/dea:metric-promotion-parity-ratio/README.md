## 2. Definition

Within Diversity, Equity & Inclusion, Promotion Parity Ratio (PPR) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Promotion Rate of Underrepresented Group / Promotion Rate Overall, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Compares promotion rates across demographic groups within comparable populations and time windows. It exposes whether underrepresentation at senior levels stems from pipeline supply or internal gatekeeping, a distinction headcount metrics cannot make. Guard against cohort definitions that wash out the effect and against promotion-rate parity achieved by slowing everyone down. Read with workforce diversity representation by level and pay equity ratio.

## Formula

```
Promotion Rate of Underrepresented Group / Promotion Rate Overall
```
