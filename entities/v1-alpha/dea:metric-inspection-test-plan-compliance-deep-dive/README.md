## 2. Definition

Inspection Test Plan Compliance (Deep Dive) (ITPC-D) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Construction Contracting (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is ITP Holds Tested on Schedule / Holds, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Construction practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
ITP Holds Tested on Schedule / Holds
```
