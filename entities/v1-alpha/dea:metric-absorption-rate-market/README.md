## 2. Definition

Absorption Rate (Market) (ABSR-RE) measures growth over time in the Real Estate & Mortgage Standard Measures domain. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Units Leased or Sold per Period / Total Available Units; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Real estate, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly available space is leased or sold in the market. Absorption is the demand-side pulse under every property decision. Positive absorption with heavy new supply can still mean rising vacancy. Read with vacancy rate trend and market share (Deep Dive) for the portfolio's geography.

## Formula

```
Units Leased or Sold per Period / Total Available Units
```
