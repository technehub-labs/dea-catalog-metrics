## 2. Definition

Driver Retention Rate (Deep Dive) (DRR-D) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking parcel & last-mile logistics (deep dive). The formula is Drivers Retained / Drivers Employed - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Logistics & Trade sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures driver turnover in last-mile operations. Drivers hold the route knowledge, customer relationships, and delivery-quality standards, and last-mile driver churn is the dominant quality and cost destabilizer in a labor-competitive market. Retention bought through sign-on bonuses is churn laundering - the churn simply moves to the ninety-day mark. Read with first-90-day attrition and wage positioning against local logistics competitors.

## Formula

```
Drivers Retained / Drivers Employed
```
