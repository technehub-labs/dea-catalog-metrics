## 2. Definition

Major Incident Declared Accuracy (MIDA) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the IT Incident & Major Problem Management domain. It is defined as Major Declarations Justified / Declarations; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
MIDA = Major Declarations Justified / Declarations
```
