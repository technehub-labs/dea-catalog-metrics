## 2. Definition

Meeting Load per Role (MLPR) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Operating Model & Process Health Deep Dive domain. It is computed as Meeting Hours per Role per Week, expressed in hours. It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
MLPR = Meeting Hours per Role per Week
```
