## 2. Definition

A Fitness & Wellness indicator, Member Satisfaction Score (Fitness) (MSS-F) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Satisfied Member Survey Responses / Responses, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Fitness & wellness practice.

## 3. Context

Measures member satisfaction with facilities, classes, and service. It matters because satisfaction mediates the visit-retention link in subscription fitness. Surveying only engaged members inflates the score. Read with Net Promoter Score and Member Churn Rate to confirm satisfaction tracks the whole base.

## Formula

```
MSS-F = Satisfied Member Survey Responses / Responses
```
