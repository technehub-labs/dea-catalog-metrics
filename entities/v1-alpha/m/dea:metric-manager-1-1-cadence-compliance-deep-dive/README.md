## 2. Definition

Within Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive), Manager 1:1 Cadence Compliance (Deep Dive) (MO1CC) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Reports Requiring Regular 1:1s Receiving Them / Reports, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
MO1CC = Reports Requiring Regular 1:1s Receiving Them / Reports
```
