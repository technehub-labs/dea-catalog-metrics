## 2. Definition

Manager Effectiveness Index (Deep Dive) (MEIDD) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of employee engagement & culture (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Upward Feedback Composite for People Managers, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures aggregate manager quality as rated by their teams. Managers are the largest single variable in the employee experience. Indexes that blend upward ratings from skip-levels dilute the signal. Read with manager feedback quality index and coaching program effectiveness score.

## Formula

```
MEIDD = Upward Feedback Composite for People Managers
```
