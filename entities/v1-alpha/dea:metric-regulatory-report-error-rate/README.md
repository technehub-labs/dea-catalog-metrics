## 2. Definition

Regulatory Report Error Rate (RRER) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of compliance monitoring & testing reporting. It is defined as Filings Corrected or Resubmitted / Filings; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the frequency of material errors in submitted regulatory reports. Errors erode regulator confidence and invite deeper scrutiny. Error rates counted only on self-detected errors understate reality. Read with regulatory report filing timeliness and data quality trend index (regulatory data).

## Formula

```
Filings Corrected or Resubmitted / Filings
```
