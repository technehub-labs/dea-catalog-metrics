## 2. Definition

Within Staffing & Recruiting, Offer Decline Rate (Staffing) (ODR-S) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Declined Offers / Offers Extended, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Staffing & recruiting practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of candidate offers declined in staffing placements. It matters because declines waste sourcing spend and signal compensation misalignment with market. Recruiters can suppress it by only extending sure-thing offers, shrinking reach. Read with Time to Placement and Candidate Pipeline Depth to see whether low decline means strength or caution.

## Formula

```
Declined Offers / Offers Extended
```
