## 2. Definition

Total Cost of Ownership Optimization Rate (TCOOR) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking strategic sourcing & supplier performance (deep dive). It is defined as Awards on TCO Basis rather than Price / Awards; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures progress reducing full lifecycle cost, not just purchase price. TCO is where purchase-price myopia gets corrected: acquisition is a fraction of lifecycle cost. Optimization measured on unit price alone is purchasing, not ownership. Read with total cost of ownership per application and total landed cost variance.

## Formula

```
Awards on TCO Basis rather than Price / Awards
```
