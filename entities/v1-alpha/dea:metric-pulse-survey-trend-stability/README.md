## 2. Definition

Within Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive), Pulse Survey Trend Stability (PSTS) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Variance of Pulse Scores across Periods - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
PSTS = Variance of Pulse Scores across Periods
```
