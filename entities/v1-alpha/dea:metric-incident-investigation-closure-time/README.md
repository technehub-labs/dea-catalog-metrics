## 2. Definition

Incident Investigation Closure Time (IICT) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking health & safety. It is computed as Days from Incident to Investigation Closure, expressed in days. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Days from Incident to Investigation Closure
```
