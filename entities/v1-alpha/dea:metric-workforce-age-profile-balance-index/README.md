## 2. Definition

A Standard Workforce & Pay Metrics indicator, Workforce Age Profile Balance Index (WAPBI) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Distribution Evenness across Age Bands; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the distribution of workforce tenure and age against sustainability needs. Imbalance (heavy retirement cliff or total juniority) predicts capability shocks. Indexes read as compliance rather than planning signals. Read with critical knowledge flight risk and succession plan coverage (Deep Dive - Workforce).

## Formula

```
Distribution Evenness across Age Bands
```
