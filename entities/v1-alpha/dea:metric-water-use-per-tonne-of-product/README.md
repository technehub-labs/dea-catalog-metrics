## 2. Definition

Water Use per Tonne of Product (WUTP) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking food & beverage manufacturing. Its calculation is Water Consumed / Production Tonnes, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It originates from and is most used in Food & Beverage practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Water Consumed / Production Tonnes
```
