## 2. Definition

Crisis Role Clarity Score (CRCS) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Crisis Management & Emergency Response domain. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Post-Exercise Survey on Role Clarity, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula
