## 2. Definition

Within Arts & Culture, Facility Utilization (Venue Hours) (FUH) is used to gauge the utilization or availability of capacity. It is defined as Used Venue Hours / Available Venue Hours; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Arts & culture, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of available venue hours used for performances and events. It matters because venue economics depend on earning hours covering fixed costs. Utilization can be pushed with low-margin rentals that fill calendars without profit. Read with Revenue per Used Hour and event profitability to confirm utilization is economically meaningful.

## Formula

```
Used Venue Hours / Available Venue Hours
```
