## 2. Definition

Training Completion Rate (TCR-L) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking learning & development. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Completed Enrollments / Enrollments, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Completed Enrollments / Enrollments
```
