## 2. Definition

In-App Feedback Response Rate (IFRR-S) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking software product operations (deep dive). The formula is Feedback Prompts Answered / Prompts Shown - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Technology sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
IFRR-S = Feedback Prompts Answered / Prompts Shown
```
