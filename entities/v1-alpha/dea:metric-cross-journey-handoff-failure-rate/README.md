## 2. Definition

Cross-Journey Handoff Failure Rate (JHFR) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Customer Journey & Experience domain. The formula is Handoffs Requiring Customer Re-Explanation / Handoffs - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Handoffs Requiring Customer Re-Explanation / Handoffs
```
