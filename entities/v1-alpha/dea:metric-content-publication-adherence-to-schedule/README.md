## 2. Definition

Content Publication Adherence to Schedule (CPAS) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking publishing & news media. It is computed as Items Published on Schedule / Planned, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment.

## Formula

```
Items Published on Schedule / Planned
```
