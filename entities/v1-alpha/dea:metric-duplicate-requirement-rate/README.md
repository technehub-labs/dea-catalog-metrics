## 2. Definition

Duplicate Requirement Rate (DRR-RE) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Requirements Engineering & Product Discovery domain. It is computed as Duplicate Requirements Identified / Requirements Reviewed, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Duplicate Requirements Identified / Requirements Reviewed
```
