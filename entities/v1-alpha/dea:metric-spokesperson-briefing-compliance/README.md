## 2. Definition

Spokesperson Briefing Compliance (SBC) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking corporate communications & pr. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Interviews per Approved Messaging / Interviews - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of designated spokespeople who are media-trained and current on briefings. It matters because unprepared spokespeople are how small issues become large crises. Compliance can be met by attendance records while briefing materials go unread. Read with Media Training recency and incident-response readiness exercises to confirm preparedness is real.

## Formula

```
Interviews per Approved Messaging / Interviews
```
