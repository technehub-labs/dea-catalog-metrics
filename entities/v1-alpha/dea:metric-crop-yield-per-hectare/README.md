## 2. Definition

Within Agriculture & Food Processing, Crop Yield per Hectare (CYH) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Harvested Crop Mass / Cultivated Hectares; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). In Agriculture & food, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Crop yield per hectare is agriculture's core productivity metric, and it is where seed genetics, soil health, water management, and agronomy skill compound into one number. It's also a masterclass in diminishing returns and gaming: pushing yield with nitrogen and irrigation inflates near-term output while mining soil organic matter and water tables, and yield achieved says nothing about input cost or margin per hectare. Read alongside input-use efficiency (kg of grain per kg of nitrogen), soil-health indicators, and five-year trend lines, since single-season yields are hostage to weather and tell you little about management quality.

## Formula

```
Harvested Crop Mass / Cultivated Hectares
```
