## 2. Definition

Within Corporate Venturing & Partnerships, Integration Success of Acquired Startups (ISAS) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Acquisitions Retaining Founders and Targets at 24 Months / Acquisitions; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether acquired ventures retain talent, deliver planned technology or revenue, and achieve target synergies post-acquisition. Startup acquisitions often fail at integration: founders exit, cultures clash, and the acquired roadmap stalls. Define success pre-close - retention of named key people, milestone delivery, synergy realization - and resist post-hoc redefinitions. Read with regretted attrition in acquired teams and venture portfolio return multiple.

## Formula

```
Acquisitions Retaining Founders and Targets at 24 Months / Acquisitions
```
