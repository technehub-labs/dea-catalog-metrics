## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard - Internal Process Perspective indicator, Innovation Process Cycle Time (IPCT-BSC) quantifies capability maturity or development. The formula is Days from Idea to Market Launch - reported in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures elapsed time from idea capture through the innovation funnel to launch decision. Slow funnels kill ideas by attrition before judgment ever occurs. Cutting cycle time by skipping validation converts speed into failure rate. Read with stage gate bypass rate and new product revenue share.

## Formula

```
IPCT-BSC = Days from Idea to Market Launch
```
