## 2. Definition

Service Center Wait Time (Public) (SCWTP) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Median Wait at Citizen Service Points; the result is stated in minutes. In Public sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
