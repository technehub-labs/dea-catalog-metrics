## 2. Definition

Within Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety Standard Measures, Care Plan Adherence Rate (CPAR-HC) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Documented Care Interventions per Care Plan / Planned Interventions - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Documented Care Interventions per Care Plan / Planned Interventions
```
