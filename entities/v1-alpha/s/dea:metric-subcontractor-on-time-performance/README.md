## 2. Definition

A Construction & Engineering indicator, Subcontractor On-Time Performance (SOTP) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It is defined as Subcontractor Milestones Met on Time / Milestones Planned; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Construction & engineering, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures subcontractor delivery against committed schedule milestones. It matters because critical-path subcontractor failure delays the entire project. Subcontractors can meet dates on paper while delivering incomplete or nonconforming work. Read with Rework Rate (Construction) and Subcontractor Quality findings to confirm punctuality includes work acceptance.

## Formula

```
SOTP = Subcontractor Milestones Met on Time / Milestones Planned
```
