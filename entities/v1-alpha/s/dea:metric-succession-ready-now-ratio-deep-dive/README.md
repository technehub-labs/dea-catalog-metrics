## 2. Definition

Succession Ready-now Ratio (Deep Dive) (SRNRD) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Standard Workforce & Pay Metrics domain. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Critical Roles with Ready-Now Successor / Critical Roles, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical roles with a successor ready to step in today. Ready-now is the honest core of succession: the rest is aspiration. Readiness judged without trial assignments is opinion. Read with bench strength index (Deep Dive) and succession plan coverage (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
SRNRD = Critical Roles with Ready-Now Successor / Critical Roles
```
