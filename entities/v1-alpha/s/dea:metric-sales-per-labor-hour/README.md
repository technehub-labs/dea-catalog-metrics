## 2. Definition

Sales per Labor Hour (SPLH) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of retail standard measures reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Net Sales / Store Labor Hours - reported in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Retail & E-commerce sector.

## Formula

```
SPLH = Net Sales / Store Labor Hours
```
