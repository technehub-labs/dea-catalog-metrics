## 2. Definition

A Publishing & News Media indicator, Sell-Through Rate (Books) (STRB) quantifies growth over time. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Retail Sales / Copies Distributed, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
STRB = Retail Sales / Copies Distributed
```
