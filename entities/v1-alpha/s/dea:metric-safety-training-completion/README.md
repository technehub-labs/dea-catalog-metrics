## 2. Definition

Safety Training Completion (STC) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Health & Safety domain. It is defined as Employees Completing Safety Training / Required; the result is stated in percent (%). It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
STC = Employees Completing Safety Training / Required
```
