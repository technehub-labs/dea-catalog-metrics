## 2. Definition

A Broadcasting & Streaming Media indicator, Startup Video Rebuffer Ratio (SVRR) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Rebuffer Time / Play Time, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
SVRR = Rebuffer Time / Play Time
```
