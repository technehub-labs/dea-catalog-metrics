## 2. Definition

Strategic Alliance Governance Cadence Compliance (SAGCC) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking alliances & partnerships management. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Joint Steering Meetings Held per Charter / Scheduled - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether alliance governance bodies meet as scheduled with executive participation. Governance is what keeps partnerships alive after the launch enthusiasm. Meetings without decision authority are social events. Read with alliance health score and joint business plan attainment rate.

## Formula

```
SAGCC = Joint Steering Meetings Held per Charter / Scheduled
```
