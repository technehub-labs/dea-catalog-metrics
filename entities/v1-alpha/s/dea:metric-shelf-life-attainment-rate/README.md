## 2. Definition

Shelf Life Attainment Rate (SLAR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of food & beverage manufacturing reporting. The formula is Batches Meeting Target Shelf Life / Batches - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Food & Beverage sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
SLAR = Batches Meeting Target Shelf Life / Batches
```
