## 2. Definition

Share of Search (Deep Dive) (SOSDD) measures growth over time in the Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Brand Searches / Total Category Searches, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the brand's share of branded search volume in the category. Search share is the digital-age proxy for demand share. Branded searches counted without bot filtering are inflated. Read with share of wallet (Deep Dive) and organic search conversion rate.

## Formula

```
SOSDD = Brand Searches / Total Category Searches
```
