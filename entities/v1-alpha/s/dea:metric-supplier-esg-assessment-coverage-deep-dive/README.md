## 2. Definition

Supplier ESG Assessment Coverage (Deep Dive) (SEAC) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking environmental, social & governance reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Suppliers Assessed / In-Scope Suppliers; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of supplier spend or risk-weighted suppliers assessed against ESG criteria. Supply chains are where most human rights and emissions exposure lives, and coverage is the control metric regulators increasingly mandate (import bans, due diligence laws). Coverage weighted by risk concentrates effort where harm is probable, rather than flattening across a long tail. Read with corrective-action closure rates and audit integrity sampling.

## Formula

```
SEAC = Suppliers Assessed / In-Scope Suppliers
```
