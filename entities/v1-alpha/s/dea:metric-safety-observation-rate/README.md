## 2. Definition

Safety Observation Rate (SOR) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of health & safety reporting. It is computed as Behavioral Safety Observations Completed per Employee, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
SOR = Behavioral Safety Observations Completed per Employee
```
