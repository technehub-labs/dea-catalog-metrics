## 2. Definition

A Marketing indicator, Share of Voice (SOV) quantifies growth over time. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Brand Mentions / Total Category Mentions, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

A brand's proportion of category conversation - classically of advertising exposure, now of earned and social mentions - against the total available. Its strategic force is the empirically stubborn link between excess share of voice over share of market and market-share growth, a relationship advertising researchers have documented across decades and which remains the main quantitative argument for maintaining brand investment in downturns. The metric has two regimes: paid SOV, which is directly purchasable and shows up in the P&L, and organic SOV, which is earned and decays when product or reputation fails. The actionable read is SOV relative to market share: brands spending above their weight tend to grow, those below tend to be harvesting.

## Formula

```
SOV = Brand Mentions / Total Category Mentions
```
