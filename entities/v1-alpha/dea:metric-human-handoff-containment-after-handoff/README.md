## 2. Definition

Human Handoff Containment after Handoff (HCHH) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking conversational ai & chatbots. The formula is Handoffs Resolved by Agent without Repeat Contact / Handoffs - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Handoffs Resolved by Agent without Repeat Contact / Handoffs
```
