## 2. Definition

Right First Time (Apparel) (RFT-A) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking textile & apparel. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Garments Passing Inspection First Time / Garments Produced, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Textile & apparel practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of garments passing quality inspection without rework. It matters because rework in apparel consumes margin and delays seasonal delivery windows. Standards can be loosened at the factory to lift pass rates, deferring defects to customer returns. Read with Return Rate and Quality Audit results to confirm first-time quality reaches the customer.

## Formula

```
Garments Passing Inspection First Time / Garments Produced
```
