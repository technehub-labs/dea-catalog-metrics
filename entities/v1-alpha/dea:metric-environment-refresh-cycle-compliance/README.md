## 2. Definition

Environment Refresh Cycle Compliance (ERCC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of release management & environments reporting. The formula is Refreshes Completed per Policy Schedule / Due - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Refreshes Completed per Policy Schedule / Due
```
