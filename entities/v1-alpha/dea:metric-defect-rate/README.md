## 2. Definition

Defect Rate (DR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of quality management reporting. The formula is Defective Units / Total Units - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Defective Units / Total Units
```
