## 2. Definition

Within Risk & Compliance, Open Regulatory Issues Age (ORIA) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. The formula is Median Age of Open Regulatory Issues - reported in days. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the average age of unresolved regulatory issues or findings. It matters because aging issues are escalating risk and a visible sign of compliance dysfunction. Teams can stop the clock by disputing findings or moving items to watch status. Read with Regulatory Finding Closure Time and enforcement action trends to confirm aging reflects genuine resolution progress.

## Formula

```
Median Age of Open Regulatory Issues
```
