## 2. Definition

On-Time In-Full Rate (Deep Dive) (OTIFDD) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of supply chain standard measures (deep dive) reporting. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Order Lines On Time and Complete / Order Lines - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of orders delivered complete and on schedule, combined. OTIF is the retailer-standard delivery promise metric. Windows defined generously make the metric a compliance formality. Read with perfect order rate (Deep Dive) and order fulfilment lead time trend (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Order Lines On Time and Complete / Order Lines
```
