## 2. Definition

Overtime Hours Ratio (OHR-WF) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of workforce planning & productivity reporting. The formula is Overtime Hours / Standard Hours - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Overtime Hours / Standard Hours
```
