## 2. Definition

Golden Record Trust Score (GRTS) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Master Data Management domain. It is computed as Business-Reported Trust in Golden Records (Survey), expressed in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures user confidence in and adoption of the golden record as the authoritative source. Trust is the adoption test: golden records nobody uses are museum pieces. Trust surveyed from the governance team rather than consumers is echo. Read with golden record confidence score and single source of truth metric adoption.

## Formula

```
Business-Reported Trust in Golden Records (Survey)
```
