## 2. Definition

Sales per Rep per Month (SPM) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking sales. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Monthly Closed Revenue / Active Sales Reps - reported in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Monthly Closed Revenue / Active Sales Reps
```
