## 2. Definition

A Education & Training (Deep Dive) indicator, Employment Outcome Rate (Deep Dive) (EOR-D) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. The formula is Graduates Employed in Field / Graduates - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Education sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of graduates employed in relevant fields within a standard window. It is the market-verdict metric of education quality, and the one learners and funders increasingly price. Field-of-study relevance matters - any-employment rates flatter programs whose graduates abandon the field - and verification against tax or payroll data beats self-report surveys. Read with employer satisfaction evidence and earnings-premium analysis.

## Formula

```
Graduates Employed in Field / Graduates
```
