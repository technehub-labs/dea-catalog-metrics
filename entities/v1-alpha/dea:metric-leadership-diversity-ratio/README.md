## 2. Definition

Within Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive), Leadership Diversity Ratio (LDR-DEI) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. Its calculation is Underrepresented Groups in Leadership / Leadership Roles, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures demographic diversity specifically in leadership and senior roles. Leadership representation is where pipeline promises are tested. Ratios computed below genuine decision-making layers overstate progress. Read with promotion equity ratio and succession plan coverage (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Underrepresented Groups in Leadership / Leadership Roles
```
