## 2. Definition

A Franchise Operations (Deep Dive) indicator, Territory Cannibalization Rate (TCR-F) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Same-Brand Sales Decline after New Opening / Baseline; the result is stated in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures sales lost by existing units to new same-brand locations. Franchise systems face a structural conflict - the franchisor earns on system growth while incumbent franchisees absorb displacement - and this metric keeps the tension honest. Attribution methodology is contested by definition, so demand consistent measurement and dispute-resolution evidence. Read with system-wide same-store sales growth (Deep Dive) and territory-approval governance.

## Formula

```
Same-Brand Sales Decline after New Opening / Baseline
```
