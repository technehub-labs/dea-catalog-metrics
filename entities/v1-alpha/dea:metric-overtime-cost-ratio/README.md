## 2. Definition

A Standard Workforce & Pay Metrics indicator, Overtime Cost Ratio (OCR-PAY) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Overtime Pay / Total Labor Cost - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Overtime Pay / Total Labor Cost
```
