## 2. Definition

Partner Enablement Certification Rate (PECR) captures capability maturity or development for organizations tracking alliances & partnerships management. Its calculation is Partner Staff Certified / Required Certifications, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Partner Staff Certified / Required Certifications
```
