## 2. Definition

Workforce Plan Accuracy (WPA-WF) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of workforce planning & productivity reporting. It is computed as Planned vs Actual Headcount Variance / Plan, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures how closely workforce demand forecasts matched actual needs. Accuracy determines whether hiring pipelines deliver the right people at the right time. Accuracy on headcount while missing skills mix is half the truth. Read with capacity-to-demand match index and strategic headcount gap ratio.

## Formula

```
Planned vs Actual Headcount Variance / Plan
```
