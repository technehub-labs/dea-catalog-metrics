## 2. Definition

Rebalancing Policy Adherence (RPA-W) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking asset & wealth management (deep dive). Its calculation is Rebalances per Policy / Rebalancing Triggers, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Rebalances per Policy / Rebalancing Triggers
```
