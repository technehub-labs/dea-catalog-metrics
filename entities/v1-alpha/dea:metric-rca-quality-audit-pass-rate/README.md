## 2. Definition

RCA Quality Audit Pass Rate (RQAPR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of it incident & major problem management reporting. Its calculation is RCAs Meeting Quality Bar / Audited, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
RCAs Meeting Quality Bar / Audited
```
