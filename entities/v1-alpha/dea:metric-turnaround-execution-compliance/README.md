## 2. Definition

Turnaround Execution Compliance (TAEC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of oil & gas reporting. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Turnarounds Completed on Schedule and Budget / Turnarounds, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Oil & gas practice.

## 3. Context

Measures whether refinery turnarounds complete on schedule and scope. It matters because turnaround overruns are massive cost and lost-production events. Compliance can be declared while punch-list items carry into normal operations. Read with Post-Turnaround Reliability and Cost Variance to confirm turnarounds truly finished.

## Formula

```
Turnarounds Completed on Schedule and Budget / Turnarounds
```
