## 2. Definition

Within Retail & E-commerce, Customer Repeat Rate within 90 Days (CRR90) is used to gauge the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. It is defined as Customers Purchasing Again within 90 Days / Customers; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Retail, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of customers purchasing again within 90 days of their first purchase. It matters because early repeat behavior is the strongest predictor of durable customer economics. Heavy first-order coupons can buy a second discounted purchase without real loyalty. Read with Repeat Purchase Rate and Customer Lifetime Value to confirm early repeats become habitual customers.

## Formula

```
Customers Purchasing Again within 90 Days / Customers
```
