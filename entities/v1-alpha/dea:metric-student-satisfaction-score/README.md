## 2. Definition

Student Satisfaction Score (SSS) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking education. The formula is Satisfied Student Survey Responses / Responses - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Education sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures student ratings of courses, teaching, and experience. It matters because satisfaction drives retention, referrals, and rankings in competitive education markets. It can be gamed by surveying only engaged students or timing surveys before grading stress. Read with Course Completion Rate and post-graduation outcomes to confirm satisfaction tracks substance, not ease.

## Formula

```
Satisfied Student Survey Responses / Responses
```
