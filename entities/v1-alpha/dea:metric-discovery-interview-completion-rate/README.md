## 2. Definition

Within Requirements Engineering & Product Discovery, Discovery Interview Completion Rate (DICR-RE) is used to gauge how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its calculation is Customer Discovery Interviews Completed per Cycle / Planned, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Customer Discovery Interviews Completed per Cycle / Planned
```
