## 2. Definition

Scope 1 Emissions Intensity (S1EI) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking environmental, social & governance reporting. It is defined as Scope 1 tCO2e / Revenue Unit; the result is stated in tonnes CO2 equivalent (tCO2e). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures direct emissions normalized by output. It is the primary-decarbonization metric, and intensity framing separates real reduction from production declines - though intensity improvements that accompany absolute growth do not serve climate goals. Calculation methodology consistency (boundaries, factors) determines comparability across years. Read with science-based target progress and abatement-project pipeline delivery.

## Formula

```
S1EI = Scope 1 tCO2e / Revenue Unit
```
