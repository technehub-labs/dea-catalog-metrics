## 2. Definition

Biodiversity Index - Farm (BIF) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking agriculture & agronomy (deep dive). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Composite Habitat and Species Score, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Agriculture.

## 3. Context

Measures on-farm species and habitat diversity against baseline or regional references. It is the licence-to-operate metric as agricultural sustainability regulation and buyer requirements harden, and it also proxies long-term ecosystem service health (pollination, pest control). Index construction is contested, so methodology stability matters more than level. Read with habitat-area share and regenerative-practice adoption rates.

BIF = Composite Habitat and Species Score
