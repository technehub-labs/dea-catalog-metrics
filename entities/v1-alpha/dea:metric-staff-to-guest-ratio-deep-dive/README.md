## 2. Definition

Staff-to-Guest Ratio (Deep Dive) (SGR-D) measures the utilization or availability of capacity in the Hotel & Resort Operations (Deep Dive) domain. The formula is Staff / Guests in House - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is a standard measure in the Hospitality sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.
