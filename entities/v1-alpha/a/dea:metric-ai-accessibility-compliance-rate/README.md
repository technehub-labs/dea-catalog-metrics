## 2. Definition

AI Accessibility Compliance Rate (AAICR) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai reporting. The formula is AI Interfaces Meeting Accessibility Standards / Interfaces - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
AAICR = AI Interfaces Meeting Accessibility Standards / Interfaces
```
