## 2. Definition

Average Length of Trip (ALOT) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking travel & tourism. The formula is Total Traveler Nights / Trips - reported in days. It is a standard measure in the Travel & tourism sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures mean nights or days per booked trip. It matters because trip length drives per-booking revenue and destination economics. Length can be inflated through bundling that raises price per booking without real value. Read with Average Daily Rate and Guest Satisfaction to confirm longer trips are higher-value, not just higher-priced.

## Formula

```
ALOT = Total Traveler Nights / Trips
```
