## 2. Definition

Within Alliances & Partnerships Management, Alliance Risk Register Coverage (ARRC) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Alliances with Joint Risk Register / Strategic Alliances, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely alliance-specific risks are identified, owned, and reviewed. Partnerships fail on unmanaged risks (dependence, misalignment, leakage) more than on performance. Registers reviewed only at renewal are archaeology. Read with strategic dependency concentration (Alliances) and alliance health score.

## Formula

```
ARRC = Alliances with Joint Risk Register / Strategic Alliances
```
