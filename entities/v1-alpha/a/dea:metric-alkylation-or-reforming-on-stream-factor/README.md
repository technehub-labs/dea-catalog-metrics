## 2. Definition

Alkylation or Reforming On-Stream Factor (AOSF) captures the utilization or availability of capacity for organizations tracking oil & gas downstream & refining. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Unit Operating Time / Calendar Time, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Oil & Gas practice.

## Formula

```
AOSF = Unit Operating Time / Calendar Time
```
