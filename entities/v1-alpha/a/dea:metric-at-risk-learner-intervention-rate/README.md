## 2. Definition

At-Risk Learner Intervention Rate (ALIR) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Education & Training (Deep Dive) domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is At-Risk Learners Receiving Support / Identified - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Education sector.

## Formula

```
ALIR = At-Risk Learners Receiving Support / Identified
```
