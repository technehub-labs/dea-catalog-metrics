## 2. Definition

A Investment Research & Trading Operations indicator, Algorithmic Execution Adoption Rate (AEAR-T) quantifies capability maturity or development. Its calculation is Orders via Algos / Orders, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
AEAR-T = Orders via Algos / Orders
```
