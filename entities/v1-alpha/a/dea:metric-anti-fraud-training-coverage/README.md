## 2. Definition

Anti-Fraud Training Coverage (AFTC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of enterprise fraud & financial crime management reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Staff Completing Fraud Awareness Training / Staff in Scope - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
AFTC = Staff Completing Fraud Awareness Training / Staff in Scope
```
