## 2. Definition

Attention Metric Score (Ads) (AMS) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of advertising & media buying reporting. Its calculation is Composite Attention Measurement Score, measured in score (points). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.
