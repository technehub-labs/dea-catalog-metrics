## 2. Definition

Within Arts & Culture, Attendance per Capita of Catchment (APCC) is used to gauge growth over time. It is computed as Attendance / Catchment Population, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Its use is concentrated in Arts & culture. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Attendance per capita of catchment measures admissions against the population within the market area, and it is the market-penetration metric of arts organizations - the honest size-adjustment that lets a small-city institution compare its engagement against a metro institution without the population-size distortion. The catchment definition is the honest-measurement challenge: drive-time bands, tourist flow, and commuter patterns all complicate it, and organizations choose definitions that flatter. The metric is the demand-side view of mission (community engagement per resident) and the constraint on programming ambition (attendance potential sets the revenue ceiling). Read with penetration by demographic segment, tourist versus resident attendance share, and repeat-attendance rate since per-capita figures driven by a small hyper-engaged core differ meaningfully from broad engagement.

## Formula

```
APCC = Attendance / Catchment Population
```
