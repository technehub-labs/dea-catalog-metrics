## 2. Definition

Supplier Statement Reconciliation Rate (SSRR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking accounts payable & receivable operations (deep dive). It is defined as Statements Reconciled Monthly / Suppliers; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Statements Reconciled Monthly / Suppliers
```
