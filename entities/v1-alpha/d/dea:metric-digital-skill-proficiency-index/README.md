## 2. Definition

Digital Skill Proficiency Index (DSPI-BSC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Assessed Proficiency across Digital Skill Framework; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures workforce proficiency against the digital capabilities the operating model requires. Digital transformation stalls at the skill layer regardless of technology spend. Self-rated proficiency correlates poorly with tested capability. Read with strategic competency coverage and training investment per employee.

## Formula

```
DSPI-BSC = Assessed Proficiency across Digital Skill Framework
```
