## 2. Definition

Days on Market (Real Estate) (DOM-RE) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of real estate & mortgage standard measures reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Average Days from Listing to Contract - reported in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Real estate sector.

## Formula
