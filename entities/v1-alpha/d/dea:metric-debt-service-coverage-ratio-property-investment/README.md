## 2. Definition

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (Property Investment) (DSCRPR) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking real estate & mortgage standard measures. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Net Operating Income / Annual Debt Service, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Real estate.

## 3. Context

Measures property cash flow available to service its debt. DSCR is the viability test of income-property lending. Pro-forma rents in the numerator make the ratio fictional until achieved. Read with debt service coverage ratio (Deep Dive) and capitalization rate.

## Formula

```
DSCRPR = Net Operating Income / Annual Debt Service
```
