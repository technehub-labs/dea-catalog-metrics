## 2. Definition

Digital Subscription Conversion Rate (Deep Dive) (DSCR-D) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of publishing & news media reporting. It is defined as New Digital Subscribers / Anonymous Visitors; the result is stated in percent (%). In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of registered or anonymous readers converting to paid digital subscriptions. It is the pivot metric of publishing's business model transition, and it is bounded by the metering decision: paywalls too hard suppress habit formation, too soft never convert. Conversion must be read with churn and lifetime value, since aggressive trial offers inflate conversion with low-intent subscribers. Read with fact-check compliance rate as the product-quality input and ARPU - Publishing (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
DSCR-D = New Digital Subscribers / Anonymous Visitors
```
