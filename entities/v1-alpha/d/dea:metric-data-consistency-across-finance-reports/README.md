## 2. Definition

Data Consistency across Finance Reports (DCFPR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking financial planning & analysis. It is computed as Reports Reconciled to Single Source / Reports, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
DCFPR = Reports Reconciled to Single Source / Reports
```
