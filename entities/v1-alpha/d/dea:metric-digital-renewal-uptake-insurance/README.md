## 2. Definition

Within Insurance Distribution - Direct & Digital, Digital Renewal Uptake (Insurance) (DRU-I) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its calculation is Renewals Completed Digitally / Renewals, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Insurance practice.

## Formula

```
DRU-I = Renewals Completed Digitally / Renewals
```
