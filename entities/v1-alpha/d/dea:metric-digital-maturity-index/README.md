## 2. Definition

Digital Maturity Index (DMI) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of digital transformation reporting. The formula is Weighted Index of Strategy, Technology, Data, Process, Culture Dimensions - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

A composite score across capability dimensions - technology, talent, operations, data, culture - summarizing digital progress on a single scale. It is useful for board narrative and benchmarking, and dangerous as a management target: composite indices can be improved by gaming sub-scores with weak evidence. Insist on transparent scoring rubrics, independent assessment, and the maturity-to-value link in the same conversation. Read with digital revenue share and digital initiative value realization rather than treating the index as the outcome.

## Formula

```
DMI = Weighted Index of Strategy, Technology, Data, Process, Culture Dimensions
```
