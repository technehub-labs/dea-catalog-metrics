## 2. Definition

Syndication and Licensing Revenue Ratio (SLRR) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of publishing & news media reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Licensing Revenue / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. It is a standard measure in the Media & Entertainment sector.

## Formula

```
Licensing Revenue / Total Revenue
```
