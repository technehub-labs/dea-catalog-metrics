## 2. Definition

Labeling Accuracy Rate (Food) (LAR-F) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of food & beverage manufacturing reporting. It is computed as Labels Matching Specification / Labels Sampled, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Food & Beverage.

## Formula

```
LAR-F = Labels Matching Specification / Labels Sampled
```
