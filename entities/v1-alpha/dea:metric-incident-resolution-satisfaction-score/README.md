## 2. Definition

Incident Resolution Satisfaction Score (IRSS-IT) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the IT Incident & Major Problem Management domain. It is defined as User CSAT after Incident Resolution; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
