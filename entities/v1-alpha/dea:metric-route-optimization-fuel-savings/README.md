## 2. Definition

A Parcel & Last-Mile Logistics (Deep Dive) indicator, Route Optimization Fuel Savings (ROFS) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. The formula is Fuel Saved vs Baseline / Baseline - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Logistics & Trade sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
ROFS = Fuel Saved vs Baseline / Baseline
```
