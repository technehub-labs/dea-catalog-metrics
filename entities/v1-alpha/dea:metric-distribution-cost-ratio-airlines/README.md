## 2. Definition

A Airline Operations (Deep Dive) indicator, Distribution Cost Ratio (Airlines) (DCR-A) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Distribution Costs / Revenue; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. In Airlines, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures distribution spend - GDS fees, OTA commissions, agency incentives - against revenue. It is the margin battleground of airline distribution, and direct-channel strategies (website, app, NDC connections) exist to move it. Direct-shift savings that come with lost corporate-travel visibility can cost more than they save, so read channel shift with segment revenue retention. Read with direct-booking share and corporate-contract compliance.

## Formula

```
Distribution Costs / Revenue
```
