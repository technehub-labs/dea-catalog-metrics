## 2. Definition

Crisis Fund Disbursement Time (CFDT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Crisis Management & Emergency Response domain. It is computed as Hours from Approval to Funds Available in Crisis, expressed in hours. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly emergency funds reach affected operations or people. Slow disbursement turns financial preparedness into a rumor of it. Times measured from approval rather than crisis start hide the true gap. Read with emergency assistance disbursement time and cash buffer coverage.

## Formula

```
Hours from Approval to Funds Available in Crisis
```
