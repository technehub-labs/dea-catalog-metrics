## 2. Definition

Continuity Training Completion Rate (CTCR) measures capability maturity or development in the Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery domain. It is computed as Staff Completing Continuity Role Training / Staff in Roles, expressed in percent (%). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing Continuity Role Training / Staff in Roles
```
