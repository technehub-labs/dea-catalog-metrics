## 2. Definition

A Operations Energy Management indicator, Energy Cost Ratio to Output (ECRO) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Total Energy Cost / Units of Output, expressed in currency per unit. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures energy cost as a share of production value or total operating cost. It converts energy strategy into plant economics and price-shock exposure analysis, and it identifies where efficiency investment pays fastest. Ratio moves need decomposition: energy price, intensity, and product mix each drive it differently, and only intensity is operationally controllable. Read with energy intensity trends and energy savings project realization.

## Formula

```
Total Energy Cost / Units of Output
```
