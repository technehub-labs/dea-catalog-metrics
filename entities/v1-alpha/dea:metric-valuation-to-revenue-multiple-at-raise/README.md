## 2. Definition

Valuation-to-Revenue Multiple at Raise (VRMR) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of startup & venture-backed business reporting. Its calculation is Post-Money Valuation / Trailing Revenue, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the valuation paid by the market at each raise against revenue. It prices both the company's growth quality and the financing climate, and mismatched multiples at raise (too high) set up down rounds and repricing trauma later. Multiple context (sector, growth rate, margin) determines whether the number is confidence or froth. Read with the growth rates and retention metrics that justify it, and follow-on round outcomes.

## Formula

```
Post-Money Valuation / Trailing Revenue
```
