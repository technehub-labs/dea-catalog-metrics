## 2. Definition

Direct Booking Ratio (Deep Dive) (DBR-D) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of hotel & resort operations (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Direct Reservations / Total Reservations, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Hospitality.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of room nights booked through brand or property channels rather than intermediaries. It is the margin and data-ownership metric of hotel distribution: direct bookings avoid commission and retain the guest relationship for remarketing. Watch for ratio gains from withholding inventory from OTAs that then lose total demand. Read with OTA commission ratio (Deep Dive) and loyalty member stay share (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Direct Reservations / Total Reservations
```
