## 2. Definition

Free Cash Flow Yield (FCFY) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking valuation & corporate finance standard measures. It is defined as Free Cash Flow / Enterprise Value; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures free cash flow relative to enterprise value. It is the cash-based valuation reality check on market prices. FCF computed before maintenance capex is not free. Read with EBITDA margin (Deep Dive) and earnings yield (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Free Cash Flow / Enterprise Value
```
