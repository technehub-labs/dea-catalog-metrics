## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective, Customer Data Accuracy Perception Score (CDAP) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Customer-Rated Accuracy of Communications and Records, expressed in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

Customer-Rated Accuracy of Communications and Records
