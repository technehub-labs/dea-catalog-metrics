## 2. Definition

A Veterinary & Animal Health indicator, Treatment Plan Compliance Rate (Vet) (TPCR-V) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Completed Plans / Prescribed Plans, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Healthcare.

## Formula

```
TPCR-V = Completed Plans / Prescribed Plans
```
