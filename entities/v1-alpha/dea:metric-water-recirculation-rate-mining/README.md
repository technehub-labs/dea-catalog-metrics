## 2. Definition

Water Recirculation Rate (Mining) (WRR-M) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking mining & metals. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It is defined as Recirculated Water / Total Water Used; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Mining & metals, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of process water recirculated rather than drawn fresh. It matters because mining is water-intensive in often scarce basins and recirculation cuts both risk and cost. Single-loop recirculation can be counted generously as full recycling. Read with Water Intensity and tailings water management data to confirm recirculation reflects genuine closed-loop practice.

## Formula

```
Recirculated Water / Total Water Used
```
