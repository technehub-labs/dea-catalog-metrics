## 2. Definition

Student Progression Rate (Deep Dive) (SPR-D) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of education & training (deep dive) reporting. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Students Advancing to Next Level / Cohort - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Education sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of learners advancing through program milestones on schedule (credit accumulation, year-to-year advancement). It is the early-warning metric of student success: progression problems surface years before completion statistics do, while there is still time to intervene. Disaggregation by entry qualification and support-usage identifies which interventions work. Read with course completion rate (Deep Dive) and at-risk early-alert response times.

## Formula

```
Students Advancing to Next Level / Cohort
```
