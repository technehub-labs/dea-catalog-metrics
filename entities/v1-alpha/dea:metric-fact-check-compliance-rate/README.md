## 2. Definition

A Publishing & News Media indicator, Fact-Check Compliance Rate (FCCR-P) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Stories Passing Fact-Check / Stories Checked; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to the editorial verification process - sourced claims, corrections standards, pre-publication review. It is the core trust-manufacturing metric of news: credibility is the asset, and it compounds or collapses with verification discipline. Compliance rates self-reported by desk are soft; sampled audits and corrections-latency measures harden them. Read with corrections volume and speed, and audience trust tracking.

## Formula

```
Stories Passing Fact-Check / Stories Checked
```
