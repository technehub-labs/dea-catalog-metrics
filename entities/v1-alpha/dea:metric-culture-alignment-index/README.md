## 2. Definition

Culture Alignment Index (CAI) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Culture & Employee Experience domain. The formula is Weighted Index of Values Alignment Survey Items - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

An aggregate measure of how well employee-reported values and behaviors match the organization's stated target culture. It matters because strategy execution bends toward culture, and misalignment quietly taxes every transformation. The honest-read cautions: survey wording shapes results, alignment without performance is complacency, and the index can rise while specific toxic subcultures persist. Always read segment-level distributions and free-text themes. Read with engagement scores and culture change adoption rate.

Weighted Index of Values Alignment Survey Items
