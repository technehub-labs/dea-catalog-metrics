## 2. Definition

Sponsor Visibility Score (SVS) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of change management reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Stakeholder-Perceived Executive Sponsorship Rating, expressed in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how consistently and credibly change sponsors communicate, remove obstacles, and model the change. Sponsor behavior is repeatedly the strongest predictor of change success, and its absence is the most cited reason for failure. The trap is measuring communication volume rather than perceived advocacy - employees discount town halls against what leaders demonstrably prioritize. Read with change readiness score and adoption rate of change.

## Formula

```
Stakeholder-Perceived Executive Sponsorship Rating
```
