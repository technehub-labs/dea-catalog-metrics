## 2. Definition

Within Customer Analytics & Insights, Customer Segmentation Coverage (CSC-A) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. It is defined as Customers with Active Segment Assignment / Customers; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of the customer base covered by maintained segments. It matters because segmentation is the targeting backbone of marketing and service differentiation. Coverage can be met with default segments that carry no behavioral meaning. Read with Segment Profitability Coverage and campaign targeting lift to confirm segments are used and useful.

## Formula

```
Customers with Active Segment Assignment / Customers
```
