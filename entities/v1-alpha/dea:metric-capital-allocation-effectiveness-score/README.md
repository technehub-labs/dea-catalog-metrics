## 2. Definition

Capital Allocation Effectiveness Score (CAES) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking valuation & corporate finance standard measures. It is defined as Composite of Value Created per Dollar Deployed across Uses; the result is stated in score (points). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how well the organization deploys capital across competing uses over time. Allocation quality is the least measured and most consequential of leadership disciplines. Scores built on realized IRRs alone miss risk-adjustment. Read with capital allocation ROI variance and capital allocation fulfilment rate.

## Formula

```
CAES = Composite of Value Created per Dollar Deployed across Uses
```
