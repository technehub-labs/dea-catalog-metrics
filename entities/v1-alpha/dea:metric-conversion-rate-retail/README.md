## 2. Definition

Conversion Rate (Retail) (CVR-R) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking retail & e-commerce. It is defined as Purchasing Customers / Store Visitors; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Retail, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of store or site visitors who complete a purchase. It matters because it converts traffic into sales and isolates merchandising and experience effectiveness. It can be gamed by counting only engaged visitors or by driving traffic down so only buyers remain. Read with Cart Abandonment Rate and Footfall trends to confirm conversion gains are genuine persuasion, not traffic decay.

## Formula

```
Purchasing Customers / Store Visitors
```
