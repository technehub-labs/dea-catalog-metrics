## 2. Definition

A Insurance Fraud & Financial Crime indicator, Investigation Cycle Time (Deep Dive) (ICT-D) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Days from Case Open to Disposition, expressed in days. Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula
