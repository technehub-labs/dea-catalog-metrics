## 2. Definition

Within Third-Party Risk Management, Continuity Attestation Coverage (Vendors) (CACV) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is computed as Critical Vendors Attesting BCP / Critical Vendors, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical vendors providing verified business continuity attestations. Vendor continuity is your continuity for outsourced functions. Attestations accepted without ever testing the substance are paper comfort. Read with supplier continuity assurance coverage (Deep Dive) and vendor exit plan coverage.

## Formula

```
Critical Vendors Attesting BCP / Critical Vendors
```
