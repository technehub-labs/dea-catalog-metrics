## 2. Definition

Personal Data Inventory Coverage (PDIC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of data privacy & protection reporting. It is computed as Processing Activities Documented in ROPA / Identified Activities, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely the organization's personal data holdings are mapped and inventoried. You cannot protect or honor rights over data you don't know you hold. Inventories that exclude spreadsheets and shadow systems miss where risk actually lives. Read with data classification coverage and privacy impact assessment rate.

## Formula

```
Processing Activities Documented in ROPA / Identified Activities
```
