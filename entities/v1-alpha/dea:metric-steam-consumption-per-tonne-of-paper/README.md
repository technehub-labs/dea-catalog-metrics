## 2. Definition

Steam Consumption per Tonne of Paper (SCPT) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of pulp, paper & forest products reporting. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Steam Used / Production Tonnes, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice.

## Formula

```
Steam Used / Production Tonnes
```
