## 2. Definition

Digital Identity Adoption Rate (DIA) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of government & public administration reporting. It is computed as Citizens Using National Digital Identity / Eligible Citizens, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Public Sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of citizens or businesses using the national or agency digital identity for services. It is the platform metric of digital government: high adoption collapses authentication cost and enables integrated services across agencies. Trust and privacy safeguards drive adoption more than features, and exclusion risks are concentrated in elderly and marginalized populations. Read with digital channel adoption (Government) and fraud rates in identity-verified services.

## Formula

```
Citizens Using National Digital Identity / Eligible Citizens
```
