## 2. Definition

Incident Communication Cadence Compliance (ICCC-IT) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the IT Incident & Major Problem Management domain. It is computed as Major Incidents with Timely Stakeholder Updates / Major Incidents, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Major Incidents with Timely Stakeholder Updates / Major Incidents
```
