## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective indicator, Succession Plan Coverage (Deep Dive) (SPC-D) quantifies capability maturity or development. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Critical Roles with Ready Successors / Critical Roles, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical roles with a viable, tested succession plan. Coverage is the antidote to key-person risk and the leading indicator of leadership pipeline health. Plans listing a name without readiness assessment are paperwork. Read with leadership pipeline strength index and critical talent retention rate.

## Formula

```
Critical Roles with Ready Successors / Critical Roles
```
