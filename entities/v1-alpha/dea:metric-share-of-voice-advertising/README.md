## 2. Definition

Share of Voice (Advertising) (SOVDD) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of marketing & advertising standard measures (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Owned Impressions / Category Impressions - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the brand's share of category advertising impressions. SOV above market share predicts share growth in classical advertising theory. Voice counted on spend rather than delivered impressions misweights media efficiency. Read with brand lift (Ad Recall) and share of search (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Owned Impressions / Category Impressions
```
