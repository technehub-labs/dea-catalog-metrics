## 2. Definition

Three Lines Model Compliance Score (TLMCS) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of enterprise decision effectiveness & governance monitoring reporting. The formula is Composite Independence and Coverage Assessment - reported in score (points). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures structural conformance with separation of management, risk/compliance oversight, and independent assurance. Blurred lines are how both control failures and their concealment happen. Structural conformance without behavioral independence is shell compliance. Read with assurance map coverage and internal audit coverage.

## Formula

```
TLMCS = Composite Independence and Coverage Assessment
```
