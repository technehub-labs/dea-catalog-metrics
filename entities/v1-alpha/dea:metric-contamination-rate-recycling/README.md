## 2. Definition

Contamination Rate (Recycling) (CNTR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking waste & environmental services. The formula is Non-Recyclable Items in Recycling Stream / Recycling Stream - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Environmental services sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of non-target or unusable material in recycled streams. It matters because contamination destroys recyclate value and can make whole loads unmarketable. Sampling only easy streams can flatter the average. Read with Rejected Load trends and Material Recovery Facility output quality to confirm the true contamination picture.

## Formula

```
Non-Recyclable Items in Recycling Stream / Recycling Stream
```
