## 2. Definition

Speed to First Repeat Purchase (SFRP-S) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of startup & venture-backed business reporting. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Days from First to Second Purchase, expressed in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.
