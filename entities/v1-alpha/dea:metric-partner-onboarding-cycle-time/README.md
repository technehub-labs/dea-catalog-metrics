## 2. Definition

Partner Onboarding Cycle Time (POCT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Alliances & Partnerships Management domain. It is defined as Days from Agreement to First Transaction; the result is stated in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
