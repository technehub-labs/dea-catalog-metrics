## 2. Definition

Emergency Communication Reach Rate (ECRR) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking business continuity & disaster recovery. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Staff Reached within Target Time / Staff in Test, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Staff Reached within Target Time / Staff in Test
```
