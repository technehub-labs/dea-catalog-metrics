## 2. Definition

Scope 1+2 Emissions Reduction (S12R) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Sustainability & ESG domain. The formula is (Base-Year Emissions - Current Emissions) / Base-Year Emissions - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

The percentage reduction in combined direct and purchased-energy emissions against a baseline year - the headline progress metric of corporate climate strategy, and the one that disclosure regimes and science-based target frameworks track against stated trajectories. Its strategic content is the baseline problem: reduction is measured against a chosen base year, and the choice (often a high-emissions year near the start of the reporting era) can contribute more percentage points than any operational change - which is why credible reporting shows absolute current emissions alongside the percentage. Read the driver decomposition: reductions from efficiency, from energy-source switching, and from portfolio change (divestitures, outsourcing) have entirely different durability, and a trajectory built on divestitures does not decarbonize the planet, only the perimeter. The honest trajectory test is against the linear interim milestone, not the distant end-state.

## Formula

```
(Base-Year Emissions - Current Emissions) / Base-Year Emissions
```
