## 2. Definition

Digital Initiative Value Realization (DIVR) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Digital Transformation domain. The formula is Realized Benefits / Planned Benefits of Digital Portfolio - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Tracks the share of business-case benefits from digital programs that is actually confirmed in run-rate results after delivery. It matters because the value gap between promised and realized digital benefits is consistently large, and unverified cases compound into unfundable roadmaps. Insist on benefit confirmation by finance against a pre-agreed baseline, and beware benefits claimed from re-baselining volume effects. Read with digital investment ratio and cost reduction program realization patterns.

## Formula

```
Realized Benefits / Planned Benefits of Digital Portfolio
```
