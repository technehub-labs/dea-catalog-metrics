## 2. Definition

Youth Academy Progression Rate (YAPR) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of sports clubs & leagues (deep dive) reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Academy Players to First Team / Intake Cohort, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Sports.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of academy players advancing to professional contracts or first-team appearances. It is the yield metric of player development, and given transfer-market economics, a productive academy is among the highest-ROI investments a club can make. Long development windows demand cohort-based measurement, and progression achieved by lowering first-team standards is fake yield. Read with minutes played by academy graduates and transfer value generated.

## Formula

```
Academy Players to First Team / Intake Cohort
```
