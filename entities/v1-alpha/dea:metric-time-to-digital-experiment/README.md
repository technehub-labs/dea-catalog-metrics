## 2. Definition

Within Digital Transformation, Time to Digital Experiment (TTDE) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Days from Idea to Live Digital Experiment - reported in days. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures elapsed time from hypothesis to live customer-facing experiment, the metabolic rate of a digital organization. Fast cycle times matter because learning compounds: ten small tests per quarter beat one annual initiative regardless of individual test quality. Measurement traps include counting only sanctioned experiments and stopping the clock at launch rather than at decision. Read with experiment win rates and digital initiative value realization to see speed converting into value.

Days from Idea to Live Digital Experiment
