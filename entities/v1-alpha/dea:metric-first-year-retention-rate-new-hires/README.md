## 2. Definition

First-Year Retention Rate (New Hires) (FYRR) addresses the retention of customers, users, or assets over time and is a fixture of talent acquisition deep dive reporting. It is computed as Hires Retained at 12 Months / Hires, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of new hires still employed after twelve months. First-year attrition is usually a hiring or onboarding failure, and its cost is nearly total. Retention improved by lowering performance bars is fake retention. Read with new hire 90-day attrition rate and onboarding completion rate.

## Formula

```
Hires Retained at 12 Months / Hires
```
