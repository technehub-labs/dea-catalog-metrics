## 2. Definition

Records Management Training Coverage (RMTC) measures capability maturity or development in the Records & Information Management domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Staff Completing RIM Training / Staff Creating Records - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing RIM Training / Staff Creating Records
```
