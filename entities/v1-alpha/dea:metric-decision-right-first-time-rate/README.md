## 2. Definition

Within Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring, Decision Right First Time Rate (DRFT) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Decisions Not Revisited or Reversed within Year / Decisions - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how often decisions are made at the correct level on the first pass rather than escalated or re-made. First-time-right decisions signal well-calibrated delegation. Rates improve artificially when authority is centralized. Read with delegation of authority compliance and decision reversal rate.

## Formula

```
Decisions Not Revisited or Reversed within Year / Decisions
```
