## 2. Definition

Within Investment Risk & Middle Office Operations, Pre-Trade Compliance Check Coverage (PTCCC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Orders Screened Pre-Trade / Orders - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Investment management sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula
