## 2. Definition

Syndication Revenue Share (SRS-B) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of broadcasting & streaming media reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Syndication Revenue / Total Revenue, expressed in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue from licensing content to third parties - broadcasters, platforms, international markets. It is the asset-light monetization of content libraries, smoothing production volatility and squeezing value from sunk costs. Windowing strategy determines yield, and syndication that cannibalizes owned-platform exclusivity can be value-destructive despite the revenue. Read with library aging and rights availability schedules.

## Formula

```
Syndication Revenue / Total Revenue
```
