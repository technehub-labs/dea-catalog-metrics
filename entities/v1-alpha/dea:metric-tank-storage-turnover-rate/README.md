## 2. Definition

A Oil & Gas Downstream & Refining indicator, Tank Storage Turnover Rate (TSTR) quantifies the utilization or availability of capacity. It is defined as Annual Throughput / Average Tank Inventory; the result is stated in times per period. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Oil & Gas, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## Formula

```
Annual Throughput / Average Tank Inventory
```
