## 2. Definition

A Veterinary & Animal Health indicator, Appointment Utilization (Veterinary) (AUV) quantifies the utilization or availability of capacity. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Booked Slots / Available Slots - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector.

## Formula

```
Booked Slots / Available Slots
```
