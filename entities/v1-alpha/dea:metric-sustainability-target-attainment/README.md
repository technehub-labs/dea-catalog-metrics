## 2. Definition

Sustainability Target Attainment (STA) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of sustainability & esg reporting. Its calculation is Sustainability Targets Met / Targets Set, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

The share of published sustainability targets achieved on schedule - the accountability metric for commitments, which have proliferated faster than delivery: the research and journalistic tradition of tracking corporate climate pledges finds that misses and quietly narrowed targets outnumber hits in most sectors. Its strategic weight is credibility exposure: targets now appear in regulatory filings, bond documentation (sustainability-linked instruments attach coupon steps to them), and brand claims simultaneously, so a missed target can trigger financial consequences, greenwashing findings, and reputational cost in one event. The honest measurement watches target mutation - baselines rebased, scopes excluded, dates moved - which is how attainment is manufactured, and why serious attainment reporting publishes the target as originally stated alongside the result.

## Formula

```
Sustainability Targets Met / Targets Set
```
