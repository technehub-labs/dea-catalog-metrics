## 2. Definition

Regulatory Change Response Time (Deep Dive) (RCRT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Operations domain. Its calculation is Days from Regulation Publication to Impact Assessment, measured in days. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Days from Regulation Publication to Impact Assessment
```
