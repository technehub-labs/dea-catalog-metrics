## 2. Definition

Within Gig & Contingent Workforce, Gig Quality Score (GQS) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Average Quality Rating of Completed Tasks, expressed in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
GQS = Average Quality Rating of Completed Tasks
```
