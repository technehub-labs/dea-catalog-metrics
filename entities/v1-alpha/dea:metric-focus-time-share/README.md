## 2. Definition

Within Digital Workplace & Collaboration, Focus Time Share (FTS-DW) is used to gauge the utilization or availability of capacity. It is defined as Non-Meeting Work Time / Total Working Time; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of work time available for uninterrupted deep work. It matters because fragmentation is the hidden tax on knowledge worker productivity. Calendar-based measures can overstate focus by counting technically free but interrupt-prone blocks. Read with Meeting Load trends and self-reported productivity to confirm focus time is real.

## Formula

```
Non-Meeting Work Time / Total Working Time
```
