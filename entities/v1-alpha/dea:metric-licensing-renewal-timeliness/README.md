## 2. Definition

Licensing Renewal Timeliness (LRT) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of regulatory affairs & compliance operations reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Licenses Renewed before Expiry / Licenses Due - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Licenses Renewed before Expiry / Licenses Due
```
