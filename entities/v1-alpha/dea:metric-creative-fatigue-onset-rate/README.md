## 2. Definition

Within Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive), Creative Fatigue Onset Rate (CFOR) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Creatives Depreciating below Threshold before Refresh / Creatives - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Creatives Depreciating below Threshold before Refresh / Creatives
```
