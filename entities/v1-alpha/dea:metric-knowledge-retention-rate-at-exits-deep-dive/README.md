## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective, Knowledge Retention Rate at Exits (Deep Dive) (KRE-D) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Critical Knowledge Transferred on Exit / Exits with Critical Knowledge, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Critical Knowledge Transferred on Exit / Exits with Critical Knowledge
```
