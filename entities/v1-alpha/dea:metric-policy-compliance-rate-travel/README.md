## 2. Definition

A Corporate Travel Management indicator, Policy Compliance Rate (Travel) (PCR-T) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Bookings within Travel Policy / Total Bookings; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of bookings made within travel policy. It matters because policy is the mechanism that converts negotiated rates into real savings. Compliance can be lifted by loosening policy rather than changing behavior. Read with Out-of-Policy Spend Rate and Travel Cost per Trip to confirm compliance reflects genuine control.

## Formula

```
Bookings within Travel Policy / Total Bookings
```
