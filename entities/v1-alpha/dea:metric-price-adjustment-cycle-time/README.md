## 2. Definition

A Pricing & Revenue Management indicator, Price Adjustment Cycle Time (PACT) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Days from Price Decision to Effective in Market - reported in days. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.
