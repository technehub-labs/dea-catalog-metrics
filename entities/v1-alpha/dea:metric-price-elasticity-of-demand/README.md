## 2. Definition

A Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures indicator, Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Percent Change in Quantity / Percent Change in Price; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures how sensitively demand responds to price changes. Elasticity is the mathematical basis of pricing strategy: inelastic demand is pricing power. Elasticity measured historically embeds the range of prices actually tested. Read with pricing power index and price realization rate (Market).

## Formula

```
PED = Percent Change in Quantity / Percent Change in Price
```
