## 2. Definition

Component Defect Rate (SMT) (CDR-SMT) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking semiconductor & electronics. The formula is Defective Placements per Million Components Placed - reported in events per thousand or million (scaled). It is a standard measure in the Electronics & semiconductors sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Defective Placements per Million Components Placed
```
