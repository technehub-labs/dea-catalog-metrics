## 2. Definition

Privacy Program Maturity Score (PPMS) measures capability maturity or development in the Data Privacy & Protection domain. The formula is Assessed Maturity against Privacy Framework - reported in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the overall maturity of the privacy program across governance, process, and technology. Maturity trajectories predict regulatory resilience better than incident counts. Scores benchmarked internally without external reference drift upward. Read with privacy compliance audit findings and personal data inventory coverage.

## Formula

```
Assessed Maturity against Privacy Framework
```
