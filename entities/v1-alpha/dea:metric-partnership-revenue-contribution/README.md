## 2. Definition

Partnership Revenue Contribution (PRC-AP) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Alliances & Partnerships Management domain. It is defined as Partner-Attributed Revenue / Total Revenue; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue attributable to alliances and partnerships. It quantifies whether the partnership function is a growth engine or overhead. Attribution rules (last-touch vs. influenced) drive the number. Read with partner-initiated pipeline ratio and alliance margin contribution.

## Formula

```
Partner-Attributed Revenue / Total Revenue
```
