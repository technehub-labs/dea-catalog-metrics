## 2. Definition

Team Happiness Index (THI-AG) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of agile delivery & team effectiveness reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Composite Team Sentiment Score, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures delivery-team self-reported satisfaction and morale. Sustainable delivery requires sustainable teams; burnout is paid for in defects, attrition, and knowledge loss. Happiness without delivery discipline can indicate under-challenge; misery with strong delivery predicts a cliff. Read with psychological safety score and employee engagement score.
