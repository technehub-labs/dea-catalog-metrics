## 2. Definition

A Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety Standard Measures indicator, Average Length of Stay (Inpatient) (ALOSDD) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Inpatient Days / Discharges; the result is stated in days. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. In Healthcare, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures mean inpatient days per stay. LOS is the efficiency lever with direct cost and capacity consequences. LOS reduced by premature discharge converts into readmissions. Read with 30-day readmission rate (Deep Dive) and bed occupancy rate.

## Formula

```
Inpatient Days / Discharges
```
