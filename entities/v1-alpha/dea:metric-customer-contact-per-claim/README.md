## 2. Definition

A Claims Operations (Insurance) indicator, Customer Contact per Claim (CPCl) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Customer Contacts per Claim Lifetime - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector.

## Formula
