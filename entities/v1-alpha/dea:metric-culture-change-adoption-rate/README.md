## 2. Definition

Culture Change Adoption Rate (CCAR) captures transformation for organizations tracking culture & employee experience. It is computed as Behaviors Demonstrating Target Culture Observed / Behaviors Expected, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of target behaviors or practices from a culture program demonstrably adopted in daily work - observed behaviors, not survey agreement. Culture change notoriously fails between workshop and workflow, so adoption is the truthful lagging indicator. Guard against counted-but-performed behaviors (rituals done for the scorecard) and observational sampling that only visits compliant teams. Read with sustained adoption at 90 Days for durability and culture alignment index.

## Formula

```
Behaviors Demonstrating Target Culture Observed / Behaviors Expected
```
