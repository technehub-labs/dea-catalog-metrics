## 2. Definition

Franchisee Training Completion (Deep Dive) (FTCD) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking franchise operations (deep dive). The formula is Franchisees Completing Mandatory Training / Franchisees - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Franchisees Completing Mandatory Training / Franchisees
```
