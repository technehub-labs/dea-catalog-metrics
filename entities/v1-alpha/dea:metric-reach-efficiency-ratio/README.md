## 2. Definition

A Advertising & Media Buying indicator, Reach Efficiency Ratio (RER) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Unique Audience Reached / Gross Impressions, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
Unique Audience Reached / Gross Impressions
```
