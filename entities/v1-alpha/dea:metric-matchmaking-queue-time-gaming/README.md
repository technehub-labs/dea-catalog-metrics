## 2. Definition

A Gaming Development & Live Operations indicator, Matchmaking Queue Time (Gaming) (MQT) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Median Seconds to Match, expressed in seconds. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is concentrated in Gaming.
