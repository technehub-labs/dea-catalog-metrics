## 2. Definition

A Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures indicator, Substitution Rate (SUBR) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. The formula is Customers Switching to Alternatives / Customer Base - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how fast customers replace the product with alternatives (including non-obvious ones). Substitution is the disruptive threat that share metrics miss entirely. Self-reported switching intent understates realized substitution. Read with churn rate and competitive win-loss analysis coverage.

## Formula

```
Customers Switching to Alternatives / Customer Base
```
