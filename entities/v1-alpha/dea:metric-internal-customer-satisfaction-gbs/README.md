## 2. Definition

A Shared Services & Global Business Services indicator, Internal Customer Satisfaction (GBS) (ICSAT) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Satisfied Business Customer Responses / Responses; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures business satisfaction with shared services delivery. It matters because GBS legitimacy depends on internal customers choosing it over shadow alternatives. Satisfaction can drift high when escalation is politically costly. Read with Shadow Vendor Count and Service Level Attainment (GBS) to confirm satisfaction is genuine rather than resigned.

## Formula

```
Satisfied Business Customer Responses / Responses
```
