## 2. Definition

Within Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety Standard Measures, NTSV Cesarean Rate (NTSV) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its calculation is Cesarean Deliveries among Low-Risk First Births / Such Births, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Healthcare practice.

## 3. Context

Measures cesarean births among low-risk, first-time mothers. It is the signature measure of obstetric over-intervention and a public quality benchmark. Case-mix risk adjustment determines whether comparisons are fair. Read with vaginal birth after cesarean rate and labor and delivery unit outcomes.

## Formula

```
Cesarean Deliveries among Low-Risk First Births / Such Births
```
