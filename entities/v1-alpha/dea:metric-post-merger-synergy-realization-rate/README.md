## 2. Definition

Post-Merger Synergy Realization Rate (PSRR) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of mergers & acquisitions reporting. It is defined as Realized Synergies / Promised Synergies; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures captured synergies against the deal model, in money and timing. It is the report card of deal logic: most deals miss plan, and under-realization is the leading indicator of impairment. Insist on finance-owned baselines and synergy attribution rules set before integration starts, or realization becomes a negotiation between the deal sponsor and reality. Read with deal value creation ratio and headcount and cost run-rate evidence.

## Formula

```
Realized Synergies / Promised Synergies
```
