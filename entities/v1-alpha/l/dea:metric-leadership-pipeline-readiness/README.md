## 2. Definition

A Learning & Development indicator, Leadership Pipeline Readiness (LPR) quantifies capability maturity or development. The formula is Leadership Roles with Ready Internal Candidates / Leadership Roles - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Leadership pipeline readiness measures the bench strength for key roles - typically the ratio of ready-now successors per critical position, formalized in Ram Charan's leadership pipeline framework. It is the succession metric that boards increasingly audit directly, because the alternative is expensive external searches with high failure rates (external senior hires fail at roughly double internal-promotion rates in many studies). The measurement risk is calibration inflation: what one executive calls ready-now, another calls ready-in-two-years, so credible programs use assessment centers and cross-calibrated talent reviews rather than single-rater judgments. Read with internal-fill rate for leadership roles, diversity of the ready-now pool, and regretted leadership attrition, since a strong pipeline that leaks at promotion time still fails.

## Formula

```
LPR = Leadership Roles with Ready Internal Candidates / Leadership Roles
```
