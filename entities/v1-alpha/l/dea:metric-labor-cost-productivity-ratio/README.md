## 2. Definition

Labor Cost Productivity Ratio (LCPR) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Workforce Planning & Productivity domain. It is defined as Revenue or Output / Total Labor Cost; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures output value generated per unit of labor cost. It converts workforce decisions into return language. Ratios improved by suppressing wages invite attrition and quality costs. Read with labor cost percentage and human capital ROI.

## Formula

```
LCPR = Revenue or Output / Total Labor Cost
```
