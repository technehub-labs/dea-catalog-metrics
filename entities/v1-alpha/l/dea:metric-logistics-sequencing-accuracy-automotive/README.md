## 2. Definition

Logistics Sequencing Accuracy (Automotive) (LSA) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of automotive manufacturing (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Sequenced Deliveries Correct / Deliveries, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Automotive. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
LSA = Sequenced Deliveries Correct / Deliveries
```
