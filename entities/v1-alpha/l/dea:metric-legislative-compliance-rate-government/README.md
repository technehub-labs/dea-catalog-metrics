## 2. Definition

Within Government & Public Administration, Legislative Compliance Rate (Government) (LCR-G) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is computed as Obligations Met on Time / Obligations, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Public Sector.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to statutory duties - reporting deadlines, mandated service standards, legal response obligations. Beyond avoiding sanctions, it is a governance health signal: chronic noncompliance with unglamorous statutes predicts failure under stress. Be skeptical of compliance self-assessments without testing, and prioritize by consequence severity rather than count. Read with repeat regulatory findings rate and audit outcomes.

## Formula

```
LCR-G = Obligations Met on Time / Obligations
```
