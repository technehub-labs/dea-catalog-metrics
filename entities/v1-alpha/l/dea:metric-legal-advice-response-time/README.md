## 2. Definition

A Legal Operations indicator, Legal Advice Response Time (LART) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Hours or Days from Request to Substantive Legal Response - reported in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
LART = Hours or Days from Request to Substantive Legal Response
```
