## 2. Definition

Scorecard Debate Depth Score (SDDS) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking balanced scorecard implementation & review. It is defined as Rubric Score of Challenge Quality in BSC Reviews; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the quality of structured challenge that occurs in scorecard reviews. Debate quality is the best observable proxy for whether reviews change decisions. Conflict-avoidant review cultures score high on harmony and low on this metric. Read with decision quality audit score and post-diction accuracy rate.
