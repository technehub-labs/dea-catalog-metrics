## 2. Definition

Cost per Available Seat Kilometer (CASK) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of airline & transportation standard measures (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Operating Cost / Available Seat Kilometers, expressed in currency per unit. Its use is concentrated in Airlines & Aviation. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures operating cost per seat-kilometer offered. CASK is the standard unit cost comparator across fleets and carriers. CASK cut below maintenance safety margins is destruction deferred. Read with revenue per available seat kilometer and fuel burn per block hour (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Operating Cost / Available Seat Kilometers
```
