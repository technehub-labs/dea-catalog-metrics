## 2. Definition

Partnership Revenue Share (PRS) measures growth over time in the Corporate Venturing & Partnerships domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Partner-Attributed Revenue / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of total revenue generated through or attributable to partnerships and alliances. It signals whether ecosystem strategy is real or rhetorical, and diversifies growth beyond owned channels. Attribution rules are the battleground - last-touch crediting undercounts enabling partnerships, while generous influence models claim too much. Read with partnership profitability, not revenue alone, and strategic alliance health score for durability.

## Formula

```
Partner-Attributed Revenue / Total Revenue
```
