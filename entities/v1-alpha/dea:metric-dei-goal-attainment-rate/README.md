## 2. Definition

DEI Goal Attainment Rate (DEIGAR) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of diversity, equity & inclusion (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Published DEI Goals Achieved / Goals; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures achievement of the organization's stated DEI goals. Attainment converts DEI from aspiration to accountable program. Goals defined as activity counts (trainings held) are attainable and meaningless. Read with DEI representation trend (Deep Dive) and promotion equity ratio.

## Formula

```
DEIGAR = Published DEI Goals Achieved / Goals
```
