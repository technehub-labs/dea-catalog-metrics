## 2. Definition

Assisted Digital Drop-off Rate (ADDR) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Users Abandoning Digital Journey to Assisted Channel / Digital Starts - reported in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Public sector sector.

## Formula

```
Users Abandoning Digital Journey to Assisted Channel / Digital Starts
```
