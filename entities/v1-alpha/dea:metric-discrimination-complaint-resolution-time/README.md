## 2. Definition

Discrimination Complaint Resolution Time (DCRT) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of diversity, equity & inclusion reporting. The formula is Days from Complaint Filed to Case Closure - reported in days. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures time to investigate and resolve discrimination complaints. It matters because slow handling compounds legal risk and signals institutional indifference. Resolution can be declared at investigation close while remedies go unimplemented. Read with Complaint Recurrence and DEI climate survey results to confirm resolution changes outcomes.

Days from Complaint Filed to Case Closure
