## 2. Definition

A Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Campaign Pacing Compliance (CPC-MK) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Delivery within Pacing Tolerance of Plan / Campaigns - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Delivery within Pacing Tolerance of Plan / Campaigns
```
