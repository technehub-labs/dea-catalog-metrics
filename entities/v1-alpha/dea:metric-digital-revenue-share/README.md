## 2. Definition

A Digital Transformation indicator, Digital Revenue Share (DRS) quantifies growth over time. The formula is Digitally Originated Revenue / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of total revenue from digitally originated, delivered, or priced products and channels. It is the headline honesty test of digital strategy: many transformations spend billions to move a revenue mix that barely shifts. The definitional risk is the word digital itself - slapping an app on a physical service or counting online billing as digital revenue can inflate the metric without changing the business. Read with digital initiative value realization and customer digital preference share.

## Formula

```
Digitally Originated Revenue / Total Revenue
```
