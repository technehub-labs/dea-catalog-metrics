## 2. Definition

Problem Record Creation Rate from Incidents (PRCRI) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of it incident & major problem management reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Problems Opened from Incident Trends / Major or Repeat Incidents; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how often recurring or major incidents spawn problem investigations. The problem record is the bridge from firefighting to root-cause elimination. Creating records without resourcing them builds a backlog of ignored analysis. Read with recurring incident ratio and root cause analysis completion rate.

## Formula

```
Problems Opened from Incident Trends / Major or Repeat Incidents
```
