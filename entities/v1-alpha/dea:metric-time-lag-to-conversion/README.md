## 2. Definition

A Marketing Attribution & Analytics indicator, Time Lag to Conversion (TLTC) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Days from First Attribution Touch to Conversion, expressed in days. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.
