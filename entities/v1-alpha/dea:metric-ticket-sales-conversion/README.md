## 2. Definition

Ticket Sales Conversion (TSC) captures growth over time for organizations tracking sports & events. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Tickets Sold / Tickets Offered, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Sports & events.

## 3. Context

Ticket sales conversion measures the share of interested prospects (site visitors, price-checkers, single-game buyers) who purchase, and it is the sales-effectiveness metric of sports and live-entertainment organizations that now think like e-commerce businesses - dynamic pricing, demand-based inventory allocation, and funnel optimization are standard practice. Conversion in tickets has distinctive drivers: opponent quality and event timing are often non-negotiable product features while price presentation, bundle design, and seat-reveal UX are controllable. The secondary-market interaction is structural: high resale values suppress primary conversion when fans wait for price drops, while low resale values signal weak demand. Read with conversion by channel (primary versus resale-influenced), price-sensitivity curves by seat category, renewal intent capture at events, and funnel abandonment analysis.

## Formula

```
Tickets Sold / Tickets Offered
```
