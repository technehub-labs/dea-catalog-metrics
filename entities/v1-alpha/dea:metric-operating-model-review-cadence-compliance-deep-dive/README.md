## 2. Definition

Operating Model Review Cadence Compliance (Deep Dive) (OMRCC) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking operating model & process health deep dive. Its calculation is Scheduled Operating Model Reviews Held / Scheduled, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether scheduled operating model reviews actually occur with the right participants and inputs. Cadence is what keeps the operating model a managed asset rather than a historical artifact. Attendance without decision authority is ceremony. Read with operating model review cadence compliance and decision quality audit score.

## Formula

```
Scheduled Operating Model Reviews Held / Scheduled
```
