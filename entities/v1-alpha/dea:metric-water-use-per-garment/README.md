## 2. Definition

Water Use per Garment (WUG) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Textile & Apparel (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Litres of Water Used / Garment, measured in kilolitres (kl). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Textiles & Apparel practice.

## 3. Context

Measures water consumed per unit produced, including wet processing. Textile wet processing is among the most water-intensive industrial processes, and per-garment intensity is the metric that buyer sustainability programs and basin-level water stress increasingly target. Intensity improved by outsourcing wet processing shifts the footprint, not the impact, so boundary honesty is essential. Read with effluent compliance and water-stressed sourcing location exposure.

## Formula

```
Litres of Water Used / Garment
```
