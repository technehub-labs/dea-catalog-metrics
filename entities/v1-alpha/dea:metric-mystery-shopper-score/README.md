## 2. Definition

Mystery Shopper Score (MSS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking customer. It is defined as Average Audit Score across Mystery Shopper Evaluations; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures standardized evaluations of service quality by trained anonymous assessors. It matters because it audits the actual customer experience against standards, catching gaps self-reported metrics miss. Scores can be gamed by staff recognizing shoppers or locations coach-ing only on scored behaviors during audit windows. Read with Customer Satisfaction Score and Schedule Adherence to check frontline consistency between audits.

## Formula

```
Average Audit Score across Mystery Shopper Evaluations
```
