## 2. Definition

Within Field Maintenance & Technical Field Service (Deep Dive), Windshield Time Ratio (WSTR-F) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Travel Hours / Total Field Hours - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Travel Hours / Total Field Hours
```
