## 2. Definition

Collection Route Efficiency (CRE) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Waste & Environmental Services domain. It is computed as Households Served per Route Hour, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Its use is concentrated in Environmental services. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula
