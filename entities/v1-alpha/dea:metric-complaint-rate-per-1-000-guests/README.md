## 2. Definition

A Travel & Tourism indicator, Complaint Rate per 1,000 Guests (CRPG) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Complaints / Guests x 1,000; the result is stated in events per thousand or million (scaled). In Travel & tourism, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures complaint frequency normalized by guest volume in travel and hospitality. It matters because complaints are leading indicators of review damage and repeat-business loss. Making complaint channels harder suppresses the numerator. Read with Guest Satisfaction and Online Review Rating trends to confirm falling complaints mean better service.

## Formula

```
CRPG = Complaints / Guests x 1,000
```
