## 2. Definition

Change Readiness Score (CRS-C) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Change Management domain. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Weighted Index of Awareness, Sponsorship, and Capacity Items; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

A pre-change diagnostic of the organization's capacity, willingness, and context for a specific change - measuring history, sponsorship, skills, and competing pressures. It matters because interventions designed for readiness levels prevent the standard failure mode: launching into a fatigued, low-trust environment and blaming the audience. Be wary of single-point scores; readiness varies by site, function, and level, so segment. Read with change fatigue attrition contribution and sponsor visibility score.

## Formula

Weighted Index of Awareness, Sponsorship, and Capacity Items
