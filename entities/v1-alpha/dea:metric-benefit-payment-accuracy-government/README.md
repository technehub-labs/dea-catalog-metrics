## 2. Definition

Benefit Payment Accuracy (Government) (BPA) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of government & public administration reporting. It is defined as Correct Benefit Payments / Payments; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Public Sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of benefit payments that are correct in eligibility and amount. Errors in both directions - overpayments (fiscal leakage, recovery costs) and underpayments (hardship, appeals, reputational damage) - carry costs, so accuracy must be measured symmetrically. Right-first-time is the metric that matters, not error detection speed. Read with appeals and complaint rates and recovery write-off rates.

## Formula

```
Correct Benefit Payments / Payments
```
