## 2. Definition

Author Advance Earnout Rate (AAER-P) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of publishing & news media reporting. Its calculation is Titles Earning out Advances / Titles with Advances, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of author advances earned back through sales. It is the unit-economics honesty metric of trade publishing: the advance system prices risk optimistically, and earnout rates reveal how well editorial judgment predicts the market. Portfolio-level reads matter more than per-title, since publishing economics run on bestsellers subsidizing misses. Read with title profitability distribution and format-mix (audiobook, ebook) contribution.

## Formula

```
AAER-P = Titles Earning out Advances / Titles with Advances
```
