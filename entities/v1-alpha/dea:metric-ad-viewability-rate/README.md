## 2. Definition

Ad Viewability Rate (AVR-MK) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking marketing & advertising standard measures (deep dive). It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It is defined as Viewable Impressions / Measurable Impressions; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
AVR-MK = Viewable Impressions / Measurable Impressions
```
