## 2. Definition

Material Utilization Rate (Packaging) (MUR-P) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of packaging & plastics reporting. Its calculation is Product in Packaging / Material Consumed, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice.

## Formula

```
MUR-P = Product in Packaging / Material Consumed
```
