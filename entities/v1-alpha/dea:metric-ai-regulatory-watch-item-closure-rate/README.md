## 2. Definition

AI Regulatory Watch Item Closure Rate (AIRWIC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of ai governance & model risk management reporting. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Regulatory Watch Actions Closed by Date / Actions, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Regulatory Watch Actions Closed by Date / Actions
```
