## 2. Definition

Within Culture & Employee Experience, Onboarding Satisfaction Rate (OSR-E) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. The formula is Satisfied New Hire Survey Responses / Responses - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures new employee satisfaction with onboarding experience. It matters because onboarding quality predicts early retention and time to productivity. Surveys taken at day one measure excitement, not onboarding. Read with New Hire 90-Day Retention and Time to Productivity to confirm satisfaction reflects genuine integration.

## Formula

```
Satisfied New Hire Survey Responses / Responses
```
