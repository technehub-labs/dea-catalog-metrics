## 2. Definition

A Asset & Wealth Management (Deep Dive) indicator, Alpha Generation Consistency (AGC-W) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. It is computed as Periods with Positive Alpha / Periods, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the persistence of risk-adjusted excess returns across periods. Consistency is what separates skill from luck in active management, and it is the metric institutional consultants screen on, since a manager with volatile alpha adds little even if average alpha is positive. Attribution consistency by source (security selection versus allocation) is the deeper question. Read with information ratio (Deep Dive) and out-of-benchmark factor exposure checks.

## Formula

```
Periods with Positive Alpha / Periods
```
