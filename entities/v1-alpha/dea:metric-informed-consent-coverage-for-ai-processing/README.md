## 2. Definition

Informed Consent Coverage for AI Processing (ICCAP) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the AI Ethics, Fairness & Responsible AI domain. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Processing Purposes with Valid Consent / Purposes, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether AI processing of personal data rests on informed, purpose-matched consent where required. Beyond legal compliance, consent integrity is the trust foundation of AI-powered personalization: consent obtained through bundled or obscured terms is legal-risk and reputation-risk combined. Coverage must be measured at the processing-purpose level, since blanket consent rarely covers specific AI uses. Read with privacy consent compliance rate patterns and consent-withdraw handling.

## Formula

```
Processing Purposes with Valid Consent / Purposes
```
