## 2. Definition

Order Routing Quality Score (ORQS-T) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of investment research & trading operations reporting. Its calculation is Composite Routing Cost and Fill Score, measured in score (points). It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

Composite Routing Cost and Fill Score
