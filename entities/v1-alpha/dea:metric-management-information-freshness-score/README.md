## 2. Definition

Within Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring, Management Information Freshness Score (MIFS-GOV) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Reports Presented with Data under Refresh SLA / Reports, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how current the information reaching decision-makers is relative to the pace of the business. Stale information makes confident decisions about the past. Freshness without accuracy is noise delivered promptly. Read with single source of truth metric adoption and budget forecast accuracy - financial planning.

## Formula

```
Reports Presented with Data under Refresh SLA / Reports
```
