## 2. Definition

Data Uniqueness Rate (DUR-DQ) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of data quality management reporting. It is computed as Unique Identifiers / Identifiers Sampled, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Unique Identifiers / Identifiers Sampled
```
