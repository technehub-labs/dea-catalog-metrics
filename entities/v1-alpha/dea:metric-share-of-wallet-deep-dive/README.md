## 2. Definition

Share of Wallet (Deep Dive) (SWDD) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. It is computed as Customer Spend with Entity / Total Category Spend, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the company's share of each customer's total category spend. Wallet share compounds: expanding existing relationships is far cheaper than acquiring. Category definitions supplied by customers are inconsistent. Read with customer share of wallet (Deep Dive) and net revenue retention.

## Formula

```
SWDD = Customer Spend with Entity / Total Category Spend
```
