## 2. Definition

New Product Revenue Share (Deep Dive) (NPRS-D) captures growth over time for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - internal process perspective. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Revenue from Products under Three Years / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue from products launched within a defined recent window. It is the scoreboard of whether the innovation engine actually feeds the business. Definitions of 'new' (repaint versus genuine) determine whether the number is meaningful. Read with innovation pipeline conversion rate and revenue growth rate.

## Formula

```
Revenue from Products under Three Years / Total Revenue
```
