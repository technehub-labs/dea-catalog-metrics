## 2. Definition

A Investment Management - Portfolio Operations indicator, ESG Integration Coverage in Portfolio (Deep Dive) (ESGIC-D) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Holdings ESG-Assessed / Total Holdings - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Financial Services sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of portfolio assets under systematic ESG assessment and integration. Integration is becoming a mandate prerequisite and regulatory expectation across major markets. Coverage with exclusion-only screening overstates integration depth. Read with ESG incident exposure and stewardship activity completion rate.

## Formula

```
Holdings ESG-Assessed / Total Holdings
```
