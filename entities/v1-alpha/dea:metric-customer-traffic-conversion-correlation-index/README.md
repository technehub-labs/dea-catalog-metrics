## 2. Definition

Customer Traffic Conversion Correlation Index (CTCCI) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking retail standard measures. The formula is Strength of Traffic-to-Sales Relationship across Stores - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is a standard measure in the Retail & E-commerce sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
