## 2. Definition

Scrap Rate (Plastics) (SRP) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Packaging & Plastics domain. The formula is Scrap Material / Total Material - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Manufacturing sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Scrap Material / Total Material
```
