## 2. Definition

Business Glossary Term Coverage (BGTC) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Data Governance & Stewardship domain. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Terms Defined and Approved / Terms in Use, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Terms Defined and Approved / Terms in Use
```
