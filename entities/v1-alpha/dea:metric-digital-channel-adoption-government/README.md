## 2. Definition

Digital Channel Adoption (Government) (DCAD) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Government & Public Administration domain. The formula is Digital Service Transactions / Total Transactions - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Public Sector sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of eligible citizen transactions completed digitally. It matters because digital is the main lever on cost per transaction and on inclusion when paired with assisted-digital support. The equity trap is real: adoption achieved by closing counters pushes vulnerable citizens out of services entirely, so track assisted-channel queues alongside. Read with service delivery completion rate (Government) and vulnerable population uptake.

## Formula

```
Digital Service Transactions / Total Transactions
```
