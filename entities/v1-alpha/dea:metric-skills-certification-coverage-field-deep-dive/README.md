## 2. Definition

A Field Maintenance & Technical Field Service (Deep Dive) indicator, Skills Certification Coverage - Field (Deep Dive) (SCCF-D) quantifies capability maturity or development. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Technicians Certified for Assigned Work / Technicians; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of field technicians holding current certifications required for the asset mix they service. Certification gaps force second visits, over-rely on specialists, and create liability. Coverage on paper without practical competency assessment overstates readiness. Read with first-time fix rate - field and training investment per employee.

## Formula

```
SCCF-D = Technicians Certified for Assigned Work / Technicians
```
