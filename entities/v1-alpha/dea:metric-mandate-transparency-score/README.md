## 2. Definition

Within PMO & Portfolio Governance, Mandate Transparency Score (MTS) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Stakeholder Clarity Rating of Portfolio Priorities, expressed in score (points). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how clearly the PMO's own authority and scope are defined and understood. Unclear mandates produce either toothless governance or resented micromanagement. Scores surveyed from the PMO's own stakeholders drift toward politeness. Read with decision rights clarity score and stage gate bypass rate.

## Formula

```
Stakeholder Clarity Rating of Portfolio Priorities
```
