## 2. Definition

Payback on Marketing Investment (Deep Dive) (PROMID) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking unit economics & growth (deep dive). It is computed as Attributed Gross Profit / Marketing Spend, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Its use is concentrated in Software. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Attributed Gross Profit / Marketing Spend
```
