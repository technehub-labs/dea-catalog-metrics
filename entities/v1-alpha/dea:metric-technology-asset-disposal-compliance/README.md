## 2. Definition

A IT Financial Management & Technology Budgeting indicator, Technology Asset Disposal Compliance (TADC-ITFM) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Assets Disposed per Policy / Disposals, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Assets Disposed per Policy / Disposals
```
