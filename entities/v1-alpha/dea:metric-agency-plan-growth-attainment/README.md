## 2. Definition

Agency Plan Growth Attainment (APGA) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of insurance distribution & agency management reporting. It is defined as Actual Agency Growth / Planned Growth; the result is stated in percent (%). In Insurance, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures achievement of agency production plans - new business, lines of coverage, target segments. It converts distribution strategy into an accountability mechanism, and consistent under-attainment reveals either unrealistic planning or weak enablement. Attainment earned through price concessions or line-slipping damages the book long after the plan celebrates. Read with underwriting loss ratio by agency cohort and persistency.

## Formula

```
Actual Agency Growth / Planned Growth
```
