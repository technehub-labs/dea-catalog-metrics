## 2. Definition

A Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning indicator, Blind Spot Review Frequency (BSRF) quantifies governance. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Formal Unknown-Risk Reviews per Year - reported in count (items per period). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how often the risk process deliberately searches for what it is not seeing. Blind-spot reviews are the only countermeasure to systematic blind spots. Reviews that only re-examine known risks are inventory checks. Read with post-diction accuracy rate and emerging risk board reporting depth score.

## Formula

```
BSRF = Formal Unknown-Risk Reviews per Year
```
