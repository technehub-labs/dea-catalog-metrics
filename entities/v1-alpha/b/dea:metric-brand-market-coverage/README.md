## 2. Definition

Brand Market Coverage (BMC) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of market structure & economics standard measures reporting. The formula is Segments Served / Addressable Segments - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely the brand's presence covers the strategic market definition. Coverage is where brand strategy meets distribution reality. Coverage counted as distribution presence without shelf performance is nominal. Read with channel coverage of target markets and brand preference score.

## Formula

```
BMC = Segments Served / Addressable Segments
```
