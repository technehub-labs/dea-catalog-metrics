## 2. Definition

A Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) indicator, Belonging Index (BI-DEI) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its calculation is Survey Composite of Belonging and Psychological Safety, measured in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures employees' felt sense of being valued and able to be themselves at work. Belonging predicts retention and contribution better than representation counts. Survey instruments that require self-identification depress honesty. Read with engagement and inclusion index and inclusive leadership score.

## Formula

```
BI-DEI = Survey Composite of Belonging and Psychological Safety
```
