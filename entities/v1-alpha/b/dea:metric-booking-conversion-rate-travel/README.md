## 2. Definition

Booking Conversion Rate (Travel) (BCR-T) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking travel & tourism. It is defined as Completed Bookings / Search Sessions; the result is stated in percent (%). In Travel & tourism, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of searches or quotes that become bookings. It matters because it isolates how well a travel offer converts demand once interest exists. Conversion can be lifted with teaser prices that later inflate with fees, damaging trust. Read with Cancellation Rate (Travel) and Booking Abandonment to confirm conversion is honest.

## Formula

```
BCR-T = Completed Bookings / Search Sessions
```
