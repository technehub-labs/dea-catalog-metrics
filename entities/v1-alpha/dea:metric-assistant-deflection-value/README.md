## 2. Definition

Assistant Deflection Value (ADV-LLM) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Generative AI & LLM Operations domain. It is defined as Human Contact Cost Avoided by AI Resolution; the result is stated in currency value. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the economic value of work deflected from human channels by AI assistance. It converts containment into the currency of business cases: deflected contacts times fully loaded cost per contact, adjusted for quality (repeat contacts, escalations) that erode the value. Deflection that pushes customers to abandon rather than resolve is negative value counted as savings. Read with LLM containment rate (Deep Dive) and satisfaction-adjusted cost per resolution.

## Formula

```
Human Contact Cost Avoided by AI Resolution
```
