## 2. Definition

Solvent Recovery Rate (SVR) addresses environmental and social sustainability performance and is a fixture of chemicals reporting. It is defined as Solvent Recovered and Reused / Solvent Consumed; the result is stated in percent (%). In Chemicals, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of solvents recovered and reused in chemical processes. It matters because recovery cuts raw material cost, waste, and hazardous handling risk. Recovery can be inflated by counting once-recovered solvent that is degraded and unusable. Read with Waste Intensity and input purity data to confirm recovery reflects usable quality.

## Formula

```
Solvent Recovered and Reused / Solvent Consumed
```
