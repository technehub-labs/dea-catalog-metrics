## 2. Definition

AI Use Case Approval Cycle Time (AIAC) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the AI Governance & Model Risk Management domain. Its calculation is Days from Proposal to Governance Decision, measured in days. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

Days from Proposal to Governance Decision
