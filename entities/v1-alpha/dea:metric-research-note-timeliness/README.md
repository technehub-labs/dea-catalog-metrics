## 2. Definition

Within Investment Research & Trading Operations, Research Note Timeliness (RNT-RS) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Notes Published within Event SLA / Events, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula
