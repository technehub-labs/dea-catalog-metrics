## 2. Definition

Offboarding Completeness Rate (OCR-TP) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking third-party risk management. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Offboardings with Access, Data, Asset Recovery Completed / Offboardings - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Offboardings with Access, Data, Asset Recovery Completed / Offboardings
```
