## 2. Definition

Beverage Cost Percentage (BCP) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of hospitality & food service reporting. It is defined as Beverage Cost / Beverage Revenue; the result is stated in percent (%). In Hospitality, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures beverage cost as a share of beverage sales. It matters because beverages carry the highest margins in hospitality and cover a large share of profit. Gaming risk includes diluting pours or counting complimentary drinks outside the metric. Read with Average Check and inventory variance reports to confirm beverage margins hold at standard pours.

## Formula

```
BCP = Beverage Cost / Beverage Revenue
```
