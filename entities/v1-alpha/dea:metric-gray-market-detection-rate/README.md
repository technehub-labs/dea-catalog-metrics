## 2. Definition

Within Channel & Distribution Management, Gray Market Detection Rate (GMDR) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Unauthorized Channel Incidents Confirmed / Investigated, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how much unauthorized or diverted product trade is detected and acted on. Gray market leakage erodes pricing integrity and brand control. Detection concentrated in obvious channels misses the sophisticated ones. Read with brand protection enforcement actions and channel program compliance rate.

## Formula

```
Unauthorized Channel Incidents Confirmed / Investigated
```
