## 2. Definition

A Product Management indicator, Roadmap Delivery Rate (RDR) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Committed Roadmap Items Delivered / Items Committed; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of committed roadmap items delivered on schedule. It matters because roadmap credibility underpins sales commitments and stakeholder trust. Teams can hit it by quietly dropping or rescoping items after commitment. Read with Feature Adoption Rate and commitments-versus-delivered change logs to detect scope laundering.

## Formula

```
Committed Roadmap Items Delivered / Items Committed
```
