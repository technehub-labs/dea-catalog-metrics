## 2. Definition

Partner Data Sharing Compliance (PDSC-AP) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of alliances & partnerships management reporting. Its calculation is Data Exchanges under Agreed Terms / Exchanges, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether data exchanged with partners follows the agreed controls and restrictions. Partner data flows extend your privacy and security perimeter beyond your control. Compliance assumed from contract clauses rather than verified controls is hope. Read with vendor privacy assurance coverage and data loss prevention incident rate.

## Formula

```
Data Exchanges under Agreed Terms / Exchanges
```
