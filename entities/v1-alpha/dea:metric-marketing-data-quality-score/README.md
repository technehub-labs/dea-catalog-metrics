## 2. Definition

A Marketing Attribution & Analytics indicator, Marketing Data Quality Score (MDQS) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Weighted Index of Tracking Completeness and Consistency, measured in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Weighted Index of Tracking Completeness and Consistency
```
