## 2. Definition

Active Share (Deep Dive) (ACTSH) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Investment Performance Measurement domain. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Portfolio Holdings Differing from Benchmark / Holdings, expressed in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Its use is concentrated in Investment management.

## 3. Context

Measures the percentage of portfolio holdings that deviate from the benchmark. It is the tool for identifying closet indexers charging active fees. High active share without skill is expensive differentiation. Read with active share and tracking error.

## Formula

```
Portfolio Holdings Differing from Benchmark / Holdings
```
