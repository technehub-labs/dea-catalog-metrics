## 2. Definition

Supplier Defect Cost Recovery Rate (SDCRR) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of strategic sourcing & supplier performance (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Quality Costs Recovered from Suppliers / Recoverable Costs - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Quality Costs Recovered from Suppliers / Recoverable Costs
```
