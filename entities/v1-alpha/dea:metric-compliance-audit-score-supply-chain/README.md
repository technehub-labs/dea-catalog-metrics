## 2. Definition

Compliance Audit Score (Supply Chain) (CASC) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking textile & apparel. Its calculation is Weighted Score of Social and Environmental Audits, measured in score (points). It originates from and is most used in Textile & apparel practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Supply chain compliance audit scores measure supplier adherence to labor, safety, and environmental standards - the social compliance infrastructure the apparel industry built after Rana Plaza forced traceable accountability onto buyer brands. The measurement problem is structural: announced audits measure audit-day performance, and the industry has learned that unauthorized subcontracting is where the real violations hide, so audit scores flatter while actual conditions diverge. The remediation view is more informative than the score: open corrective action plan items and their closure speed show whether compliance is improving or theater. Read with CAP closure rates and aging, unannounced audit share, subcontracting transparency, and worker-voice grievance data which bypasses audit filtration entirely.
