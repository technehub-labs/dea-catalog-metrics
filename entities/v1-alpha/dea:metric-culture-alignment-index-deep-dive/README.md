## 2. Definition

Culture Alignment Index (Deep Dive) (CAI-EE) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of employee engagement & culture (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Survey Composite of Stated vs Lived Values; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how consistently experienced culture matches the declared culture across units. Misalignment between espoused and lived culture drives cynicism and disengagement. Index scores from HR-owned surveys carry politeness bias. Read with employee engagement score (Deep Dive) and values-based behavior assessment.

## Formula

```
Survey Composite of Stated vs Lived Values
```
