## 2. Definition

Defect Rate per Hundred Garments (DRHG) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking textile & apparel. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Defects Found / Garments Inspected x 100 - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Textile & apparel sector.

## Formula

```
Defects Found / Garments Inspected x 100
```
