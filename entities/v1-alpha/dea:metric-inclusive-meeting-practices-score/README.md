## 2. Definition

Inclusive Meeting Practices Score (IMPS) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Observed Rubric Score of Meeting Inclusivity, measured in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
