## 2. Definition

Within Program & Portfolio Management, Initiative Kill Decision Timeliness (IKDT) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Failing Initiatives Stopped at Defined Checkpoints / Failing - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly underperforming initiatives are stopped once evidence of failure emerges. Killing fast is a capability: sunk-cost momentum keeps zombies alive for quarters. Timeliness requires clear escalation triggers, not just governance forums. Read with portfolio rebalancing frequency and stage gate bypass rate.

## Formula

```
Failing Initiatives Stopped at Defined Checkpoints / Failing
```
