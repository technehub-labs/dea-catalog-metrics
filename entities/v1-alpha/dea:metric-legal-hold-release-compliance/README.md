## 2. Definition

Legal Hold Release Compliance (LHRC) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking records & information management. It is computed as Holds Released and Data Reverted to Schedule / Released Holds, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Holds Released and Data Reverted to Schedule / Released Holds
```
