## 2. Definition

Public Procurement Award Cycle Time (PPIACT) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking public sector & citizen service standard measures (deep dive). Its calculation is Days from Solicitation to Contract Award, measured in days. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Public sector practice.

## Formula

Days from Solicitation to Contract Award
