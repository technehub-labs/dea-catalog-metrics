## 2. Definition

System-wide Same-Store Sales Growth (Deep Dive) (SSSG-D) measures growth over time in the Franchise Operations (Deep Dive) domain. The formula is Comparable Sales Growth across System - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures like-for-like sales growth across franchise and corporate units. It is the demand truth of the brand, stripping expansion from performance, and it is the metric on which franchise valuations and royalty growth ride. System-wide blending can hide corporate-versus-franchise divergence, so report both cuts. Read with franchise unit economics consistency and traffic-versus-ticket decomposition.
