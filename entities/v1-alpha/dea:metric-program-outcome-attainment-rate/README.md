## 2. Definition

Within Government & Public Administration, Program Outcome Attainment Rate (POAR) is used to gauge how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Programs Meeting Outcome Targets / Programs - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Public Sector sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of government programs achieving stated outcomes, not merely delivering outputs. It is the antidote to activity reporting: services delivered, staff deployed, funds disbursed are all inputs. Outcome attribution in complex social programs is genuinely hard, so require defined measurement windows and comparison baselines at design stage. Read with program evaluations and benefit payment accuracy (Government).

## Formula

```
POAR = Programs Meeting Outcome Targets / Programs
```
