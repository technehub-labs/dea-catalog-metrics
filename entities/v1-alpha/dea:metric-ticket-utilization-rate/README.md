## 2. Definition

Ticket Utilization Rate (TUR-A) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of arts & culture reporting. It is computed as Tickets Used / Tickets Sold or Issued, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Arts & culture. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of sold or issued tickets actually used for arts and culture events. It matters because paid-but-unused tickets hide true audience reach and demand signals. Comp tickets can be classified to flatter utilization statistics. Read with Attendance figures and Subscription Renewal Rate to confirm engagement is real.

## Formula

```
Tickets Used / Tickets Sold or Issued
```
