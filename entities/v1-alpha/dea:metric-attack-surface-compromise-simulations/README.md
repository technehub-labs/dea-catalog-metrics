## 2. Definition

Attack Surface Compromise Simulations (ASCS) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of threat intelligence & red team reporting. Its calculation is Compromise Paths Demonstrated per Exercise, measured in count (items per period). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
