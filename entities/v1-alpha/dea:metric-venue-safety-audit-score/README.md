## 2. Definition

A Entertainment Venues & Attractions indicator, Venue Safety Audit Score (VSAS-V) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Composite Venue Safety Audit Score; the result is stated in score (points). In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures performance against venue safety audit protocols - crowd management, emergency egress, ride and stage safety, vendor compliance. Venue safety failures are catastrophic-reputation events, so audit scores are the preventive proxy before incidents test reality. The honest read weights findings by severity and checks closure aging, since a high score with open critical items is a comfort blanket. Read with guest incident rate (Venues) and drill exercise performance.
