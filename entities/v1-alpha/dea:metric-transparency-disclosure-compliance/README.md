## 2. Definition

Transparency Disclosure Compliance (TDC-AI) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as User-Facing AI Disclosures per Policy / Touchpoints, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to disclosure requirements that users interact with AI or that decisions are automated. Transparency is both an emerging regulatory mandate and a trust practice, and disclosure compliance measured against the strictest applicable regime is the safe posture. Disclosure quality matters - hidden-in-terms disclosures comply technically while failing practically. Read with user comprehension testing and complaint themes about undisclosed automation.

## Formula

```
User-Facing AI Disclosures per Policy / Touchpoints
```
