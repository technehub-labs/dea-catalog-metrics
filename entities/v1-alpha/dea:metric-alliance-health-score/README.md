## 2. Definition

A Alliances & Partnerships Management indicator, Alliance Health Score (AHS-AP) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Composite Relationship Survey across Both Parties - reported in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the composite condition of a strategic alliance across relationship, execution, and economics. Health scores catch decaying alliances before the renewal surprises you. Scores from the alliance managers on both sides drift polite. Read with strategic alliance governance cadence compliance and partner retention rate.

## Formula

```
Composite Relationship Survey across Both Parties
```
