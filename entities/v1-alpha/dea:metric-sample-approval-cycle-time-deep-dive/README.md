## 2. Definition

A Textile & Apparel (Deep Dive) indicator, Sample Approval Cycle Time (Deep Dive) (SACT-D) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Days from Sample Submission to Approval - reported in days. It is a standard measure in the Textiles & Apparel sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Days from Sample Submission to Approval
```
