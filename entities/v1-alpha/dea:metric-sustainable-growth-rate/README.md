## 2. Definition

Sustainable Growth Rate (SGR-VCF) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of valuation & corporate finance standard measures reporting. It is computed as ROE x Retention Ratio, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the growth rate fundable from retained earnings without new leverage or equity. It is the arithmetic boundary between self-funded strategy and financed strategy. Growth above SGR requires capital raises or debt, each with costs. Read with retention ratio (Earnings) and ROIC-WACC spread.

## Formula

```
ROE x Retention Ratio
```
