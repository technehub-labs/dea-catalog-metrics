## 2. Definition

Workforce Diversity Representation Index (WDRI) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of diversity, equity & inclusion (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Composite Representation across Levels vs Labor Market; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the composition of the workforce across demographic dimensions. Representation is the baseline measure of the DEI system. Aggregate representation can mask steep imbalance by level or function. Read with DEI representation trend (Deep Dive) and leadership diversity ratio.

## Formula

```
Composite Representation across Levels vs Labor Market
```
