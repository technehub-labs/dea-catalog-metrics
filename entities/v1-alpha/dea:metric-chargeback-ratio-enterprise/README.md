## 2. Definition

Chargeback Ratio (Enterprise) (CBR-EF) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Enterprise Fraud & Financial Crime Management domain. It is computed as Chargebacks / Total Card Transactions, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Chargebacks / Total Card Transactions
```
