## 2. Definition

Crisis Simulation Participation Rate (CSPR-BC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of business continuity & disaster recovery reporting. The formula is Teams Participating in Annual Simulation / Teams in Scope - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Teams Participating in Annual Simulation / Teams in Scope
```
