## 2. Definition

Whiplash / Soft Tissue Claim Share (WSTS) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of insurance claims deep dive reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Soft Tissue Claims / Bodily Injury Claims - reported in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector.

## Formula

```
Soft Tissue Claims / Bodily Injury Claims
```
