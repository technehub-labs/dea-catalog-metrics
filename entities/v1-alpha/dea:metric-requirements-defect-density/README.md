## 2. Definition

Requirements Defect Density (RDD) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Requirements Engineering & Product Discovery domain. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Requirement Defects per 100 Requirements, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Requirement Defects per 100 Requirements
```
