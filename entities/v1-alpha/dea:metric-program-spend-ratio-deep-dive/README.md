## 2. Definition

Program Spend Ratio (Deep Dive) (PSR-D) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking nonprofit & donor operations (deep dive). It is computed as Program Expenses / Total Expenses, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Nonprofit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of spending directed to programs versus administration and fundraising. It is the headline accountability metric donors and rating platforms use, and it is genuinely flawed as a sole measure: program quality requires administrative investment, and ratio-starved organizations can be ineffective at low overhead. Read alongside outcome metrics to keep the ratio honest in both directions. Read with program outcome measurement coverage and administrative cost ratio (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Program Expenses / Total Expenses
```
