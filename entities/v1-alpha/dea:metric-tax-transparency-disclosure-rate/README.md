## 2. Definition

Tax Transparency Disclosure Rate (TTDR) measures governance in the Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting domain. It is defined as Country-by-Country Items Disclosed / Required; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures completeness of jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction tax reporting (country-by-country data, strategy disclosures). Tax transparency is the ESG metric where regulatory obligation and public scrutiny meet, and partial disclosure reads as evasion risk in the court of public opinion. Disclosure completeness should be measured against the strictest applicable regime, not the most lenient. Read with effective tax rate reconciliation quality and controversy history.

## Formula

```
Country-by-Country Items Disclosed / Required
```
