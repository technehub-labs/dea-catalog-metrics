## 2. Definition

Knowledge Article Effectiveness (KAE) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of knowledge management reporting. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Articles Rated Helpful / Article Views with Rating, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Articles Rated Helpful / Article Views with Rating
```
