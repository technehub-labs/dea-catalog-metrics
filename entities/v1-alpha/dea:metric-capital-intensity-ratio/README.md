## 2. Definition

Capital Intensity Ratio (CIR-VCF) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Valuation & Corporate Finance Standard Measures domain. It is computed as Capital Expenditure / Revenue, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the capital required per unit of output or revenue. Intensity defines the economics of scale in the business model. Low intensity is not virtue: some strategies require bought assets. Read with fixed asset turnover (Statement Analysis) and return on invested capital (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Capital Expenditure / Revenue
```
