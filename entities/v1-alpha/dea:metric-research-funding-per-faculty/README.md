## 2. Definition

A Education indicator, Research Funding per Faculty (RFF) quantifies growth over time. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Total Research Funding / Faculty FTEs, measured in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Education practice.

## 3. Context

External research funding divided by faculty count - the research-intensity metric that ranking systems and institutional strategy both privilege, since sponsored research is simultaneously money, prestige, and the training apparatus of the research enterprise. Its strategic significance is the concentration it reveals: research funding distributes along a steep power curve, a small share of faculty holding most grants, so averages are dominated by outliers and the honest view splits the distribution rather than quoting its mean. Read with the indirect-recovery context: grant funding arrives with facilities-and-administration recovery rates that determine what the institution actually nets, and growth in funding that erodes recovery economics is volume without margin - the research-university version of revenue without profit.

## Formula

```
Total Research Funding / Faculty FTEs
```
