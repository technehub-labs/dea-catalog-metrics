## 2. Definition

A Product Costing & Margin Management indicator, Cost Reduction Program Realization (CRPR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is computed as Realized Cost Reductions / Committed Reductions, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Tracks the share of targeted savings from a cost program that actually lands in budgets, run-rates, or P&L, as opposed to announced or banked in slides. It matters because large programs routinely realize well under half of announced value, and the gap is a leading indicator of weak execution culture. Be alert to double counting, savings from volume declines booked as efficiency, and baseline manipulation. Read with EBIT margin trajectory and headcount or run-rate cost trends for corroboration.

## Formula

```
CRPR = Realized Cost Reductions / Committed Reductions
```
