## 2. Definition

Article Defect Rate (ADR-KM) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Knowledge Management (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Articles Flagged Incorrect / Articles, measured in events per thousand or million (scaled). It is applied across virtually every sector. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Articles Flagged Incorrect / Articles
```
