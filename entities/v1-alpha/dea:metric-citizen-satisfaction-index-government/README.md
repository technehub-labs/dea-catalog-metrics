## 2. Definition

Citizen Satisfaction Index (Government) (CSI-G) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of government & public administration reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Weighted Citizen Survey Score, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Public Sector.

## 3. Context

Measures aggregated satisfaction with government services, typically survey-based across touchpoints. It is the demand-side legitimacy metric for public institutions, and the early-warning indicator for channel abandonment toward informal routes. Beware selection bias: only engaged users answer, and the disengaged are the policy problem. Segment by demographic and digital-access characteristics, not just channel. Read with digital channel adoption (Government) and complaint volumes.
