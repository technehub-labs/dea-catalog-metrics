## 2. Definition

Innovation and Slack Time Ratio (ISTR-AG) measures capability maturity or development in the Agile Delivery & Team Effectiveness domain. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Slack or Exploration Time / Total Time; the result is stated in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of team capacity reserved for improvement and exploration work. Slack is what converts delivery teams into improving teams; 100% feature utilization guarantees stagnation. Slack consumed by unplanned work is not slack. Read with toil-to-work ratio and innovation pipeline conversion rate.

## Formula

```
Slack or Exploration Time / Total Time
```
