## 2. Definition

Requirement Ambiguity Review Pass Rate (RARPR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of requirements engineering & product discovery reporting. The formula is Requirements Passing Ambiguity Review / Reviewed - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Requirements Passing Ambiguity Review / Reviewed
```
