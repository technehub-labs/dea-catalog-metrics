## 2. Definition

Learner Satisfaction Score (Deep Dive - L&D) (LSSDD) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking learning & development effectiveness (deep dive). The formula is Post-Learning Satisfaction Rating - reported in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
