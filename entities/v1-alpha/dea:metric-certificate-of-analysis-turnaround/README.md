## 2. Definition

A Chemicals & Specialty Materials indicator, Certificate of Analysis Turnaround (COAT) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Hours from Batch Completion to Issued CoA, expressed in hours. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is concentrated in Chemicals.

## Formula

```
Hours from Batch Completion to Issued CoA
```
