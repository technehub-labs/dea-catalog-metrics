## 2. Definition

Franchisee Royalty Collection Rate (FRCR-D) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking franchise operations (deep dive). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Royalties Collected / Royalties Due, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures royalties actually collected against royalties billed. It is the cash-integrity metric of franchising: royalty leakage through under-reported sales is the classic franchise-system dispute, and collection rates reveal both franchisee financial health and the franchisor's audit enforcement posture. Collection improvement from waived arrears is generosity, not performance. Read with audit program findings and franchisee unit economics consistency.

## Formula

```
Royalties Collected / Royalties Due
```
