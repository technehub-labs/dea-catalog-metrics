## 2. Definition

Net Effective Rent per Square Metre (NER) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of real estate portfolio & property management reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Net Rent Net of Concessions / Lettable Area, expressed in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Real Estate.

## 3. Context

Measures rent actually achieved after concessions, free periods, fit-out contributions, and incentives, standardized per area. It is the true pricing metric of leasing, and the gap between face and net effective rates widens in weak markets before headline rents admit it. Concession accounting is the gaming battleground, so standardize amortization conventions across assets. Read with economic occupancy rate and market rent comparables.

## Formula

```
Net Rent Net of Concessions / Lettable Area
```
