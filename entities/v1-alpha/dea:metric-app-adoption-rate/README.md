## 2. Definition

App Adoption Rate (AAR-MOB) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of mobile & client development reporting. Its calculation is Active App Users / Eligible Users, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Software practice.

## Formula

```
Active App Users / Eligible Users
```
