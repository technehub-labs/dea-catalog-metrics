## 2. Definition

Audience Share (ASH) measures growth over time in the Media & Entertainment domain. The formula is Audience for Property / Total Category Audience - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Media & entertainment sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

The audience of a channel, program, or platform as a share of total audience in the market and slot - the competitive-position metric of media, and the one that expresses relative standing better than any absolute number: total audience moves with the market (seasonality, weather, news cycles), share strips the market effect and shows the head-to-head result. Its strategic significance is the advertising arithmetic: share is the audience version of market share, and the share-versus-share-of-advertising-spend comparison reveals whether a channel monetizes its audience at market rates - the yield question that determines whether share leadership converts to revenue leadership. Read with the platform context: linear share versus total-video share are different games, and a channel holding linear share while the linear market shrinks is winning a shrinking contest - the distinction that makes share-only analysis dangerous in the streaming era. The slot-level view is where programming decisions actually live.

## Formula

```
Audience for Property / Total Category Audience
```
