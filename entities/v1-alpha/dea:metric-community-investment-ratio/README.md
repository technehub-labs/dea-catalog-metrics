## 2. Definition

A Sustainability & ESG indicator, Community Investment Ratio (CIR2) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. Its calculation is Community Investment Spend / Pre-Tax Profit, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Corporate contribution to community causes - funding, in-kind resources, and employee volunteering - as a share of pre-tax profit, the conventional benchmarking form used across the corporate citizenship tradition. Its strategic weight is license to operate: for operations whose presence has visible local consequence - extraction, heavy industry, large employers - community investment is a maintenance cost of social acceptance, and underinvestment surfaces as permitting delay and opposition at exactly the moment the operation needs goodwill. The honest analysis separates strategic investment (programs aligned to business context and tracked for outcome) from dispersed philanthropy (donations tracked only by amount), since the two carry entirely different return profiles. Read with local-spend and employment data: a company investing heavily locally while extracting value elsewhere tells a different community story than the ratio alone conveys.

## Formula

```
Community Investment Spend / Pre-Tax Profit
```
