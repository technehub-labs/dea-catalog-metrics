## 2. Definition

Savings Realization Rate (Sourcing) (SRR-SS) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking strategic sourcing & supplier performance (deep dive). It is defined as Realized vs Identified Savings / Identified Savings; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether negotiated savings appear in actual run-rate spend. Realization is the credibility test of the sourcing function: unrealized savings are negotiating points. Baselines crafted to manufacture savings corrupt the measure. Read with cost avoidance realization rate and year-over-year spend reduction verified.

## Formula

```
Realized vs Identified Savings / Identified Savings
```
