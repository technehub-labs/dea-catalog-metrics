## 2. Definition

Market Entry Ramp Rate (MERR) captures growth over time for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Share Achieved within Plan Period after Entry / Plan; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly a new market entry reaches target share or volume. Ramp rate validates entry strategy before capital is fully committed. Targets set soft post-entry to declare success corrupt the measure. Read with market penetration rate (Deep Dive) and time-to-market for new entries.

## Formula

```
Share Achieved within Plan Period after Entry / Plan
```
