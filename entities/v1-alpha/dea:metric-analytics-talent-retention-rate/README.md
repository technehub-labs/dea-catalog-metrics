## 2. Definition

Within Data Science & Analytics Operations, Analytics Talent Retention Rate (ATRR-DS) is used to gauge the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. It is defined as Analytics Staff Retained / Analytics Staff; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures retention of data scientists, engineers, and analysts. Analytics talent is scarce, expensive to reacquire, and unusually sensitive to organizational frustration - legacy tooling, unclear impact, and undelivered infrastructure promises drive attrition. Retention here protects the accumulated context that makes analytics productive. Read with regretted attrition by team and engagement evidence about tooling and impact clarity.

## Formula

```
Analytics Staff Retained / Analytics Staff
```
