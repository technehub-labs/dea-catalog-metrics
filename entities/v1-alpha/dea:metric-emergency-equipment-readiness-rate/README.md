## 2. Definition

Within Crisis Management & Emergency Response, Emergency Equipment Readiness Rate (EERR) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Emergency Equipment Passing Inspection / Equipment Items - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Emergency Equipment Passing Inspection / Equipment Items
```
