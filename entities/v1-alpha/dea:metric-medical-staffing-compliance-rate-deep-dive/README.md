## 2. Definition

Within Sports Clubs & Leagues (Deep Dive), Medical Staffing Compliance Rate (Deep Dive) (MSCR-D) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Events with Full Medical Coverage / Events - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Sports sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to required medical staffing standards for training and matchday - qualified personnel present, equipment protocols, emergency preparedness. It is the minimum safety infrastructure of sport, and compliance failures convert routine injuries into career and liability events. Verify presence and currency of credentials, not just roster positions. Read with injury burden rate (Deep Dive) and emergency action plan drill performance.

## Formula

```
MSCR-D = Events with Full Medical Coverage / Events
```
