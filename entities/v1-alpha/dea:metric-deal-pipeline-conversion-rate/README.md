## 2. Definition

Deal Pipeline Conversion Rate (DPCR) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking mergers & acquisitions. It is defined as Deals Completed / Deals Screened; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of identified targets or opportunities advancing through diligence to signed deals. It disciplines corporate development: a fat pipeline that never converts is a symptom of unfocused screening or overpriced markets. Stage definitions decide the number, so fix conversion criteria before comparing periods or teams. Read with time to close (M&A) and deal value creation ratio for what conversion actually yields.

## Formula

```
Deals Completed / Deals Screened
```
