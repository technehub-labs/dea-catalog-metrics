## 2. Definition

A Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Operations indicator, Policy Attestation Rate (Deep Dive) (PAR-C) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Staff Attesting to Policy Updates / Staff in Scope - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Staff Attesting to Policy Updates / Staff in Scope
```
