## 2. Definition

Within Talent Acquisition Deep Dive, Candidate Experience Score (CXS-TA) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Post-Process Candidate Survey Score, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
CXS-TA = Post-Process Candidate Survey Score
```
