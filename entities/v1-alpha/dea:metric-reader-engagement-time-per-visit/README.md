## 2. Definition

Reader Engagement Time per Visit (RETV) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of publishing & news media reporting. Its calculation is Total Time on Site / Visits, measured in minutes. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
Total Time on Site / Visits
```
