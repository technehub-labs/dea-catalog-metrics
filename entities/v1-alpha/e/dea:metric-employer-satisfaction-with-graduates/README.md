## 2. Definition

Employer Satisfaction with Graduates (ESG-ED) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Education & Training Outcome Standard Measures domain. Its calculation is Employer Survey Score of Graduate Readiness, measured in score (points). It originates from and is most used in Education practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures employer-rated readiness and performance of hired graduates. Employer signals close the loop between curriculum and labor market reality. Surveys dominated by a few large recruiting partners skew. Read with graduate employment rate and curriculum currency rate.

## Formula

```
ESG-ED = Employer Survey Score of Graduate Readiness
```
