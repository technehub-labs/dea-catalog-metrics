## 2. Definition

Emergency Evacuation Drill Compliance (EEDC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of crisis management & emergency response reporting. It is computed as Sites Completing Required Drills / Sites, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
EEDC = Sites Completing Required Drills / Sites
```
