## 2. Definition

Email Open Rate (EOR) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking marketing. It is computed as Email Opens / Emails Delivered, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
EOR = Email Opens / Emails Delivered
```
