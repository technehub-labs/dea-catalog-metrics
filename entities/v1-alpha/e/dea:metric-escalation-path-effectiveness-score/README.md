## 2. Definition

A Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring indicator, Escalation Path Effectiveness Score (EPES) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Survey and Case-Based Escalation Effectiveness; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
EPES = Survey and Case-Based Escalation Effectiveness
```
