## 2. Definition

Engagement Action Plan Closure Rate (EAPCR) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking employee engagement & culture (deep dive). The formula is Post-Survey Actions Closed by Due Date / Actions - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
EAPCR = Post-Survey Actions Closed by Due Date / Actions
```
