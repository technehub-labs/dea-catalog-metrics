## 2. Definition

E-Discovery Readiness Score (EDRS) measures capability maturity or development in the Records & Information Management domain. It is defined as Rubric of Indexing, Collection and Processing Readiness; the result is stated in score (points). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.
