## 2. Definition

Exhibitor Net Promoter Score (XNPS) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of events & exhibitions reporting. The formula is Exhibitor NPS Survey Result - reported in score (points). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Media & Entertainment sector.

## 3. Context

Measures exhibitor satisfaction and likelihood to rebook-recommend. It is the leading indicator of the rebook rate, and it surfaces ROI disappointment while there is still time to fix the next edition. Score drivers (lead quality, attendee composition, logistics) map to the improvement levers, so read with verbatims. Read with exhibitor rebook rate and lead-conversion reporting quality.

## Formula

XNPS = Exhibitor NPS Survey Result
