## 2. Definition

A Operating Model Monitoring & Design Effectiveness indicator, Decision Rights Clarity Score (DRCS-OM) quantifies capability maturity or development. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Decision Types with Assigned Owner / Decision Types, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether people know who decides what, at what threshold. Unclear decision rights are the root cause of both slow and contested decisions. Scores from surveys measure perceived clarity, not documented or exercised rights. Read with delegation of authority compliance and decision right first time rate.

## Formula

```
Decision Types with Assigned Owner / Decision Types
```
