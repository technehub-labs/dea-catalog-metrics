## 2. Definition

A Sustainability & ESG indicator, Waste Diversion Rate (WDR) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. The formula is Non-Landfilled Waste / Total Waste - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of waste diverted from landfill through recycling, reuse, or recovery. It matters because it is the headline operational circularity metric for stakeholders and regulators. Gaming risk includes counting waste-to-energy as diversion where rules disallow it, or contamination-laden bales counted as recycled. Read with Landfill Rate and Waste Intensity to confirm diversion is paired with genuine waste reduction.

## Formula

```
Non-Landfilled Waste / Total Waste
```
