## 2. Definition

Regulatory Horizon Anticipation Rate (RHAR) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning domain. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Regulatory Changes Anticipated before Formal Proposal / Material Changes, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how often regulatory changes were identified before they were formally proposed or announced. Anticipation converts regulatory surprise into planning time. Rates counted only on enacted rules that everyone saw coming are easy. Read with regulatory change adoption timeliness and compliance obligation register coverage.

## Formula

```
Regulatory Changes Anticipated before Formal Proposal / Material Changes
```
