## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective, Brand Preference Score (BPS-BSC) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Survey-Based Preference versus Competitors, expressed in score (points). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of target customers who name the brand as their preferred choice. Preference is the demand-side moat that permits pricing power and lowers CAC. Aided-recall survey formats inflate preference versus spontaneous measures. Read with pricing power index and market share trend.

## Formula

```
Survey-Based Preference versus Competitors
```
