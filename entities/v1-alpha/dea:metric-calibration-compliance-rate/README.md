## 2. Definition

A Quality Management indicator, Calibration Compliance Rate (CCR-Q) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is In-Calibration Gauges / Gauges in Service - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
In-Calibration Gauges / Gauges in Service
```
