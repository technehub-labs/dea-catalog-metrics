## 2. Definition

Partner Retention Rate (PRTN) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking alliances & partnerships management. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Active Partners Year-over-Year / Prior Active Partners, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how many strategic partners remain active and committed year over year. Partner churn destroys compounding ecosystem value. Retention counted on paper partnerships that no longer transact is nominal. Read with alliance health score and partner NPS.

## Formula

```
Active Partners Year-over-Year / Prior Active Partners
```
