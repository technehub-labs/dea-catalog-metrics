## 2. Definition

Within IT Financial Management & Technology Budgeting, Showback or Chargeback Coverage (SBC-ITFM) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. It is defined as Business Units Receiving Cost Visibility / Units; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of technology cost made visible to, or billed to, consuming business units. Visibility is the precondition for demand-side accountability. Showback without consequences changes awareness, not behavior. Read with technology unit cost per user and shadow IT spend ratio.

## Formula

```
Business Units Receiving Cost Visibility / Units
```
