## 2. Definition

Single Source of Truth Metric Adoption (SSTMA) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking enterprise decision effectiveness & governance monitoring. The formula is Governed Metrics from Authoritative Source / Governed Metrics - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how widely decisions rely on the governed metric definitions rather than departmental variants. Competing 'versions of the truth' turn meetings into data disputes. Adoption is cultural and often overstated in self-report. Read with management information freshness score and operating model KPI coverage ratio.

## Formula

```
Governed Metrics from Authoritative Source / Governed Metrics
```
