## 2. Definition

Human Rights Risk Assessment Coverage (HRRAC) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking environmental, social & governance reporting. The formula is Sites and Suppliers Assessed / In-Scope - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of operations, suppliers, and geographies covered by human-rights due diligence. Mandatory human-rights due diligence law is spreading across jurisdictions, and coverage gaps are legal exposure as much as ethical risk. Depth matters as much as breadth - a desktop assessment of a high-risk supply chain is not due diligence. Read with remediation case outcomes and supplier ESG assessment coverage (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Sites and Suppliers Assessed / In-Scope
```
