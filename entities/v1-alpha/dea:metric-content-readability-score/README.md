## 2. Definition

Content Readability Score (CRS-UX) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of ux & design systems reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Standardized Reading Grade of Product Copy - reported in score (points). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.
