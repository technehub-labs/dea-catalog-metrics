## 2. Definition

Tenant Satisfaction Score (Portfolio) (TSS-P) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Real Estate Portfolio & Property Management domain. Its calculation is Average Tenant Survey Score, measured in score (points). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Real Estate practice.

## 3. Context

Measures occupant satisfaction with building services, management responsiveness, and environment. Satisfaction is the soft leading indicator of retention and of tenants' willingness to pay escalations, and it flags building problems before they become lease problems. Watch for survey nonresponse as a signal, and segment by building for actionable focus. Read with tenant retention rate (Portfolio) and work-order resolution times.
