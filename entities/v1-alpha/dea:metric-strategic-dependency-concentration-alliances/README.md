## 2. Definition

Strategic Dependency Concentration (Alliances) (SDCA) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Alliances & Partnerships Management domain. Its calculation is Alliance Value from Single Partner / Total Alliance Value, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how much strategic capability rests on a small number of alliances. Concentrated dependence converts partner drift into strategic risk. Dependency classified as mutual often is not. Read with alliance risk register coverage and concentration risk ratio.

## Formula

```
Alliance Value from Single Partner / Total Alliance Value
```
