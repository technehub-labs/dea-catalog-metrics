## 2. Definition

Learning Transfer Rate (Deep Dive) (LTR-BSC) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective. It is defined as Learned Skills Applied on Job within 90 Days / Skills Taught; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how much training investment demonstrably changes job performance. Transfer is where learning ROI lives: most training decays without reinforcement. Transfer measured by post-course surveys measures satisfaction, not behavior. Read with training investment per employee and digital skill proficiency index.

## Formula

```
Learned Skills Applied on Job within 90 Days / Skills Taught
```
