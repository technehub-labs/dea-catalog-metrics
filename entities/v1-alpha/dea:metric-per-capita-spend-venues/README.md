## 2. Definition

Within Entertainment Venues & Attractions, Per Capita Spend (Venues) (PCSV) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Total Revenue / Attendance; the result is stated in currency per unit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures average spend per visitor on food, beverage, merchandise, and experiences. It is the monetization metric of attractions and venues, and its composition (upsell quality versus captive-pricing resentment) determines sustainability of the rate. Time-in-venue and dwell patterns drive spend, so read with throughput and capacity utilization. Read with guest satisfaction and secondary-spend mix trends.

## Formula

```
Total Revenue / Attendance
```
