## 2. Definition

Regulatory Barrier Exposure Score (RBES) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Weighted Exposure of Market Strategy to Regulatory Constraints, expressed in score (points). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how exposed the market position is to regulatory barriers changing. Barrier removal can erase a protected position overnight. Scores that treat barriers as static misread regulatory momentum. Read with regulatory horizon anticipation rate and four-firm concentration ratio.

## Formula

```
Weighted Exposure of Market Strategy to Regulatory Constraints
```
