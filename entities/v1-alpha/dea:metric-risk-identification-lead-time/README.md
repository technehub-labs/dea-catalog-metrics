## 2. Definition

Risk Identification Lead Time (RILT) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of project risk & issue management reporting. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It is defined as Days Risk Identified before Impact vs Realized; the result is stated in days. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Days Risk Identified before Impact vs Realized
```
