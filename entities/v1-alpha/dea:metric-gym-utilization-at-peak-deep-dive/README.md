## 2. Definition

Within Fitness & Wellness (Deep Dive), Gym Utilization at Peak (Deep Dive) (GUP-D) is used to gauge the utilization or availability of capacity. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Peak Attendees / Usable Capacity, expressed in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Its use is concentrated in Health & Fitness.

## Formula

```
Peak Attendees / Usable Capacity
```
