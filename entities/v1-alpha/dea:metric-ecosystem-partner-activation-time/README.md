## 2. Definition

Within Corporate Venturing & Partnerships, Ecosystem Partner Activation Time (EPAT) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Days from Partner Agreement to First Joint Value Delivered - reported in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures time from partner agreement signing to first revenue-generating activity. It matters because partner value is realized at activation, not at signature. Clock definitions can start late, hiding onboarding friction. Read with Partner Revenue Ramp and Partner Channel Conflict Rate to confirm activation converts into performance.

## Formula

```
Days from Partner Agreement to First Joint Value Delivered
```
