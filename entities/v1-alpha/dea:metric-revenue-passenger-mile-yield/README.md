## 2. Definition

Revenue Passenger Mile Yield (RPMY) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of airline & transportation standard measures (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Passenger Revenue / Revenue Passenger Miles; the result is stated in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Airlines & Aviation, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue per passenger mile actually flown. Yield is the pricing power signal beneath load factor. Yield compared across different stage lengths misleads. Read with passenger load factor (Deep Dive) and revenue per available seat kilometer.

## Formula

```
Passenger Revenue / Revenue Passenger Miles
```
