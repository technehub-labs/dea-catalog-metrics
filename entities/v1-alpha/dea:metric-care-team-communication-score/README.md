## 2. Definition

A Medical Practice & Clinical Operations indicator, Care Team Communication Score (CTCS) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Staff Survey on Clinical Communication Effectiveness; the result is stated in score (points). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Healthcare, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.
