## 2. Definition

Within Data Privacy & Protection, Privacy Impact Assessment Rate (PIAR) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as High-Risk Projects with Completed PIA / High-Risk Projects; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of new systems and processing activities completing PIAs before launch. PIAs are where privacy-by-design actually happens. Assessments completed retroactively are archaeology. Read with privacy by design compliance rate and personal data inventory coverage.

## Formula

```
High-Risk Projects with Completed PIA / High-Risk Projects
```
