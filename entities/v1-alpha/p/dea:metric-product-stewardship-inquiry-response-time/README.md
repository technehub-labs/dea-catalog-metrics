## 2. Definition

Within Chemicals & Specialty Materials, Product Stewardship Inquiry Response Time (PIRT) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Days to Respond to Product Stewardship Request, expressed in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Its use is concentrated in Chemicals.

## Formula

```
PIRT = Days to Respond to Product Stewardship Request
```
