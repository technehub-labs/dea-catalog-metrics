## 2. Definition

Within Airline & Transportation Standard Measures (Deep Dive), Punctuality Recovery Index (PRI-AT) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. The formula is Post-Disruption On-Time Share within 24 Hours - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Airlines & Aviation sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
PRI-AT = Post-Disruption On-Time Share within 24 Hours
```
