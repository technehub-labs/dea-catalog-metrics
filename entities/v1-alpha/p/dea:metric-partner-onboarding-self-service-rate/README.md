## 2. Definition

A Channel & Distribution Management indicator, Partner Onboarding Self-Service Rate (POSSR) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Onboarding Steps Completed without Human Touch / Steps - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
POSSR = Onboarding Steps Completed without Human Touch / Steps
```
