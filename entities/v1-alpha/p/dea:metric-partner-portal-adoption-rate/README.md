## 2. Definition

Partner Portal Adoption Rate (PPA-AP) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Alliances & Partnerships Management domain. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It is defined as Active Partner Users / Registered Partner Users; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
PPA-AP = Active Partner Users / Registered Partner Users
```
