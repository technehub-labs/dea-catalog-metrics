## 2. Definition

Performance Data Quality Score - Reviews (PDQS-OKR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking enterprise okr & performance management. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Reviews with Complete Evidence / Reviews - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
PDQS-OKR = Reviews with Complete Evidence / Reviews
```
