## 2. Definition

Pay Equity Audit Completion Rate (PEACR) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Audits Completed per Mandated Cycle / Mandated Audits; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether required pay equity analyses are completed on schedule with rigor. Audits are the detection mechanism for inequity. Completion without remediation follow-through is diagnosis without treatment. Read with pay equity gap and remediation plan completion rate.

## Formula

```
PEACR = Audits Completed per Mandated Cycle / Mandated Audits
```
