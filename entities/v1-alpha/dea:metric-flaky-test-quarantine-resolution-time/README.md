## 2. Definition

Flaky Test Quarantine Resolution Time (FTQRT) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking continuous integration. It is computed as Days a Test Remains Quarantined, expressed in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
