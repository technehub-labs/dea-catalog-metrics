## 2. Definition

ARPU - Publishing (Deep Dive) (ARPU-P) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of publishing & news media reporting. Its calculation is Total Revenue / Active Readers or Subscribers, measured in currency per unit. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue per subscriber, blended across tiers, bundles, and corporate subscriptions. It is the monetization summary of publishing strategy, and its decomposition (price, tier mix, promotions, churn composition) determines whether growth is real. ARPU gains from harvesting (price rises to a shrinking base) read differently from ARPU stable on a growing base. Read with digital subscription conversion rate (Deep Dive) and churn composition.

## Formula

```
Total Revenue / Active Readers or Subscribers
```
