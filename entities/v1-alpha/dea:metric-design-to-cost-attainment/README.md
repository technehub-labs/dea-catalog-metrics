## 2. Definition

Design-to-Cost Attainment (DCA) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of engineering & design reporting. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Designs Meeting Cost Targets / Designs - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether products achieve their target cost at design release and at production ramp. It extends target costing into an execution scorecard, closing the classic gap between what engineering promised and what manufacturing delivers. Attainment claimed at nominal volumes or favorable exchange rates is soft; insist on standard-cost-basis reporting. Read with cost of quality ratio and procurement savings on the same parts.

## Formula

```
Designs Meeting Cost Targets / Designs
```
