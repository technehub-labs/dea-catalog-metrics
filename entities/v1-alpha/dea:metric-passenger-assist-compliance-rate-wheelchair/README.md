## 2. Definition

A Airline & Transportation Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Passenger Assist Compliance Rate (Wheelchair) (PACRW) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Assistance Delivered within SLA / Assistance Requests - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Airlines & Aviation sector.

## Formula

Assistance Delivered within SLA / Assistance Requests
