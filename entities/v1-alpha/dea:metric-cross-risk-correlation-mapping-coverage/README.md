## 2. Definition

Cross-Risk Correlation Mapping Coverage (CRCMC) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking emerging risk & horizon scanning. It is defined as Mapped Risk Interdependencies / Key Risk Pairs; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how well interactions between risks (compounding and cascading effects) are mapped. Risks correlate in crises: individually tolerable risks combine into intolerable ones. Correlation from historical calm-period data understates crisis correlation. Read with risk network density index and stress test scenario coverage.

## Formula

```
Mapped Risk Interdependencies / Key Risk Pairs
```
