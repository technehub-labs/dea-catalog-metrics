## 2. Definition

Within Engineering & Design, Tolerance Compliance Yield (TCY) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Dimensions within Tolerance / Dimensions Measured - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Dimensions within Tolerance / Dimensions Measured
```
