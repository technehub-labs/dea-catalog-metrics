## 2. Definition

A Channel & Distribution Management indicator, Partner Satisfaction Score (Deep Dive) (PSSDD) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Annual Partner Survey Composite - reported in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures partner-rated satisfaction with the program, economics, and support. Partner satisfaction predicts program participation and shelf-space priority. Surveys answered only by managed partners miss the disengaged. Read with partner NPS and partner retention rate.

## Formula

```
PSSDD = Annual Partner Survey Composite
```
