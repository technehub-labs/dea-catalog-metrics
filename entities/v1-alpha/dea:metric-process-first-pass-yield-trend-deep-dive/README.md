## 2. Definition

Process First-Pass Yield Trend (Deep Dive) (FPYT-D) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - internal process perspective. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Change in First-Pass Yield between Periods, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of process outputs done right the first time, tracked over time. First-pass yield is the purest process-quality signal: rework is the tax on defects. Yield measured post-inspection (with rework quietly fixed) overstates capability. Read with rework rate and process cost per transaction.
