## 2. Definition

Community Sentiment Score (Gaming) (CSS-G) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Gaming Development & Live Operations domain. It is defined as Composite Player Sentiment Score; the result is stated in score (points). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Gaming, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.
