## 2. Definition

A Textile & Apparel (Deep Dive) indicator, Delivery Lead Time - Factory to DC (Deep Dive) (DLT-D) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its calculation is Days from Ex-Factory to DC Receipt, measured in days. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Textiles & Apparel practice.
