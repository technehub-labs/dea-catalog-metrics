## 2. Definition

A Corporate Communications & PR (Deep Dive) indicator, Share of Positive Coverage (SOPC) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. The formula is Positive Stories / Total Stories Monitored - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of media mentions that are favorable. It is the tone metric of communications, and it is most informative on stories the company did not plant - earned tone reflects reputation more than coverage volume does. Sentiment classification quality determines credibility, so validate the scoring against human-coded samples. Read with reputation risk radar coverage and message pull-through in key narratives.

## Formula

```
SOPC = Positive Stories / Total Stories Monitored
```
