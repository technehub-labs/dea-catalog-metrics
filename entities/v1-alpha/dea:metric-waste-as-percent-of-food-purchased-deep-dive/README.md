## 2. Definition

Waste as Percent of Food Purchased (Deep Dive) (WPF-D) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking restaurant operations (deep dive). It is defined as Food Wasted / Food Purchased; the result is stated in percent (%). In Restaurants, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Food Wasted / Food Purchased
```
