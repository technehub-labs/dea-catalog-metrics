## 2. Definition

Within Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting, Renewable Electricity Share (RES-E) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. The formula is Renewable Electricity / Total Electricity - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of electricity consumption from renewable sources, contracted or generated. It is the flagship Scope 2 reduction instrument and the most commonly cited public commitment, which makes accounting quality the honesty issue - annual matching versus hourly, bundled versus unbundled certificates. Read the procurement structure, not just the percentage. Read with Scope 2 Emissions Intensity trends and grid-hour matching progress.

## Formula

```
RES-E = Renewable Electricity / Total Electricity
```
