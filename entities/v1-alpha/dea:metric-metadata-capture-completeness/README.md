## 2. Definition

Metadata Capture Completeness (MDCC) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of records & information management reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Required Record Metadata Captured at Filing / Required Fields - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Required Record Metadata Captured at Filing / Required Fields
```
