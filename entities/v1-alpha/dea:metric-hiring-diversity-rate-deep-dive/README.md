## 2. Definition

Hiring Diversity Rate (Deep Dive) (HDR-DEI) captures capability maturity or development for organizations tracking diversity, equity & inclusion (deep dive). The formula is Diverse Hires / Total Hires - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures demographic diversity of new hires relative to goals and pipeline availability. Hiring is the fastest lever on workforce composition. Rates achieved by lowering standards or by reclassifying roles are fake progress. Read with diversity hiring ratio and source of hire effectiveness index.

## Formula

```
Diverse Hires / Total Hires
```
