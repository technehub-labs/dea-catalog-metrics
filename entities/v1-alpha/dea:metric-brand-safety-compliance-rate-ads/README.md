## 2. Definition

Brand Safety Compliance Rate (Ads) (BSCR-A) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking advertising & media buying. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Impressions on Approved Content / Impressions, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
Impressions on Approved Content / Impressions
```
