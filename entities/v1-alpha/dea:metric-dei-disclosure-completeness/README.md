## 2. Definition

DEI Disclosure Completeness (DEIDC) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Required External DEI Disclosures Filed / Required - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how fully the organization's DEI reporting meets disclosure frameworks and expectations. Disclosure is increasingly investor- and regulator-expected. Completeness measured against comfortable frameworks rather than required ones is selective. Read with ESG disclosure accuracy and regulatory report filing timeliness.

## Formula

```
Required External DEI Disclosures Filed / Required
```
