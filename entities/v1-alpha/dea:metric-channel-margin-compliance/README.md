## 2. Definition

Channel Margin Compliance (CMC-CD) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking channel & distribution management. It is defined as Sales within Approved Channel Margin Framework / Sales Sampled; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Sales within Approved Channel Margin Framework / Sales Sampled
```
