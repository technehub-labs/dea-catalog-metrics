## 2. Definition

A Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive) indicator, Engagement Survey Participation Rate (ESPR) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Responses Received / Surveyed Employees; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Responses Received / Surveyed Employees
```
