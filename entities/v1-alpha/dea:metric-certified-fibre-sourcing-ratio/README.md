## 2. Definition

Within Pulp, Paper & Forest Products, Certified Fibre Sourcing Ratio (CFSR) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Certified Fibre / Total Fibre, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Manufacturing.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of virgin fibre from certified (FSC/PEFC) or controlled-wood sources. It is the deforestation-risk control for paper and packaging supply chains, and the primary evidence customers and NGOs demand. Chain-of-custody integrity is the weak link: certification certificates must trace to actual delivery lots. Read with supplier audit findings and mixed-input control procedures.

## Formula

```
Certified Fibre / Total Fibre
```
