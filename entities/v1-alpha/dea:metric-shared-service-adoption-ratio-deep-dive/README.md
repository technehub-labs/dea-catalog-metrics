## 2. Definition

Shared Service Adoption Ratio (Deep Dive) (SSAR-D) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking operating model monitoring & design effectiveness. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Business Units Using Shared Services / Eligible Units, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of eligible demand routed through shared services rather than local provision. Adoption is where shared-service economics are actually realized. Mandated adoption without service quality breeds shadow functions. Read with cost of governance ratio and process standardization ratio.

## Formula

```
Business Units Using Shared Services / Eligible Units
```
