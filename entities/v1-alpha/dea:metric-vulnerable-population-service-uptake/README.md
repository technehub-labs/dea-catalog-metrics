## 2. Definition

Vulnerable Population Service Uptake (VPSU) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Eligible Vulnerable Citizens Receiving Entitlements / Eligible; the result is stated in percent (%). In Public sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of eligible vulnerable citizens actually receiving entitled services. Non-uptake is silent exclusion: the neediest access services least. Uptake measured on those who applied excludes those deterred from applying. Read with service accessibility standard compliance (Public) and benefit take-up rate.

## Formula

```
Eligible Vulnerable Citizens Receiving Entitlements / Eligible
```
