## 2. Definition

Fan Engagement Rate (Digital Sports) (FER-S) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Sports Clubs & Leagues (Deep Dive) domain. It is computed as Engaged Digital Fans / Follower Base, expressed in percent (%). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Sports.

## Formula

```
Engaged Digital Fans / Follower Base
```
