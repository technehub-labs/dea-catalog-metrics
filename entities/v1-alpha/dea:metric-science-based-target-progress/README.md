## 2. Definition

Science-Based Target Progress (SBTP) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking environmental, social & governance reporting. It is computed as Emissions Reduction Achieved / Committed Pathway, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures cumulative emission reductions against validated science-based target trajectories. SBTs convert ambition into an accountable linear pathway, and progress against the line is the honest test - most companies that miss, miss early and quietly, then face steep back-loaded reductions. Progress from portfolio divestments rather than operational reduction satisfies accounting but not the atmosphere. Read with Scope 1 and 2 intensity trends and abatement capital deployment.

## Formula

```
Emissions Reduction Achieved / Committed Pathway
```
