## 2. Definition

A Insurance Claims Deep Dive indicator, Excess Recovery Ratio (ERR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Recoveries from Excess / Reinsurers' Share / Share - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Recoveries from Excess / Reinsurers' Share / Share
```
