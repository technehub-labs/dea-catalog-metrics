## 2. Definition

Click-and-Collect On-Time Ready Rate (CCTR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of retail standard measures reporting. The formula is Orders Ready by Promised Time / Orders - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Retail & E-commerce sector.

## Formula

```
CCTR = Orders Ready by Promised Time / Orders
```
