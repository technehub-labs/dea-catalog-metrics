## 2. Definition

E-learning Completion Rate (ELCR) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking learning & development. It is computed as Completed Online Courses / Courses Started, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
ELCR = Completed Online Courses / Courses Started
```
