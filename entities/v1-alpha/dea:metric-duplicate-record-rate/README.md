## 2. Definition

Duplicate Record Rate (DRR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking data management & analytics. It is computed as Duplicate Records / Total Records, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Duplicate Records / Total Records
```
