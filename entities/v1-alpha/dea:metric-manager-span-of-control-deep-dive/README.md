## 2. Definition

A Standard Workforce & Pay Metrics indicator, Manager Span of Control (Deep Dive) (SOCDD) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Direct Reports per Manager; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the average number of direct reports per manager. Span is the structural lever of organizational cost and agility. Optimal spans differ by work complexity; uniform targets misfire. Read with operating model span and layer index and span of control deviation rate.

Direct Reports per Manager
