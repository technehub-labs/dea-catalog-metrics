## 2. Definition

Interaction to Next Paint (P75) (INP75) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of web & digital analytics standard measures reporting. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is 75th Percentile Interaction Latency - reported in milliseconds (ms). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Digital product sector.
