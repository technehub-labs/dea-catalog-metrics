## 2. Definition

Supplier Diversity Spend Share (SDS) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion domain. It is computed as Spend with Diverse Suppliers / Total Procurement Spend, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of procurement spend with certified diverse suppliers. It extends DEI commitments to the economic ecosystem and often carries regulatory or client-driven requirements in public contracting. Honest reads require spend addressability analysis - not all categories have diverse supply - and certification integrity, since pass-through arrangements can mask conventional suppliers. Read with supplier performance scores to counter the prejudice that diverse means riskier.

## Formula

```
Spend with Diverse Suppliers / Total Procurement Spend
```
