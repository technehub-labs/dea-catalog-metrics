## 2. Definition

Within Elder & Social Care, Care Plan Review Compliance (CPRC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Care Plans Reviewed within Cycle / Care Plans - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Social care sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of care recipients with current, on-time care plan reviews. It matters because outdated plans drive both care quality failures and regulatory findings. Reviews can be administratively completed without genuine reassessment. Read with Care Outcome measures and regulator inspection findings to confirm reviews change care.

## Formula

```
Care Plans Reviewed within Cycle / Care Plans
```
