## 2. Definition

Within Credit & Collections Risk (Enterprise), Utilization Ratio (Credit) (URC-CC) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Drawn Balance / Approved Limit - reported in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Drawn Balance / Approved Limit
```
