## 2. Definition

Regulatory Fines and Penalties Ratio (RFPR) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Operations domain. It is computed as Total Penalties / Revenue, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures enforcement costs - fines, penalties, disgorgement, mandated remediation spend - relative to revenue or regulatory exposure. It aggregates compliance failure into financial consequence, enabling board-level risk pricing. Small recurring penalties can signal more systemic weakness than rare large ones, so track frequency alongside magnitude. Read with repeat regulatory findings rate and compliance obligation completion rate.

## Formula

```
Total Penalties / Revenue
```
