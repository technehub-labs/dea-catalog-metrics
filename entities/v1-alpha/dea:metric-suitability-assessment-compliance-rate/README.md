## 2. Definition

A Asset & Wealth Management (Deep Dive) indicator, Suitability Assessment Compliance Rate (SACR-W) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is computed as Suitable Recommendations on Audit / Sampled, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Financial Services. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to required suitability processes - documented needs analysis, risk profiling, product-need matching. Suitability failures are the root scandal of wealth distribution, carrying both enforcement risk and civil liability, and they cluster in high-commission product pushes. File-level sampling with evidence checks, not process attestation, is the honest measurement. Read with complaint root causes and product concentration in client portfolios.

## Formula

```
Suitable Recommendations on Audit / Sampled
```
