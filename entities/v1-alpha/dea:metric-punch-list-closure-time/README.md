## 2. Definition

Within Construction & Engineering, Punch List Closure Time (PLCT) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Days from Handover Inspection to Punch List Completion, expressed in days. Its use is concentrated in Construction & engineering. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

Days from Handover Inspection to Punch List Completion
