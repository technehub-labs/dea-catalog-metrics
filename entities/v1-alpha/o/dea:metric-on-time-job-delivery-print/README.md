## 2. Definition

Within Printing & Publishing, On-Time Job Delivery (Print) (OTJD) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Jobs Delivered by Promised Date / Jobs; the result is stated in percent (%). In Printing & publishing, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of print jobs delivered by the promised date. It matters because print usually feeds downstream schedules (marketing launches, store openings) where late means wasted. Easy promise dates can inflate the metric while responsiveness suffers. Read with Cost per Page Printed and rework to confirm punctuality coexists with efficiency.

## Formula

```
OTJD = Jobs Delivered by Promised Date / Jobs
```
