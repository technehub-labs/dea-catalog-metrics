## 2. Definition

Offcut Recovery Rate (ORR) addresses environmental and social sustainability performance and is a fixture of furniture & wood products reporting. It is computed as Offcuts Reused / Offcuts Generated, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Manufacturing. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
ORR = Offcuts Reused / Offcuts Generated
```
