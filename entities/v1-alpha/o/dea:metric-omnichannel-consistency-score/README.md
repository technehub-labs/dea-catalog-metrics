## 2. Definition

Within Customer Journey & Experience, Omnichannel Consistency Score (OCS) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Weighted Score of Experience Consistency across Channels; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Captures how uniformly pricing, product information, policy answers, and service quality hold up across web, app, store, and contact center. It matters because customers increasingly compose their own journeys, and inconsistency reads as incompetence or worse, deception. The score is only as good as its mystery-shop or audit methodology; self-reported consistency tends to be optimistic. Watch for weightings that average away the worst channels rather than exposing them. Read with journey completion rate and complaint themes by channel.

## Formula

```
OCS = Weighted Score of Experience Consistency across Channels
```
