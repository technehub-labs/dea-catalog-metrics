## 2. Definition

Ecosystem Gravity Score (ECGS) captures capability maturity or development for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. It is defined as Attractiveness of Market Position to Complementary Players; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the strength of attraction the company's ecosystem exerts on partners and customers. Gravity compounds: strong ecosystems make switching multi-dimensional and expensive. Scores mixing installed base with genuine integration are inflated. Read with ecosystem coverage ratio and net dollar retention (Deep Dive).

Attractiveness of Market Position to Complementary Players
