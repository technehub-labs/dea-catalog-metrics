## 2. Definition

Patient Medication Review Completion (PMRC) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of pharmacy retail reporting. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Completed Medication Reviews / Due, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Healthcare.

## Formula

```
Completed Medication Reviews / Due
```
