## 2. Definition

Downgauging Achievement Rate (DAR) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Packaging & Plastics domain. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Products Meeting Reduced Material Targets / Products, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice.

## Formula

```
Products Meeting Reduced Material Targets / Products
```
