## 2. Definition

Governance Layer Efficiency Ratio (GLER-OM) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of operating model monitoring & design effectiveness reporting. Its calculation is Governance Bodies with Active Charters and Reviews / Bodies, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the cost and throughput of governance structures relative to decisions produced. Governance should be an investment with measurable throughput; usually it is overhead with unmeasured cost. Ratios counting only formal boards miss the meeting sprawl that dominates governance cost. Read with cost of governance ratio and decision cycle time - strategic decisions.

## Formula

```
Governance Bodies with Active Charters and Reviews / Bodies
```
