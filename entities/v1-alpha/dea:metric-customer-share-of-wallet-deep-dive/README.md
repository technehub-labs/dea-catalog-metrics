## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective, Customer Share of Wallet (Deep Dive) (CSW-D) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Spend with Company / Total Category Spend; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the company's share of each customer's total spend in the category. Wallet share grows far cheaper than new-customer acquisition. Self-reported category sizing from customers is unreliable. Read with net revenue retention and customer lifetime value trend.

## Formula

```
Spend with Company / Total Category Spend
```
