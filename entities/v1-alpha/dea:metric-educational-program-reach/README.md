## 2. Definition

Educational Program Reach (EPR-A) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking arts & culture. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Participants in Education Programs per Period, expressed in count (items per period). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Arts & culture.

## 3. Context

Educational program reach measures participation in arts education programming - school partnerships, workshops, student matinees, teaching-artist residencies - and it is the pipeline metric of audience development: decades of evidence link childhood arts exposure to adult attendance, so education reach today is the audience of decades ahead. The measurement honesty issue is depth versus count: a one-hour assembly and a semester-long residency both count as reach, so the metric needs dosage weighting. The funder economics matter too: education programs are frequently the most subsidized activity, making cost-per-participant the companion. Read with reach by school and grade band, repeat-participation rate (multi-year residencies matter more than one-offs), conversion evidence (student participants becoming family attenders), and educator-partnership depth.
