## 2. Definition

Channel Coverage of Target Markets (CCTM) measures growth over time in the Channel & Distribution Management domain. It is computed as Markets with Active Channel Presence / Target Markets, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether partner capacity covers priority segments, geographies, and verticals. Coverage maps partner supply onto demand strategy. Coverage by headcount rather than by active certified capacity is nominal. Read with ecosystem coverage ratio and partner enablement completion rate.

## Formula

```
Markets with Active Channel Presence / Target Markets
```
