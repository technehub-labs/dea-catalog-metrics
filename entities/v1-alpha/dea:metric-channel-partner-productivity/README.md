## 2. Definition

A Channel & Distribution Management indicator, Channel Partner Productivity (CPP-CD) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Revenue per Active Partner - reported in currency per unit. It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue generated per channel partner (or per partner tier). Productivity separates partners that build the business from those that consume program cost. Productivity computed on registered revenue rather than closed business inflates. Read with partner-initiated pipeline ratio and channel program ROI.

## Formula

Revenue per Active Partner
