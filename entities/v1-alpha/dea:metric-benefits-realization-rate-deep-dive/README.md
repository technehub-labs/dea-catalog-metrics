## 2. Definition

Within Project Management (Deep Dive), Benefits Realization Rate (Deep Dive) (BRR-D) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Realized Benefits / Business Case Benefits - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of promised business benefits actually delivered post-implementation. The point of projects is benefits, not go-lives; most organizations never verify. Benefits defined vaguely (efficiency) are unverifiable by design. Read with portfolio value delivery rate and benefits tracking coverage.

## Formula

```
BRR-D = Realized Benefits / Business Case Benefits
```
