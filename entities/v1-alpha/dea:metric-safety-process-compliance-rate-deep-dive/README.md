## 2. Definition

Safety Process Compliance Rate (Deep Dive) (SPCR-D) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Balanced Scorecard - Internal Process Perspective domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Safety Process Audits Passed / Audits - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to defined safety processes and controls. Safety process lapses are the leading indicators of injury and liability. Compliance reported by the same unit being audited tends toward optimism. Read with safety incident rate and safety audit compliance rate.

## Formula

```
Safety Process Audits Passed / Audits
```
