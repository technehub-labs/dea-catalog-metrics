## 2. Definition

Within Sustainability & ESG, Volunteering Hours per Employee (VHPE) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Volunteering Hours / Average Headcount, expressed in hours. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures average employee volunteering hours contributed. It matters as a visible signal of community investment and a driver of engagement and employer brand. Gaming risk includes counting company-mandated events or counting hours without verification. Read with Employee Engagement Score and CSR program outcome metrics to confirm volunteering is authentic rather than performative.

## Formula

```
Volunteering Hours / Average Headcount
```
