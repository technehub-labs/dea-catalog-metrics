## 2. Definition

Tailings Dam Inspection Compliance (TDIC) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Metals & Mining Operations (Deep Dive) domain. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Inspections per SMP Schedule / Required; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Metals & Mining, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence to scheduled dam safety inspections, instrumentation monitoring, and independent review for tailings storage facilities. Tailings failures are catastrophic-impact events, and post-Brumadinho industry standards treat inspection discipline as the minimum bar of legitimacy. Compliance must include closure of instrumentation anomalies, not just inspection events. Read with dam safety review findings and water management balance.

## Formula

```
Inspections per SMP Schedule / Required
```
