## 2. Definition

Within PMO & Portfolio Governance, Funding Reallocation Velocity (FRV) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Days from Decision to Reallocate Portfolio Funds - reported in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly capital and people shift from underperforming initiatives to better ones. Velocity is the practical test of whether portfolio management is real. High velocity from churn rather than evidence is different dysfunction. Read with portfolio rebalancing frequency and initiative kill decision timeliness.

## Formula

Days from Decision to Reallocate Portfolio Funds
