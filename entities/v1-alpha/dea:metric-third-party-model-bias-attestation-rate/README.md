## 2. Definition

Within AI Ethics, Fairness & Responsible AI, Third-Party Model Bias Attestation Rate (TMBAR) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Vendor Models with Bias Attestation / Vendor Models; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of external models or AI vendors providing credible bias testing attestations. Third-party models embed their training data's biases into your decisions, and attestation coverage transfers that diligence burden contractually. Attestations must be evidence-backed and current, since a vendor's dated self-assessment is liability transfer in disguise. Read with third-party AI risk assessment coverage (Deep Dive) and contractual indemnification terms.

## Formula

```
Vendor Models with Bias Attestation / Vendor Models
```
