## 2. Definition

A Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting indicator, Restatement Rate (ESG Disclosures) (RRESD) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Restated Disclosures / Disclosures, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the frequency of corrections to previously published ESG figures. Restatements are the credibility metric of the reporting regime: since ESG data carries few of finance's control disciplines, restatement rates reveal whether the process is measurement or messaging. Distinguish methodology-driven from error-driven restatements, which the market judges differently. Read with ESG data completeness trends and assurance findings.

## Formula

```
Restated Disclosures / Disclosures
```
