## 2. Definition

Digital Claims FNOL Adoption (DCFA-I) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of insurance distribution - direct & digital reporting. It is computed as Digital First Notifications / Total FNOLs, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Insurance.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of first notices of loss submitted through digital channels. FNOL is the highest-emotion, highest-cost manual touchpoint in claims, and digital adoption improves both customer control and data quality at intake. Adoption achieved by hiding phone numbers is deflection that damages vulnerable or complex claimants, so monitor assisted paths alongside. Read with claim decision automation rate and claims cycle time for where intake quality lands.

## Formula

```
Digital First Notifications / Total FNOLs
```
