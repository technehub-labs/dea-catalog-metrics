## 2. Definition

A Metals & Mining Operations (Deep Dive) indicator, Water Recirculation Rate (Deep Dive) (WRR-M) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Recirculated Water / Total Water Used, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Metals & Mining.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of process water reused rather than withdrawn fresh. In water-stressed mining jurisdictions, recirculation is both licence-to-operate and cost lever, and disclosure expectations are hardening fast. High recirculation with contaminated discharge still fails the environmental test, so read discharge quality alongside. Read with water balance reporting and community water risk exposure.

## Formula

```
WRR-M = Recirculated Water / Total Water Used
```
