## 2. Definition

Wholesale Broker Submission Win Rate (WBSW) measures growth over time in the Insurance Distribution & Agency Management domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Deals Bound / Submissions to Wholesale Market - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector.

## Formula

```
WBSW = Deals Bound / Submissions to Wholesale Market
```
