## 2. Definition

Social Engagement Rate (SER) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of marketing reporting. It is computed as Interactions / Followers or Impressions, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Interactions / Followers or Impressions
```
