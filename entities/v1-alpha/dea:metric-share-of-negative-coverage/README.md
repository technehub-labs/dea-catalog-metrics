## 2. Definition

Share of Negative Coverage (SNC) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of corporate communications & pr reporting. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Negative Articles / Total Articles Monitored - reported in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of media mentions that are negative. It matters because tone, not volume, is what shapes reputation perception. Sentiment classification can be tuned leniently so borderline critical coverage scores neutral. Read with Promoter Score trends and crisis incident history to interpret coverage share in context.

## Formula

```
Negative Articles / Total Articles Monitored
```
