## 2. Definition

Member Visits per Month (Deep Dive) (MVM-D) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking fitness & wellness (deep dive). It is defined as Total Check-ins / Active Members; the result is stated in times per period. In Health & Fitness, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures average attendance per member. Visit frequency is the strongest behavioral predictor of retention - members who attend four or more times monthly rarely cancel - and it is the honest measure of engagement versus mere membership. New-member visit patterns in the first 90 days predict their renewal with striking reliability. Read with member retention rate (Deep Dive) and program or class participation.

## Formula

```
MVM-D = Total Check-ins / Active Members
```
