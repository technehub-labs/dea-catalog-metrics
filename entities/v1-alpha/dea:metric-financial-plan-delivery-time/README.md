## 2. Definition

Within Asset & Wealth Management (Deep Dive), Financial Plan Delivery Time (FPDT) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its calculation is Days from Data Gathering to Plan Presentation, measured in days. It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
FPDT = Days from Data Gathering to Plan Presentation
```
