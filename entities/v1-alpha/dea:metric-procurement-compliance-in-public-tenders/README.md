## 2. Definition

Procurement Compliance in Public Tenders (PCPT) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Public Sector & Government domain. It is computed as Tenders Meeting Procedure Requirements / Tenders Conducted, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Public sector.

## 3. Context

The share of public procurement executed in compliance with the tendering regime - the integrity metric of public purchasing, whose strategic significance is the corruption-and-value exposure it guards: public procurement is the largest single corruption-surface in most governments, and compliance with the competitive process is the structural control that the oversight institutions (audit bodies, integrity commissions) are built to audit. The metric's weight rose with the open-contracting movement: publication of tender terms, awards, and amendments is what makes the compliance rate externally verifiable rather than self-reported, and the compliance-versus-transparency pairing is how modern regimes measure it. The honest read is by exception type: sole-source justifications, emergency-procedure use, and post-award amendment patterns are the classic back doors, and compliance measured only on the main procedure while exceptions grow is discipline at the front door and leakage at the side ones.

## Formula

```
PCPT = Tenders Meeting Procedure Requirements / Tenders Conducted
```
