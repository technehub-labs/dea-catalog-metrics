## 2. Definition

Online Reputation Score (Hotels) (ORS-H) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking hotel & resort operations (deep dive). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Weighted Average of Review Platform Scores, expressed in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Hospitality.

## 3. Context

Measures review-platform performance - ratings, volume, response rates, sentiment - across the property's digital footprint. Online reputation is the demand-side reality of a hotel: rankings gate visibility on booking platforms, and response management is a controllable lever. Score weighting across platforms matters, since volume on one platform dominates discovery. Read with guest satisfaction index (Deep Dive) and booking conversion trends.

## Formula

```
Weighted Average of Review Platform Scores
```
