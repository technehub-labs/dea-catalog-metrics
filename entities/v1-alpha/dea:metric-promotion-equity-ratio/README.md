## 2. Definition

Promotion Equity Ratio (PER-DEI) measures capability maturity or development in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Promotion Rates of Underrepresented vs Overall Population, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether promotion rates are equitable across demographic groups. Promotion is the internal pipeline's chokepoint: hiring diversity that stalls at promotion becomes attrition. Ratios on small samples swing wildly by period. Read with leadership diversity ratio and attrition equity gap.

## Formula

```
Promotion Rates of Underrepresented vs Overall Population
```
