## 2. Definition

Within Food & Beverage Manufacturing, Recall Readiness Drill Performance (RRDP) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. It is computed as Mock Recall Time and Traceability Completeness Score, expressed in score (points). Its use is concentrated in Food & Beverage. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures execution quality of simulated recalls - traceability retrieval speed, contact lists, regulatory notification, stock recovery. In food and beverage, recall performance is rehearsed or it is not possessed, and drills expose the gaps (lot traceability breaks, distribution records) that real events weaponize. Score against time thresholds, and rotate unannounced scenarios. Read with traceability coverage and mock-recall retrieval completeness.
