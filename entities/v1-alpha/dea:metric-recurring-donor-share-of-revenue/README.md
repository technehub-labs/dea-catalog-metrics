## 2. Definition

Recurring Donor Share of Revenue (RDSR-N) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Nonprofit & Donor Operations (Deep Dive) domain. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Recurring Gift Revenue / Total Donations, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Nonprofit practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue from recurring committed giving (monthly giving, memberships, sustainer programs). It is the revenue-quality metric of nonprofit sustainability: recurring revenue stabilizes budgeting, reduces re-acquisition dependence, and compounds donor lifetime value. Recurring programs built on small monthly donors carry high payment-failure attrition, so active card-update management protects the share. Read with donor retention rate (Deep Dive) and upgrade-path performance.

## Formula

```
Recurring Gift Revenue / Total Donations
```
