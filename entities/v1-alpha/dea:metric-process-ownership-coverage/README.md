## 2. Definition

Process Ownership Coverage (POC-OM) addresses governance and is a fixture of operating model monitoring & design effectiveness reporting. It is computed as Processes with Named Accountable Owner / Processes, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of end-to-end processes with a named, accountable owner. Without owners, processes improve only by accident and degrade by default. Ownership without decision rights over the metrics is sponsorship, not ownership. Read with end-to-end service accountability coverage and process compliance score.

## Formula

```
Processes with Named Accountable Owner / Processes
```
