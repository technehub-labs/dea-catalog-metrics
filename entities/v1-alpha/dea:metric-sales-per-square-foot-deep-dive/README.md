## 2. Definition

Sales per Square Foot (Deep Dive) (SPSFDD) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of retail standard measures reporting. The formula is Net Sales / Selling Area - reported in currency per unit. It is a standard measure in the Retail & E-commerce sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue productivity of selling space. It is the classic retail space economics measure. Productivity maintained by shrinking profitable service space trades long-term experience for short-term optics. Read with inventory turnover (retail) and sales per labor hour.

## Formula

```
Net Sales / Selling Area
```
