## 2. Definition

Insurance Claim Acceptance Rate (Vet) (ICAR) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Veterinary & Animal Health domain. The formula is Claims Accepted by Pet Insurers / Claims Submitted - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Claims Accepted by Pet Insurers / Claims Submitted
```
