## 2. Definition

Role Clarity Score (RCS-OM) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Operating Model & Process Health Deep Dive domain. It is defined as Employee Survey on Role and Accountability Clarity; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether employees understand their responsibilities, boundaries, and success criteria. Role ambiguity is a root cause of both duplicated effort and dropped work. Clarity measured against formal job descriptions misses the informal reality. Read with decision rights clarity index and employee engagement score (Deep Dive).
