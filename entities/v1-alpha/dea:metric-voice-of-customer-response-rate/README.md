## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective, Voice of Customer Response Rate (VOCRR) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Survey Responses / Requests Sent; the result is stated in percent (%). It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Survey Responses / Requests Sent
```
