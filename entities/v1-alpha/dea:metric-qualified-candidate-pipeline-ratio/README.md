## 2. Definition

Qualified Candidate Pipeline Ratio (QCPR) addresses the utilization or availability of capacity and is a fixture of talent acquisition deep dive reporting. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Qualified Candidates per Open Requisition, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Qualified Candidates per Open Requisition
```
