## 2. Definition

Referral Rate (Wealth) (REFW) captures growth over time for organizations tracking wealth management. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is New Clients from Referrals / New Clients, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Wealth management practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of clients providing referrals to advisors. It matters because referrals are the dominant growth engine in trust-based wealth management. Requests at renewal moments can inflate transactional referrals without advocacy. Read with Client Satisfaction and Net New Assets from referred clients to confirm referral quality.

## Formula

```
New Clients from Referrals / New Clients
```
