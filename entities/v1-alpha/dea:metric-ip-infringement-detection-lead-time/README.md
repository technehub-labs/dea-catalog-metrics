## 2. Definition

IP Infringement Detection Lead Time (IIDLT) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of intellectual property & licensing reporting. Its calculation is Days from Third-Party Infringement to Detection, measured in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

Days from Third-Party Infringement to Detection
