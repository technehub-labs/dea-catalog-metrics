## 2. Definition

Within Food & Beverage Manufacturing, Food Safety Audit Score (GFSI) (FSAS) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is GFSI-Benchmarked Audit Score - reported in score (points). It is a standard measure in the Food & Beverage sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures performance in third-party food safety certification audits under GFSI-benchmarked schemes. Certification gates market access for most retailers and manufacturers, and audit scores are the industry's shared language of safety credibility. Audit-day readiness can diverge from daily practice, so supplement with internal audit trends and complaint data. Read with HACCP compliance rate and corrective action closure aging.
