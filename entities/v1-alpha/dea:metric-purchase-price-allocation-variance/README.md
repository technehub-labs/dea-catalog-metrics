## 2. Definition

Purchase Price Allocation Variance (PPAV) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking mergers & acquisitions. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Final PPA vs Preliminary Estimate Variance - reported in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Final PPA vs Preliminary Estimate Variance
```
