## 2. Definition

A Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety Standard Measures indicator, Hand Hygiene Compliance (HHC) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Observed Hand Hygiene Opportunities Performed / Opportunities - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
HHC = Observed Hand Hygiene Opportunities Performed / Opportunities
```
