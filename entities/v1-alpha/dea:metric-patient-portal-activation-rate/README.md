## 2. Definition

Within Medical Practice & Clinical Operations, Patient Portal Activation Rate (PPAR) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its calculation is Patients Activated in Portal / Eligible Patients, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Healthcare practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Patients Activated in Portal / Eligible Patients
```
