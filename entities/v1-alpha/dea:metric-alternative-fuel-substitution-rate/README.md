## 2. Definition

Within Cement & Building Materials, Alternative Fuel Substitution Rate (AFSR) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Alternative Fuel Heat / Total Fuel Heat; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Manufacturing, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of kiln thermal energy from alternative fuels - waste-derived biomass, tires, solvents. Substitution cuts both fuel cost and net emissions (with biogenic accounting), and it sits at the center of cement decarbonization roadmaps. Substitution claims must be read with permit limits and clinker quality effects, since high substitution can degrade product strength. Read with thermal energy per tonne clinker and emissions intensity.

## Formula

```
Alternative Fuel Heat / Total Fuel Heat
```
