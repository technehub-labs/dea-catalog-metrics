## 2. Definition

Community Grievance Resolution Time (CGRT) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking mining & metals. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It is defined as Days from Grievance Filed to Resolution; the result is stated in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. In Mining & metals, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures average time to resolve community complaints around operations. It matters because slow grievance handling escalates into license-to-operate risk and protests. Teams can close grievances on administrative acceptance while community concerns persist. Read with Grievance Recurrence Rate and social license survey results to confirm resolution is community-felt.

## Formula

```
Days from Grievance Filed to Resolution
```
