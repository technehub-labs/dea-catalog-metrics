## 2. Definition

Leadership Pipeline Strength Index (LPSI-BSC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective reporting. It is computed as Ready-Now Leaders per Critical Leadership Role, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the depth and readiness of internal candidates for leadership positions. A strong internal pipeline lowers hiring risk and signals a healthy development culture. Indexes built on HR nominations rather than assessment data favor visibility over capability. Read with succession plan coverage and internal mobility rate.

## Formula

```
Ready-Now Leaders per Critical Leadership Role
```
