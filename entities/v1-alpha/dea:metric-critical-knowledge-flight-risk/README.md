## 2. Definition

Critical Knowledge Flight Risk (CKFR) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Knowledge Management (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Sole-Source Experts Nearing Departure Risk / Sole-Source Expertise; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the concentration of critical, undocumentable knowledge in individuals likely to leave. Flight risk converts succession planning into an operational urgency. Risk scoring from manager intuition without indicators is guessing. Read with key person dependency risk and critical knowledge capture rate.

## Formula

```
Sole-Source Experts Nearing Departure Risk / Sole-Source Expertise
```
