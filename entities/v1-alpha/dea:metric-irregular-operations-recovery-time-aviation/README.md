## 2. Definition

Irregular Operations Recovery Time (Aviation) (IROPSRT) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Airline & Transportation Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. The formula is Time to Restore Schedule after Disruption - reported in hours. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It is a standard measure in the Airlines & Aviation sector.

## Formula

```
Time to Restore Schedule after Disruption
```
