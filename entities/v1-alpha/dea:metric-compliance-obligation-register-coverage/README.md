## 2. Definition

Within Compliance Monitoring & Testing, Compliance Obligation Register Coverage (CORC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Legal Obligations Mapped to Controls / Identified Obligations; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely applicable regulatory obligations are captured in a governed register. Unregistered obligations are unmanaged obligations, discovered only at enforcement. Coverage is inherently perishable as regulation changes. Read with regulatory change adoption timeliness and policy lifecycle currency rate.

## Formula

```
Legal Obligations Mapped to Controls / Identified Obligations
```
