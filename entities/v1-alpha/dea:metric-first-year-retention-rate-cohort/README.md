## 2. Definition

First-Year Retention Rate (Cohort) (FYRR-ED) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking education & training outcome standard measures. It is defined as Students Enrolled Year 2 / Entering Cohort; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Education, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of an entering cohort still enrolled after year one. First-year attrition is where most student loss concentrates. Retention achieved by grade inflation degrades graduation integrity downstream. Read with graduation rate (Deep Dive) and at-risk student intervention rate.

## Formula

```
Students Enrolled Year 2 / Entering Cohort
```
