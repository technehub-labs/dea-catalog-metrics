## 2. Definition

Within Channel & Distribution Management, Channel Training Coverage (CTC-CD) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Partner Staff Completing Required Training / Partner Staff, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Partner Staff Completing Required Training / Partner Staff
```
