## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective, Internal Mobility Rate (Deep Dive) (IMR-D) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. The formula is Roles Filled Internally / Total Roles Filled - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of vacancies filled internally. Mobility retains institutional knowledge and signals genuine career opportunity. Excessive internal recycling can starve external idea inflow. Read with leadership pipeline strength index and critical talent retention rate.

## Formula

```
Roles Filled Internally / Total Roles Filled
```
