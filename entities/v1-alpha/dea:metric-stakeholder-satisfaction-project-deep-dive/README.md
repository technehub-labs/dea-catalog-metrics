## 2. Definition

Stakeholder Satisfaction - Project (Deep Dive) (SSP-D) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking project management (deep dive). Its calculation is Stakeholder Survey Score per Stage, measured in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures sponsor and key stakeholder satisfaction with project delivery. Sponsor trust determines access to decisions and continued funding. Survey timing near milestones (rather than conflicts) biases scores upward. Read with benefits realization rate and project governance review compliance.
