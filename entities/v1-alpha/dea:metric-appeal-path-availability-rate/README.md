## 2. Definition

Appeal Path Availability Rate (APAR-AI) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of ai ethics, fairness & responsible ai reporting. The formula is AI-Impacted Decisions with Appeal Mechanism / Decisions - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of automated decisions offering an accessible appeal or review route. Appeal availability is the procedural-fairness floor: individuals affected by algorithmic decisions need a contest mechanism, and its absence converts errors into grievances with no legitimate outlet. Availability must be tested from the affected person's view, since appeal rights buried in process count on paper only. Read with appeal outcomes - high overturn rates reveal upstream decision quality problems.

## Formula

```
AI-Impacted Decisions with Appeal Mechanism / Decisions
```
