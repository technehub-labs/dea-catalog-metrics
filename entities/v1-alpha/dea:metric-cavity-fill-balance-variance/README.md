## 2. Definition

Within Packaging & Plastics, Cavity Fill Balance Variance (CFBV) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Part Weight Variation across Cavities; the result is stated in percent (%). In Manufacturing, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

CFBV = Part Weight Variation across Cavities
