## 2. Definition

Curriculum Review Currency (Deep Dive) (CRC-D) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Education & Training (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Courses Reviewed within Cycle / Courses, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Education practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether program content has been reviewed within policy cycles against industry and accreditation standards. Currency is the quiet driver of education relevance: unrevised curricula teach last decade's tools, and employers read graduate quality accordingly. Reviews that document no changes should be audited as candidly as those proposing many. Read with employment outcome rate (Deep Dive) and instructor industry-engagement evidence.

## Formula

```
Courses Reviewed within Cycle / Courses
```
