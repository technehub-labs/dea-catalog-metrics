## 2. Definition

A Strategic Sourcing & Supplier Performance (Deep Dive) indicator, Sourcing Cycle Time (RFx to Award) (SCTRF) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Days from RFx Launch to Contract Award, expressed in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
