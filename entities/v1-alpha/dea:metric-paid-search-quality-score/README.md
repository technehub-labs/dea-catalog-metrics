## 2. Definition

Paid Search Quality Score (QS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking marketing. Its calculation is Average Ad Relevance and Landing Page Quality Rating, measured in score (points). It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Average Ad Relevance and Landing Page Quality Rating
```
