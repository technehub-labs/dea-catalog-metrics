## 2. Definition

Accreditation Compliance Rate (Deep Dive) (ACR-D) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of education & training (deep dive) reporting. It is defined as Standards Met at Audit / Standards Audited; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Education, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures conformance with accreditor standards across programs. Accreditation is the license-to-award of education: losing it ends credential recognition and funding eligibility, so compliance is existential, not administrative. Self-study honesty determines the real rate, since accreditors sample, and prepared-for-inspection conditions diverge from daily practice. Read with findings-remediation aging and standards-change readiness.

## Formula

```
Standards Met at Audit / Standards Audited
```
