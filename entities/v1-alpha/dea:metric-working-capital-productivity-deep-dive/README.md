## 2. Definition

Working Capital Productivity (Deep Dive) (WCP-D) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of balanced scorecard - financial perspective reporting. It is computed as Revenue / Working Capital, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue generated per unit of working capital tied up. Better productivity funds growth without new capital, compounding returns. Squeezing suppliers and inventory beyond safe levels converts productivity into disruption risk. Read with cash conversion cycle and free cash flow margin.

## Formula

```
Revenue / Working Capital
```
