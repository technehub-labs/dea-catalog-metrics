## 2. Definition

Certification Attainment Rate (L&D) (CART-LD) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of learning & development effectiveness (deep dive) reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Employees Achieving Target Certifications / Planned, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures completion of role-required certifications against plan. Certifications are the verifiable slice of capability building. Attainment without expiration tracking counts dead certifications. Read with skills certification coverage - Field (Deep Dive) and compliance training completion rate.

## Formula

```
CART-LD = Employees Achieving Target Certifications / Planned
```
