## 2. Definition

A Startup & Venture-Backed Business indicator, Dilution per Funding Round (DPRF) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is computed as Founders' Ownership Change per Round, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the ownership percentage ceded per financing. It is the founder-and-employee equity economics metric: repeated high-dilution rounds can leave a successful company's early team undercompensated, and dilution discipline (balanced against growth capital needs) shapes decision rights as much as economics. Clean-term dilution differs from liquidation-preference effective dilution, so read both. Read with the cap table's fully-diluted evolution and exit waterfall modeling.
