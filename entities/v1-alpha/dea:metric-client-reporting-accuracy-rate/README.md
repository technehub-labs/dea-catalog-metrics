## 2. Definition

Client Reporting Accuracy Rate (CRAR-IM) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking investment management - portfolio operations. It is defined as Reports without Restatement / Reports; the result is stated in percent (%). In Financial Services, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of client statements and reports issued without error. Reporting errors destroy client trust faster than performance shortfalls, because they signal operational weakness. Accuracy excluding late-found errors understates the true experience. Read with NAV publication timeliness and client onboarding cycle time.

## Formula

```
Reports without Restatement / Reports
```
