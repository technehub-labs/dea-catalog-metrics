## 2. Definition

Strategic Reallocation Speed (SRS) measures capability maturity or development in the Operating Model & Decision Effectiveness domain. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Months to Shift Budget beyond Threshold between Units, expressed in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how quickly capital, people, and management attention move from declining to rising opportunities - the practical test of whether portfolio strategy is real. Most organizations reallocate a small fraction of resources year to year, which is why entrants outrun incumbents in shifting markets. Honest measurement needs baselines and portfolio-level attribution, which are easy to game by relabeling existing spend as new investment. Read with capital allocation by growth area and legacy cost run-rate reduction.

Months to Shift Budget beyond Threshold between Units
