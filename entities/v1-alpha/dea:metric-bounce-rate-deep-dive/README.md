## 2. Definition

Bounce Rate (Deep Dive) (BRDD) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of web & digital analytics standard measures reporting. Its calculation is Single-Page Sessions / Total Sessions, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Digital product practice. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Single-Page Sessions / Total Sessions
```
