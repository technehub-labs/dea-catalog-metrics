## 2. Definition

Mitigation Optionality Score (MOS-ER) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning domain. The formula is Rubric of Prepared Mitigation Options per Emerging Risk - reported in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how many alternative response paths exist for major emerging risks. Optionality is the practical form of agility: one path is a plan, zero is a hope. Scores counting options never exercised or funded are theoretical. Read with emerging risk mitigation lead time and strategic resilience buffer index.

## Formula

```
Rubric of Prepared Mitigation Options per Emerging Risk
```
