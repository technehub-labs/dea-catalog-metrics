## 2. Definition

Within Cleaning & Environmental Services, Cleaning Audit Pass Rate (CAPR-C) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Inspections Meeting Standard / Inspections - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Facility Services sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
CAPR-C = Inspections Meeting Standard / Inspections
```
