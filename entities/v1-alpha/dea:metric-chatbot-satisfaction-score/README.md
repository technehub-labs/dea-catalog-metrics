## 2. Definition

Chatbot Satisfaction Score (CSS-CB) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Conversational AI & Chatbots domain. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Average Post-Conversation Rating - reported in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.
