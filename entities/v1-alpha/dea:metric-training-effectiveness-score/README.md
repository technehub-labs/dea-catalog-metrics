## 2. Definition

Training Effectiveness Score (TES) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of learning & development reporting. It is computed as Post-Training Assessment Score Improvement, expressed in score (points). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether training changed knowledge, behavior, or results, not just attendance. It matters because effectiveness is the only defensible justification for L&D spend. Self-reported scores can be socially inflated, and attributing broad performance gains to single courses overstates impact. Read with Level 3 behavior observation and Time to Productivity to confirm transfer beyond the classroom.
