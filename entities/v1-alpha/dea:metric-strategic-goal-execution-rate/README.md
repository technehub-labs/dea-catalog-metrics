## 2. Definition

Strategic Goal Execution Rate (SGER) measures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome in the Enterprise OKR & Performance Management domain. Its calculation is Strategic Goals Achieved on Time / Goals, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of strategic goals achieved within their horizon. It is the bottom line of the goal-management system. Goals defined vaguely enough to always be 'achieved' hollow the metric. Read with key result attainment average and portfolio value delivery rate.

## Formula

```
SGER = Strategic Goals Achieved on Time / Goals
```
