## 2. Definition

Operating Model Review Cadence Compliance (OMRCC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of operating model monitoring & design effectiveness reporting. It is defined as Scheduled Operating Model Reviews Held / Scheduled; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether operating-model reviews occur on the defined schedule with the right participants. Cadence is what turns operating-model design from a project into management practice. Compliance without decision authority in the room is ritual. Read with operating model KPI coverage ratio and decision quality audit score.

## Formula

```
Scheduled Operating Model Reviews Held / Scheduled
```
