## 2. Definition

Critical Vendor Assessment Coverage (CVAC-TP) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking third-party risk management. The formula is Critical Vendors with Current Risk Assessment / Critical Vendors - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether vendors designated critical receive the depth of assessment their criticality warrants. The critical few concentrate the risk; coverage should be near-total there. Criticality designations set once and never revisited decay. Read with critical vendor management coverage and due diligence depth compliance.

## Formula

```
Critical Vendors with Current Risk Assessment / Critical Vendors
```
