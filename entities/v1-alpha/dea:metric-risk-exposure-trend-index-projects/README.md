## 2. Definition

Risk Exposure Trend Index (Projects) (RETI) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of project risk & issue management reporting. It is defined as Period-over-Period Change in Aggregate Risk Exposure; the result is stated in index (0-100 or normalized). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether aggregate project risk exposure is rising or falling. Rising exposure with stable status reporting indicates reporting culture problems. Index construction should stay consistent to preserve trend meaning. Read with risk exposure trend index and project portfolio health distribution.

## Formula

```
Period-over-Period Change in Aggregate Risk Exposure
```
