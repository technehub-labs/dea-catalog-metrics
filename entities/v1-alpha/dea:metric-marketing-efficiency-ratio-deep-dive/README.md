## 2. Definition

Within Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive), Marketing Efficiency Ratio (Deep Dive) (MERDD) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Total Revenue / Total Marketing Spend - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures total marketing cost relative to new revenue generated. It is the blended sanity check on the whole spend envelope. Blended ratios hide efficient and wasteful channels behind one average. Read with return on advertising spend (Deep Dive) and blended ROAS by acquisition stage.

## Formula

```
MERDD = Total Revenue / Total Marketing Spend
```
