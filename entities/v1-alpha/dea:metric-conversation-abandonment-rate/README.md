## 2. Definition

Within Conversational AI & Chatbots, Conversation Abandonment Rate (CAR-CB) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Conversations Abandoned before Resolution / Conversations, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Conversations Abandoned before Resolution / Conversations
```
