## 2. Definition

A Food & Beverage Manufacturing indicator, Kilo of Product per Labor Hour (KPPLH) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Output Kilograms / Labor Hours, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Food & Beverage.

## Formula

```
KPPLH = Output Kilograms / Labor Hours
```
