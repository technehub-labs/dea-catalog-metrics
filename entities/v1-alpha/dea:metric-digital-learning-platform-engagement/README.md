## 2. Definition

Digital Learning Platform Engagement (DLPE) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking education & training outcome standard measures. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Active Learners on Platform / Enrolled Students, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Education.

## Formula

```
Active Learners on Platform / Enrolled Students
```
