## 2. Definition

Within PMO & Portfolio Governance, Benefits Tracking Coverage (PMO) (BTC-P) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Initiatives with Tracked Benefits / Initiatives Closed; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of PMO-governed initiatives with owned, measurable benefit tracks. Tracking coverage is the minimum condition for benefits accountability. Coverage counting initiative-level claims rather than finance-verified benefits is soft. Read with benefits tracking coverage and benefits realization rate.

## Formula

```
Initiatives with Tracked Benefits / Initiatives Closed
```
