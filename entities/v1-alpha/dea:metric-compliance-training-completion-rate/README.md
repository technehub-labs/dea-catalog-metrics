## 2. Definition

Compliance Training Completion Rate (CTCR-CM) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking compliance monitoring & testing. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Staff Completing Mandatory Training by Due Date / Staff - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing Mandatory Training by Due Date / Staff
```
