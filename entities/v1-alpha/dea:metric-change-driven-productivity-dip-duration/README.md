## 2. Definition

A Change Management indicator, Change-Driven Productivity Dip Duration (CPDD) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Days below Baseline Productivity after Change Go-Live; the result is stated in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures how long productivity stays depressed after major changes. It matters because long dips consume the business case of the change itself. Dips can be hidden by attributing losses to other initiatives. Read with Change Saturation Index and Benefit Realization timing to confirm recovery is genuine.

## Formula

```
Days below Baseline Productivity after Change Go-Live
```
