## 2. Definition

A Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety Standard Measures indicator, Left Without Being Seen Rate (LWBS) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is ED Patients Leaving before Clinical Evaluation / ED Arrivals - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Healthcare sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
LWBS = ED Patients Leaving before Clinical Evaluation / ED Arrivals
```
