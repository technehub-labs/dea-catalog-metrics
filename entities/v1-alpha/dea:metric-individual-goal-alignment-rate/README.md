## 2. Definition

Individual Goal Alignment Rate (IGAR-OKR) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of enterprise okr & performance management reporting. The formula is Individual Goals Mapped to Team OKRs / Goals - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Individual Goals Mapped to Team OKRs / Goals
```
