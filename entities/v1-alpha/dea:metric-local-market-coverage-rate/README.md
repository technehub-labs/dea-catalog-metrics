## 2. Definition

Within Advertising & Media Buying, Local Market Coverage Rate (LMCR) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its calculation is Designated Markets Covered per Plan / Planned Markets, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Designated Markets Covered per Plan / Planned Markets
```
