## 2. Definition

A Construction Contracting (Deep Dive) indicator, Claim and Dispute Recovery Rate (CDRR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Claims Recovered / Claims Filed, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Construction.

## 3. Context

Measures successful recovery of contract claims - variations, delays, disruptions - against claims raised. Construction margins routinely live in the claims process: contracts are priced competitively and economically recovered through change and disruption compensation, and recovery discipline is what separates profitable contractors from busy ones. Recovery through relationship-damaging escalation carries a long-tail cost on future work. Read with claim aging and backcharge realization against subcontractors.

## Formula

```
Claims Recovered / Claims Filed
```
