## 2. Definition

Contract Deviation Approval Cycle Time (CDAT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Legal Operations domain. The formula is Days to Approve Non-Standard Clauses - reported in days. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Days to Approve Non-Standard Clauses
```
