## 2. Definition

Ombudsman Case Overturn Rate (OCOR) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of government & public administration reporting. Its calculation is Decisions Overturned on Review / Decisions Reviewed, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Public Sector practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Decisions Overturned on Review / Decisions Reviewed
```
