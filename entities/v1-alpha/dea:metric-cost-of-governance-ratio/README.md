## 2. Definition

Cost of Governance Ratio (COGR-OM) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of operating model monitoring & design effectiveness reporting. The formula is Governance and Coordination Cost / Operating Cost - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures governance and oversight cost as a share of operating cost. Governance cost is real and should be evaluated like any other investment. Cutting visible committees while adding informal oversight relocates rather than reduces cost. Read with governance layer efficiency ratio and span and layer index.

## Formula

```
Governance and Coordination Cost / Operating Cost
```
