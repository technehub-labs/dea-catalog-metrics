## 2. Definition

Matter Intake Standardization Rate (MISR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking legal operations. It is computed as Matters via Standard Intake / Matters, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Matters via Standard Intake / Matters
```
