## 2. Definition

Compliance Training Completion Rate (Deep Dive) (CTCR-D) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Operations domain. It is defined as Staff Completing Mandatory Training by Deadline / Staff; the result is stated in percent (%). It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing Mandatory Training by Deadline / Staff
```
