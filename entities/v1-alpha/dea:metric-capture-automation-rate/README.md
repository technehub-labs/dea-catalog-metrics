## 2. Definition

A Records & Information Management indicator, Capture Automation Rate (CAR-RM) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Documents Auto-Classified on Ingest / Documents Ingested - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Documents Auto-Classified on Ingest / Documents Ingested
```
