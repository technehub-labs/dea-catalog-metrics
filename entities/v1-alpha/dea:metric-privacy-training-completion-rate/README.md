## 2. Definition

Privacy Training Completion Rate (PTCR) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking data privacy & protection. It is computed as Staff Completing Privacy Training by Due Date / Staff, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing Privacy Training by Due Date / Staff
```
