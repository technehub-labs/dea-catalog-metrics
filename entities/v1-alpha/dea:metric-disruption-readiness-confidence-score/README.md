## 2. Definition

Within Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning, Disruption Readiness Confidence Score (DRCS-ER) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Leadership Survey Confidence in Response Playbooks - reported in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures evidence-based confidence in surviving defined disruption scenarios. Confidence without tested capability is mood. Scores anchored in untested plans rather than rehearsals are nominal. Read with business continuity plan coverage (Deep Dive) and crisis simulation cadence compliance.

Leadership Survey Confidence in Response Playbooks
