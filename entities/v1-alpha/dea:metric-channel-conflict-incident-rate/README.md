## 2. Definition

A Channel & Distribution Management indicator, Channel Conflict Incident Rate (CCIR) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Formal Channel Conflict Cases per Period, measured in count (items per period). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Formal Channel Conflict Cases per Period
```
