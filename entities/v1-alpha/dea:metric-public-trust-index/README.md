## 2. Definition

A Government & Public Administration indicator, Public Trust Index (PTI) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. The formula is Composite Trust Survey Score - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is a standard measure in the Public Sector sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures citizen confidence in public institutions' competence, fairness, and honesty. It is the ultimate outcome metric of public administration, since trust predicts compliance, participation, and tax morale. Indices blend many drivers, so treat it as a strategic direction rather than a management dial, and watch for survey-mode effects across demographics. Read with citizen satisfaction index (Government) and integrity findings from audits.

Composite Trust Survey Score
