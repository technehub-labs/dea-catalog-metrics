## 2. Definition

Leading Indicator Share (LIS-BSC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of balanced scorecard implementation & review reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Leading Measures / Total BSC Measures; the result is stated in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of scorecard metrics that lead rather than lag outcomes. Lag-heavy scorecards report history; lead-heavy ones offer time to act. Leading indicators are noisier and need tolerance bands. Read with cause-effect linkage validation rate and management information freshness score.

## Formula

```
Leading Measures / Total BSC Measures
```
