## 2. Definition

Within Corporate Venturing & Partnerships, Venture Deal Flow Quality (VDFQ) is used to gauge how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Proposals Meeting Investment Thesis / Proposals Screened; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the caliber of startup investment opportunities a corporate venturing unit sees - stage, sector fit, founder quality, competitive tension for allocations. Weak deal flow predicts a weak portfolio regardless of selection skill, so it is the leading indicator of venturing health. Quality assessment is inherently subjective, so use structured scoring and track whether high-score deals actually outperform. Read with startup pilot conversion rate and venture portfolio return multiple.

## Formula

```
VDFQ = Proposals Meeting Investment Thesis / Proposals Screened
```
