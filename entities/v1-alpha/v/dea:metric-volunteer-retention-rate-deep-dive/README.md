## 2. Definition

Volunteer Retention Rate (Deep Dive) (VRR-D) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking nonprofit & donor operations (deep dive). Its calculation is Volunteers Returning / Prior Volunteers, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Nonprofit practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
VRR-D = Volunteers Returning / Prior Volunteers
```
