## 2. Definition

Ad Frequency (Average Exposures) (AFREQ) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Impressions / Unique Users Reached - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Impressions / Unique Users Reached
```
