## 2. Definition

Attributed Conversion Share (ACS) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Marketing Attribution & Analytics domain. Its calculation is Conversions with Attribution Confidence / Total Conversions, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Conversions with Attribution Confidence / Total Conversions
```
