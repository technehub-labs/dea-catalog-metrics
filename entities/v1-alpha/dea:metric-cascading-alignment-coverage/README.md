## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard Implementation & Review indicator, Cascading Alignment Coverage (CAC-BSC) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It is defined as Sub-Units with Cascaded Scorecards / Sub-Units; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how far the scorecard cascades into business unit and functional scorecards with coherent links. Cascading is what makes strategy everyone's job. Misaligned cascades (unit metrics contradicting enterprise objectives) are worse than none. Read with BSC objective coverage rate and OKR coverage - cascading ratio.

## Formula

```
Sub-Units with Cascaded Scorecards / Sub-Units
```
