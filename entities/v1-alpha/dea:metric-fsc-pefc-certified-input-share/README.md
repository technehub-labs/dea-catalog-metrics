## 2. Definition

FSC / PEFC Certified Input Share (FCIS) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Furniture & Wood Products domain. Its calculation is Certified Timber / Timber Consumed, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of wood inputs carrying chain-of-custody certification for furniture and wood products. It is the deforestation-risk and market-access metric for the industry, demanded by retail customers and increasingly by procurement regulation. Physical separation or strict volume-credit control makes the certification real; mixed handling without controls is paper compliance. Read with supplier audit outcomes and claims verification testing.

## Formula

```
Certified Timber / Timber Consumed
```
