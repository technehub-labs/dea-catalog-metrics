## 2. Definition

Within Government & Public Administration, Transparency Reporting Compliance (TRC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Disclosures Published on Schedule / Disclosures Required, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Public Sector practice.

## Formula

```
Disclosures Published on Schedule / Disclosures Required
```
