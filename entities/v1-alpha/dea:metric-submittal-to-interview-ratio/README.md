## 2. Definition

Submittal to Interview Ratio (SIR-S) measures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome in the Staffing & Recruiting domain. It is defined as Candidate Interviews / Candidates Submitted to Client; the result is stated in percent (%). In Staffing & recruiting, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. It is a working-level control: thresholds trigger immediate operational response. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Candidate Interviews / Candidates Submitted to Client
```
