## 2. Definition

A Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures indicator, Price Realization Rate (Market) (PRR-MKT) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Realized Price Change / List Price Change; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how much of list or target price is actually achieved in the market. Realization exposes discount discipline and price integrity. Realization maintained by shrinking the product (quantity cuts) is stealth discounting. Read with gross margin stability index and price elasticity of demand.

## Formula

```
Realized Price Change / List Price Change
```
