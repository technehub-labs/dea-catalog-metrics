## 2. Definition

Energy Intensity Index - Refining (EIIR) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of oil & gas downstream & refining reporting. It is defined as Actual Energy / Solomon or Benchmark Energy; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. In Oil & Gas, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures fuel and energy consumption normalized by throughput and complexity. Refineries are energy businesses in disguise - energy is both the largest cost and a major product input - and intensity index tracking (adjusted for crude slate and severity) separates operational discipline from mix effects. Index methodology consistency is the honest-read requirement. Read with refinery yield - clean products and emissions intensity trends.

## Formula

```
Actual Energy / Solomon or Benchmark Energy
```
