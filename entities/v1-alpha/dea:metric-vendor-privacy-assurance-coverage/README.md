## 2. Definition

Vendor Privacy Assurance Coverage (VPAC) measures exposure to adverse outcomes in the Data Privacy & Protection domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Vendors with DPAs and Assessments / Vendors Handling Personal Data - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of vendors handling personal data under verified privacy assurances. Personal data shared with vendors stays your accountability. Assurances collected as questionnaire signatures rather than verified controls are thin. Read with vendor privacy compliance rate and third-party data sharing compliance.

## Formula

```
Vendors with DPAs and Assessments / Vendors Handling Personal Data
```
