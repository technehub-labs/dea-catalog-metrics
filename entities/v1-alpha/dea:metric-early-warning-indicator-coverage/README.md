## 2. Definition

A Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning indicator, Early Warning Indicator Coverage (EWIC) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is computed as Emerging Risks with Defined Indicators / Emerging Risks, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely emerging risks have defined, monitored indicators with thresholds. Indicators convert horizon concerns into watchable signals. Indicators without owners and trigger actions are dashboards. Read with supply risk early warning coverage and leading indicator share.

## Formula

```
Emerging Risks with Defined Indicators / Emerging Risks
```
