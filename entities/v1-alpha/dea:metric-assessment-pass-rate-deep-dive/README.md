## 2. Definition

A Education & Training (Deep Dive) indicator, Assessment Pass Rate (Deep Dive) (APR-D) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Learners Passing Assessment / Assessed, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Education.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of assessed learners meeting competency standards. It is the learning-outcome proxy education systems rely on, and it is vulnerable to both directions of gaming: pass rates raised by lowering standards erode credential value, while harsh standards shift blame to learners. Item-level analysis (difficulty, discrimination) validates whether the assessment measures the intended competency. Read with course completion rate (Deep Dive) and external validation like licensure-exam performance.

## Formula

```
Learners Passing Assessment / Assessed
```
