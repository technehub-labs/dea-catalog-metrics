## 2. Definition

Scope 3 Coverage Ratio (S3CR) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking environmental, social & governance reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Categories Reported / Applicable Categories; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of value-chain emissions covered by measured or estimated data rather than proxies. Scope 3 dominates most companies' footprints, and coverage is the credibility gate: reported totals built on spend-based proxy factors are directional at best, and improving coverage is itself a material disclosure. Weight coverage by category materiality, since five well-measured categories beat twenty guessed ones. Read with supplier ESG assessment coverage (Deep Dive) and supplier engagement programs.

## Formula

```
S3CR = Categories Reported / Applicable Categories
```
