## 2. Definition

Within Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning, Post-Diction Accuracy Rate (PDAR) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Emerging Risks That Materialized and Were Previously Flagged / Materialized; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how well the risk function predicted, in advance, the risks that later materialized. Post-diction accuracy is the falsifiable test of risk-process quality. Accuracy scored by the process owners themselves inflates. Read with risk trend detection accuracy and blind spot review frequency.

## Formula

```
Emerging Risks That Materialized and Were Previously Flagged / Materialized
```
