## 2. Definition

GOPPAR - Gross Operating Profit per Available Room (GOPPAR) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking hotel & resort operations (deep dive). It is defined as Gross Operating Profit / Available Room Nights; the result is stated in currency per unit. In Hospitality, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

GOPPAR divides gross operating profit by available rooms, capturing what a property earns per unit of capacity after operating costs - the owner's per-room economic engine. Unlike RevPAR or TRevPAR it is margin-aware: two hotels with identical revenue can have very different GOPPAR depending on cost structure and department mix. Owners and lenders favor it precisely because it cannot be inflated by discount-driven occupancy, and asset managers benchmark it on the STR tradition's GOPPAR grids to judge both top-line and cost discipline together.

## Formula

```
Gross Operating Profit / Available Room Nights
```
