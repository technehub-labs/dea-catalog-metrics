## 2. Definition

Contract Compliance to Negotiated Terms (CCNT) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of strategic sourcing & supplier performance (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Invoices Matching Contract Price / Invoices Sampled - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Invoices Matching Contract Price / Invoices Sampled
```
