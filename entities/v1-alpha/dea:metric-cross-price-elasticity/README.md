## 2. Definition

Cross-Price Elasticity (CPE) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking market structure & economics standard measures. Its calculation is Percent Change in Quantity / Percent Change in Related Price, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## Formula

```
Percent Change in Quantity / Percent Change in Related Price
```
