## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective indicator, Customer Acquisition Cost Trend (Deep Dive) (CACT-D) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Change in CAC between Periods; the result is stated in currency value. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the direction of blended acquisition cost over time. CAC rising faster than customer value signals channel saturation or weakened differentiation. Blended CAC hides channel-level economics where paid efficiency decays first. Read with CAC payback period and LTV to CAC ratio.

## Formula

```
CACT-D = Change in CAC between Periods
```
