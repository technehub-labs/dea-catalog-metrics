## 2. Definition

Niche Role Search Success Rate (NRSSR) captures the utilization or availability of capacity for organizations tracking talent acquisition deep dive. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Hard-to-Fill Roles Closed within Target / Hard-to-Fill Roles, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how often hard-to-fill specialist roles are filled within target windows. Niche roles concentrate business risk: they stay open longest and cost most per vacancy. Success rates excluding roles redefined to be easier are inflated. Read with critical role vacancy duration and time-to-fill by role complexity.

## Formula

```
Hard-to-Fill Roles Closed within Target / Hard-to-Fill Roles
```
