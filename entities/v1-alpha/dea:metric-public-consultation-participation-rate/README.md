## 2. Definition

A Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Public Consultation Participation Rate (PCPR-PS) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Respondents to Consultation / Notified Population Sample - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Public sector sector.

## Formula

```
Respondents to Consultation / Notified Population Sample
```
