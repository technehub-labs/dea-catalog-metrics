## 2. Definition

Post-Implementation Benefit Confirmation (PIBC) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking change management. The formula is Initiatives with Confirmed Benefits at Review / Initiatives Closed - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether benefits promised in a change business case were independently verified after steady state, usually at six to twelve months. It closes the accountability loop most programs leave open: benefits are declared at go-live and never audited. Insist on finance-verified baselines and benefit attribution rules set before launch, or confirmation becomes negotiated storytelling. Read with adoption rate of change and the portfolio-level benefits realization trend.

## Formula

```
Initiatives with Confirmed Benefits at Review / Initiatives Closed
```
