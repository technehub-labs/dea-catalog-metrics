## 2. Definition

A Retail Standard Measures indicator, Same-Store Sales Growth (Deep Dive) (SSSGDD) quantifies growth over time. It is computed as Comparable Store Revenue Change Year-over-Year, expressed in percent (%). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Retail & E-commerce.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue growth of stores open in both comparison periods. It is the cleanest organic growth measure in retail. Store cohorts closed and quietly dropped from the base flatter the metric. Read with sales per square foot (Deep Dive) and foot traffic conversion rate.

Comparable Store Revenue Change Year-over-Year
