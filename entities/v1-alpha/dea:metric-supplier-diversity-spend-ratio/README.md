## 2. Definition

Supplier Diversity Spend Ratio (SDSR) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking diversity, equity & inclusion (deep dive). It is computed as Spend with Diverse Suppliers / Total Spend, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of procurement spend directed to certified diverse suppliers. Supplier diversity extends DEI commitments into economic impact. Tier-1 spend counted while diverse subcontracting goes unverified overstates impact. Read with addressable spend coverage and supplier compliance with diversity targets.

## Formula

```
SDSR = Spend with Diverse Suppliers / Total Spend
```
