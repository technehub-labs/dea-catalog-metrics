## 2. Definition

Retention Parity Ratio (RTP) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Diversity, Equity & Inclusion domain. It is computed as Retention Rate of Underrepresented Group / Retention Rate Overall, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Compares retention rates across demographic groups. Unequal retention is both a symptom of inequitable experience and an active driver of representation gaps, since every regretted exit resets pipeline progress. Control for role, level, tenure, and performance band, or the ratio conflates structural mix with differential treatment. Read with inclusion index and exit interview themes by group.

## Formula

```
Retention Rate of Underrepresented Group / Retention Rate Overall
```
