## 2. Definition

Promotional Reach Conversion (PRC) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of media & entertainment reporting. Its calculation is Trial Starts from Campaign / Campaign Reach, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Media & entertainment practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how effectively promotional reach converts into engagement or trial. It matters because media businesses pay for reach that only matters when it converts. Conversion can be inflated by counting giveaway entries as conversions. Read with Subscriber Conversion Rate and Marketing Campaign ROI to confirm reach converts to revenue-linked behavior.

## Formula

```
PRC = Trial Starts from Campaign / Campaign Reach
```
