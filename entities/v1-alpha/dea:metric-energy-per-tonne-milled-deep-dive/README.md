## 2. Definition

Energy per Tonne Milled (Deep Dive) (ETM-D) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Metals & Mining Operations (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Energy Consumed / Tonnes Milled, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Metals & Mining practice.

## Formula

```
Energy Consumed / Tonnes Milled
```
