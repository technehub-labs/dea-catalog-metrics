## 2. Definition

Within IT Financial Management & Technology Budgeting, Demand Intake to Funding Cycle Time (DITFCT) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Days from Demand Submission to Approved Funding; the result is stated in days. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures elapsed time from business demand submission to funded approval. Long intake cycles push business users to shadow solutions. Cycle time is gameable by pre-staging demands before formal intake. Read with strategic initiative decision latency and portfolio capacity utilization.

## Formula

```
DITFCT = Days from Demand Submission to Approved Funding
```
