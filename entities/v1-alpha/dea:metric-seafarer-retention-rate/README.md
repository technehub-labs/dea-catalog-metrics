## 2. Definition

Seafarer Retention Rate (SFR-M) measures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time in the Maritime & Shipping domain. Its calculation is Seafarers Completing Contracts and Returning / Seafarers Eligible, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Maritime & shipping practice.

## 3. Context

Measures crew continuity across contracts in shipping. It matters because experienced crews reduce incidents and training cost in a safety-critical industry. Retention can be propped up by contract structures that make leaving costly rather than attractive. Read with Crew Competency Assessment results and Safety Incident Rate to confirm retained crews are effective.

## Formula

```
Seafarers Completing Contracts and Returning / Seafarers Eligible
```
