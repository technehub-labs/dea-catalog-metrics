## 2. Definition

Within Pharmaceuticals & Life Sciences, Batch Yield (BAY) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Conforming Product Mass / Input Mass - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Pharmaceuticals sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Conforming Product Mass / Input Mass
```
