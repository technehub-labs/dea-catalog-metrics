## 2. Definition

Critical Talent Retention Rate (Deep Dive) (CTRR-D) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective. It is computed as Critical Role Holders Retained / Critical Role Holders, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures retention of employees identified as business-critical. Losing the vital few costs multiples of average attrition. Definitions of 'critical' set too broadly make the metric average attrition in disguise. Read with high performer retention rate and succession plan coverage.

## Formula

```
Critical Role Holders Retained / Critical Role Holders
```
