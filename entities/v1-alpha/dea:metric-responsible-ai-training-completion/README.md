## 2. Definition

A AI Ethics, Fairness & Responsible AI indicator, Responsible AI Training Completion (RAITC) quantifies capability maturity or development. It is defined as Staff Completing Responsible AI Training / Staff in Scope; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Staff Completing Responsible AI Training / Staff in Scope
```
