## 2. Definition

Soil Organic Matter Trend (SOMT) addresses environmental and social sustainability performance and is a fixture of agriculture & agronomy (deep dive) reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Change in Soil Organic Matter per Cycle; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Agriculture, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the multi-year trajectory of soil organic matter content. SOM is the slow-compounding capital account of farming: it drives water retention, nutrient cycling, and long-run yield resilience, and it takes years to build and seasons to destroy. Trend measurement demands consistent sampling depth and lab methodology, since analytical drift swamps real annual change. Read with cover-crop and rotation practice adoption and yield stability in drought years.
