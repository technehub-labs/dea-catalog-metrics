## 2. Definition

Cross-Functional Experience Ratio (CFER-BSC) measures capability maturity or development in the Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective domain. Its calculation is Employees with Cross-Functional Rotations / Employees, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
CFER-BSC = Employees with Cross-Functional Rotations / Employees
```
