## 2. Definition

A Mining & Metals indicator, Tailings Facility Compliance Rate (TFC) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Tailings Facilities Meeting Stability Requirements / Facilities - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Mining & metals sector.

## 3. Context

Tailings facility compliance measures adherence to engineered, inspection, and monitoring requirements for the waste embankments that hold back a mine's processed slurry - and history's dam failures at Brumadinho and elsewhere show the tail risk is catastrophic and permanent, not statistical. Compliance is a weak proxy on its own: a facility can be technically compliant and still under-monitored, so read alongside independent dam-breach analyses and the Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management conformance disclosures. Distinguish routine inspection items from structural red flags - drift, piezometer anomalies, beach development - which demand escalation regardless of the overall rate.

## Formula

```
Tailings Facilities Meeting Stability Requirements / Facilities
```
