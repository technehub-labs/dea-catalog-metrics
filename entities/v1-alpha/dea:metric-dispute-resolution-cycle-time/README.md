## 2. Definition

Dispute Resolution Cycle Time (DRCT-AR) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking accounts payable & receivable operations (deep dive). The formula is Days from Dispute Raised to Resolved - reported in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Days from Dispute Raised to Resolved
```
