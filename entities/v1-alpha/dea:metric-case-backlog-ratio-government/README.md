## 2. Definition

Case Backlog Ratio (Government) (CBR-G) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of government & public administration reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Open Cases beyond Standard / Open Cases; the result is stated in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. In Public Sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures pending cases against processing capacity, or backlog age against service standards. Backlogs are the visible symptom of capacity-demand mismatch in permits, benefits, courts, and immigration, and aging matters more than counts. Watch for backlog reduction achieved by reclassifying or fast-tracking easy cases while the complex tail grows. Read with workforce vacancy rate (Government) and cycle-time distributions.

## Formula

```
Open Cases beyond Standard / Open Cases
```
