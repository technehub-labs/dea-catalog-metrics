## 2. Definition

Labor Productivity (Construction) (LPC) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Construction & Engineering domain. It is computed as Installed Quantity / Labor Hours, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Its use is concentrated in Construction & engineering. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Installed Quantity / Labor Hours
```
