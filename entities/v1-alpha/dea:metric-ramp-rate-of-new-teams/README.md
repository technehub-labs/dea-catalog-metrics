## 2. Definition

Ramp Rate of New Teams (RRNT) measures capability maturity or development in the Workforce Planning & Productivity domain. The formula is Weeks for New Team to Reach Target Velocity - reported in weeks. It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
RRNT = Weeks for New Team to Reach Target Velocity
```
