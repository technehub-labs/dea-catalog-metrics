## 2. Definition

A Generative AI & LLM Operations indicator, Model Output Copyright Risk Screen Rate (MOCRS) quantifies exposure to adverse outcomes. It is defined as Outputs Screened for Protected Content / Outputs; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of outputs or training data screened for copyright exposure - verbatim reproduction of protected works, license-violating data use. Copyright is the unsettled legal frontier of generative AI, and screening coverage is the practical mitigation enterprises can deploy today. Screening must cover training-data rights as well as outputs, since the exposure flows from both directions. Read with indemnification terms in vendor contracts and legal-review completion for high-exposure use cases.

## Formula

```
Outputs Screened for Protected Content / Outputs
```
