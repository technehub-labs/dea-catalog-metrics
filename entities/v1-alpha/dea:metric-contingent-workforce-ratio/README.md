## 2. Definition

Within Workforce Planning & Analytics, Contingent Workforce Ratio (CWR) is used to gauge the utilization or availability of capacity. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Contingent Workers / Total Workforce; the result is stated in percent (%). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of the workforce that is contingent - contractors, agency staff, gig - of total workforce capacity. Rising ratios can be smart flexibility or silent de-capitalization of capability, and the metric alone cannot distinguish intent from drift. Classification and co-employment risk ride on this number, so definitional integrity with legal review matters. Read with misclassification audit findings and contingent cost trends.

## Formula

```
Contingent Workers / Total Workforce
```
