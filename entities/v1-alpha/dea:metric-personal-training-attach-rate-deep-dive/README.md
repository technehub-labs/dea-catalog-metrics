## 2. Definition

Personal Training Attach Rate (Deep Dive) (PTAR-D) captures growth over time for organizations tracking fitness & wellness (deep dive). It is computed as Members with PT Sessions / Members, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Health & Fitness. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
PTAR-D = Members with PT Sessions / Members
```
