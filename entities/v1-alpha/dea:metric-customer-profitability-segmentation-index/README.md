## 2. Definition

Customer Profitability Segmentation Index (CPSI-BSC) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective domain. It is defined as Share of Profitable Customers in Portfolio; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how distinctly customer segments differ in profitability. Segmentation that fails to differentiate profitability drives uniform, value-destroying service levels. Allocated cost assumptions dominate the segment math. Read with customer lifetime value trend and share of wallet.

## Formula

```
Share of Profitable Customers in Portfolio
```
