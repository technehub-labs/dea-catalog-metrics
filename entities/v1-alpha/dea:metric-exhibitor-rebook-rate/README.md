## 2. Definition

A Events & Exhibitions indicator, Exhibitor Rebook Rate (ERR-E) quantifies the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. It is defined as Exhibitors Rebooking for Next Edition / Exhibitors; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Media & Entertainment, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of exhibitors signing for the next edition of an event. Exhibitor retention is the compounding revenue engine of trade shows: rebookings sell at lower acquisition cost and anchor floor-plan credibility that attracts new exhibitors. Rebookings bought with aggressive early-bird discounting trade margin for the rate. Read with net square metre sell-through and exhibitor NPS.

## Formula

```
ERR-E = Exhibitors Rebooking for Next Edition / Exhibitors
```
