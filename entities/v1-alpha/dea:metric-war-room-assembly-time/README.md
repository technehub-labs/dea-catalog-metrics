## 2. Definition

A IT Incident & Major Problem Management indicator, War Room Assembly Time (WRAT) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Minutes to Assemble Response Team for Major Incident, expressed in minutes. It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Minutes to Assemble Response Team for Major Incident
```
