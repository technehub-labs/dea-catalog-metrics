## 2. Definition

Safety Audit Compliance Score (SACS) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of health & safety reporting. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It is defined as Weighted Audit Score of Site Safety Conditions; the result is stated in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures performance against workplace safety audit checklists. It matters because audit rigor predicts incident reduction better than lagging injury counts alone. Scores can be gamed by preparing audited areas intensively while unaudited zones decay. Read with Near Miss Reporting Rate and Corrective Action Closure Rate to confirm audit health reflects everyday conditions.

## Formula

```
Weighted Audit Score of Site Safety Conditions
```
