## 2. Definition

A Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning indicator, Risk Radar Refresh Currency (RRRC) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Risk Radar Updated per Cadence / Required Updates, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Risk Radar Updated per Cadence / Required Updates
```
