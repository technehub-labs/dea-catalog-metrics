## 2. Definition

Earnings Yield (Deep Dive) (EYDD) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Valuation & Corporate Finance Standard Measures domain. The formula is Earnings per Share / Price per Share - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures earnings as a return on market capitalization. It is the inverse of the P/E and the natural comparator to bond yields and WACC. Accounting earnings diverge from cash earnings in ways that matter here. Read with free cash flow yield and PEG ratio.

## Formula

```
Earnings per Share / Price per Share
```
