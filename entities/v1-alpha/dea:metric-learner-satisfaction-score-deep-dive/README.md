## 2. Definition

Learner Satisfaction Score (Deep Dive) (LSS-D) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking education & training (deep dive). It is computed as Average End-of-Course Rating, expressed in score (points). Its use is concentrated in Education. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

Average End-of-Course Rating
