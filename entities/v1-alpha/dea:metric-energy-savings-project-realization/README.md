## 2. Definition

Energy Savings Project Realization (ESPR) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Operations Energy Management domain. It is defined as Realized Savings / Committed Savings from Projects; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of projected energy savings from efficiency projects actually confirmed in utility bills or measured consumption. Engineering estimates routinely overstate savings by double counting interactions between projects and assuming ideal operating discipline. Insist on measurement and verification protocols (IPMVP-style) and post-project seasonal adjustment. Read with energy cost ratio to output for the aggregate effect.

## Formula

```
Realized Savings / Committed Savings from Projects
```
