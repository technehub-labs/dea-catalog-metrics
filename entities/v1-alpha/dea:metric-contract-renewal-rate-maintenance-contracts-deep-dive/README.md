## 2. Definition

A Field Maintenance & Technical Field Service (Deep Dive) indicator, Contract Renewal Rate - Maintenance Contracts (Deep Dive) (CRMC-D) quantifies the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. It is defined as Maintenance Contracts Renewed / Expiring; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of maintenance contracts renewed at term. Renewal is the compounding engine of field-service profitability; a 10% improvement transforms lifetime economics. Renewals can be bought with pre-term discounts that erode margin. Read with maintenance contract SLA attainment and net revenue retention.

## Formula

```
Maintenance Contracts Renewed / Expiring
```
