## 2. Definition

A Marketing & Advertising Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Customer Acquisition Spend Share (CASS) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is defined as Acquisition Spend / Total Marketing Budget; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of marketing budget devoted to acquisition versus retention and growth. Allocation share reveals the strategic posture behind the spend. Heavy acquisition share with weak retention is a leaky bucket. Read with retention marketing ROI and LTV-to-CAC ratio (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Acquisition Spend / Total Marketing Budget
```
