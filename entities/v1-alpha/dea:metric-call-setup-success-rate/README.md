## 2. Definition

Call Setup Success Rate (CSSR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking telecommunications. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Successfully Established Calls / Call Attempts, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Its use is concentrated in Telecommunications.

## Formula

```
Successfully Established Calls / Call Attempts
```
