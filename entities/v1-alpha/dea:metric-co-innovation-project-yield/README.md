## 2. Definition

Co-Innovation Project Yield (CIPY) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of corporate venturing & partnerships reporting. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Co-Innovation Projects Reaching Market / Projects Started; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how many joint innovation projects with partners deliver commercial outcomes - products launched, revenues, cost savings - versus started. Co-innovation is high-talent-cost work whose yield is frequently poor due to misaligned incentives and IP disputes settled by abandonment. Count abandoned projects honestly, not as paused, and attribute joint outcomes by pre-agreed rules. Read with startup pilot conversion rate and strategic alliance health score.

## Formula

```
Co-Innovation Projects Reaching Market / Projects Started
```
