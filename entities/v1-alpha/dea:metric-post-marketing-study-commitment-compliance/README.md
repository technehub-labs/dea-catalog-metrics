## 2. Definition

Post-Marketing Study Commitment Compliance (PMSC) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of pharma r&d & clinical trials reporting. It is defined as Commitments on Schedule / Commitments Made; the result is stated in percent (%). In Pharmaceuticals, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures on-time delivery of studies committed at approval. Missed commitments invite enforcement and, worse, can trigger label or approval consequences, and they erode regulator trust that accelerates future reviews. Be honest about scope renegotiations: commitments amended to fit delivery are soft noncompliance. Read with inspection findings and regulatory engagement success rate.

## Formula

```
Commitments on Schedule / Commitments Made
```
