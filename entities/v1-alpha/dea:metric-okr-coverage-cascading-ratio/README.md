## 2. Definition

OKR Coverage - Cascading Ratio (OKRCR) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Enterprise OKR & Performance Management domain. The formula is Departmental OKRs Linked to Enterprise Objectives / Objectives - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how far objectives cascade from enterprise level through functions to teams. Cascading aligns local effort with strategic priorities. Mechanical cascade (copy-paste objectives) gives coverage without alignment. Read with cross-functional OKR alignment ratio and strategic goal execution rate.

## Formula

```
Departmental OKRs Linked to Enterprise Objectives / Objectives
```
