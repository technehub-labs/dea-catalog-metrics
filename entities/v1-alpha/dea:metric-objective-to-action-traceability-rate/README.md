## 2. Definition

Objective-to-Action Traceability Rate (OTATR) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Enterprise OKR & Performance Management domain. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. The formula is Initiatives Traced to Objectives / Initiatives - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether each objective can be traced to concrete initiatives and work items. Traceability converts strategy from poster to plan. Traces that stop at initiative names without contribution logic are thin. Read with benefits tracking coverage and strategic alignment score - initiatives.

## Formula

```
Initiatives Traced to Objectives / Initiatives
```
