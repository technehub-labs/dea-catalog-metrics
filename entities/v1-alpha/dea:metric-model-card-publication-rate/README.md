## 2. Definition

Within AI Ethics, Fairness & Responsible AI, Model Card Publication Rate (MCPR-AI) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Models with Published Model Cards / Models in Production, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Models with Published Model Cards / Models in Production
```
