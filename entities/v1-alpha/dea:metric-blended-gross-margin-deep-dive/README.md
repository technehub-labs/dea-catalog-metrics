## 2. Definition

Blended Gross Margin (Deep Dive) (BGMD) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of unit economics & growth (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Gross Profit including Services / Total Revenue - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Software sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures gross margin across all revenue streams combined. Blended margin is the honest aggregate for multi-line models. Mix shifts can move blended margin without any efficiency change. Read with gross profit margin (Deep Dive) and expansion revenue rate (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
BGMD = Gross Profit including Services / Total Revenue
```
