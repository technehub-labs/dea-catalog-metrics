## 2. Definition

Experiment Win Rate - Business Experiments (EWR-B) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of data science & analytics operations reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Experiments with Statistically Significant Uplift / Experiments, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of tested hypotheses that produce confirmed improvements. It is the learning-yield metric of experimentation cultures: the honest surprise is that win rates are usually low, and a high win rate can indicate timid tests or inflated success criteria. Win-rate trends also reveal idea-pipeline quality - exhausted obvious wins precede declining rates. Read with experiment velocity and implemented-win persistence at ninety days.

## Formula

```
Experiments with Statistically Significant Uplift / Experiments
```
