## 2. Definition

Gross Profit Margin (Deep Dive) (GPMD) captures the economic value created or preserved for organizations tracking financial statement analysis ratios (deep dive). The formula is Gross Profit / Revenue - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue retained after direct costs. Gross margin is the cleanest signal of pricing power and production efficiency. Margin maintained by cost-deferral accounting is temporary. Read with gross margin stability index and net profit margin (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Gross Profit / Revenue
```
