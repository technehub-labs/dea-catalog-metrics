## 2. Definition

Within Pharma R&D & Clinical Trials, Investigator-Initiated Study Turnaround (IIST) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is computed as Days from Proposal to Study Approval, expressed in days. Its use is concentrated in Pharmaceuticals. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.
