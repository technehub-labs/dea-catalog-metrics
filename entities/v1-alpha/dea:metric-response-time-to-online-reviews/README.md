## 2. Definition

A Hotel & Resort Operations (Deep Dive) indicator, Response Time to Online Reviews (RTOR) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Hours to Respond to Guest Review - reported in hours. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. It is a standard measure in the Hospitality sector.

## Formula

```
Hours to Respond to Guest Review
```
