## 2. Definition

Within Chemicals & Specialty Materials, Solvent Recovery Rate (Deep Dive) (SRR-C2) is used to gauge environmental and social sustainability performance. Its calculation is Recovered Solvent Reused / Solvent Consumed, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Chemicals practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Recovered Solvent Reused / Solvent Consumed
```
