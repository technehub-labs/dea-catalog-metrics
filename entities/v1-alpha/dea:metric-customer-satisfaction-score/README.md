## 2. Definition

Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) captures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved for organizations tracking customer. It is defined as Satisfied Responses / Total Survey Responses; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures average customer ratings of satisfaction with a product or service interaction. It matters as a general barometer of experience quality that correlates weakly but usefully with retention. Scores can be gamed by surveying only happy customers, timing surveys after positive touchpoints, or burying unhappy responses. Read with Net Promoter Score and Customer Effort Score to triangulate experience across emotional and effort dimensions.

## Formula

```
Satisfied Responses / Total Survey Responses
```
