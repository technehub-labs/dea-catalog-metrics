## 2. Definition

A Diversity, Equity & Inclusion indicator, Leadership Diversity Representation (LDR) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Underrepresented Group in Leadership / Leadership Roles - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures demographic composition of the leadership population, typically director and above. It matters because leadership composition shapes decisions, culture, and external credibility, and it is the level where diversity progress is slowest and most contested. Set an unambiguous leadership definition, since thresholds chosen for optics change the number materially. Read with promotion parity ratio and succession plan composition for forward view.

## Formula

```
Underrepresented Group in Leadership / Leadership Roles
```
