## 2. Definition

Hiring Diversity Rate (HDR-D) captures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations for organizations tracking diversity, equity & inclusion. It is computed as Diverse Hires / Total Hires, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the demographic composition of new hires over a period. It is the fastest-moving lever on representation, and a leading indicator of whether stated diversity goals translate into sourcing and selection behavior. Watch for gaming via definition of the applicant denominator, reclassification of internal transfers as hires, and short-lived improvement driven by one team's effort. Read with workforce diversity representation by level and offer acceptance parity.

## Formula

```
Diverse Hires / Total Hires
```
