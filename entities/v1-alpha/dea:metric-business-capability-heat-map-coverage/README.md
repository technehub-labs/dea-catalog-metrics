## 2. Definition

Business Capability Heat Map Coverage (BCHMC) captures capability maturity or development for organizations tracking operating model monitoring & design effectiveness. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Capabilities with Performance and Health Ratings / Capabilities; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how completely the business capability model is assessed for health and investment needs. Heat maps focus scarce change investment on the capabilities that constrain strategy. Coverage with stale or opinion-only heat scoring creates confident misdirection. Read with capability maturity assessment currency and strategic competency coverage.

## Formula

```
Capabilities with Performance and Health Ratings / Capabilities
```
