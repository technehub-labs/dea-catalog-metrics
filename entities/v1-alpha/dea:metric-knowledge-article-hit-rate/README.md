## 2. Definition

Knowledge Article Hit Rate (KAHR-CC) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Contact Center Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. Its calculation is Contacts Resolved via Suggested Article / Article Suggestions Shown, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Contacts Resolved via Suggested Article / Article Suggestions Shown
```
