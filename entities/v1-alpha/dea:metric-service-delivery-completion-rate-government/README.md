## 2. Definition

Within Government & Public Administration, Service Delivery Completion Rate (Government) (SDCR) is used to gauge how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its calculation is Services Delivered within Standard / Services Due, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Public Sector practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of government service transactions completed within required standards, end-to-end, including follow-through. It converts administrative output into the citizen-experienced outcome, which is what budget debates actually fund. Definitional integrity matters: services deemed complete at handoff (referral, determination) understate true completion when downstream agencies drop cases. Read with cost per citizen transaction and case backlog ratio (Government).

## Formula

```
Services Delivered within Standard / Services Due
```
