## 2. Definition

Promise-to-Pay Kept Rate (PPKR) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Banking Retail Lending (Deep Dive) domain. It is computed as Promises Honored / Promises Made, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is concentrated in Banking.

## Formula

```
Promises Honored / Promises Made
```
