## 2. Definition

Revenue per FTE (Deep Dive) (RPFTE) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of workforce planning & productivity reporting. Its calculation is Revenue / Full-Time Equivalents, measured in currency per unit. It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures revenue generated per full-time equivalent employee. It is the standard top-line productivity comparator. Average FTE masks the mix shift between full-time and contingent labor. Read with contingent workforce ratio (Deep Dive) and revenue per employee (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Revenue / Full-Time Equivalents
```
