## 2. Definition

Repeat Traveler Rate (RTR-T) captures the retention of customers, users, or assets over time for organizations tracking travel & tourism. It is defined as Travelers Booking Again within Period / Travelers; the result is stated in percent (%). It bridges daily operations and executive reporting, giving managers a lever to pull within a quarter. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Travel & tourism, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of customers who book again within a period. It matters because repeat guests carry far lower acquisition cost and higher lifetime value. Discount-driven rebooking buys repeat behavior without loyalty economics. Read with Customer Repeat Rate within 90 Days and Direct Booking Share to confirm repeats are profitable and channel-efficient.

## Formula

```
Travelers Booking Again within Period / Travelers
```
