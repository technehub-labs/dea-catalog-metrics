## 2. Definition

Within Knowledge Management, Time to Find Information (TTFI) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Average Minutes to Locate Needed Information - reported in minutes. It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
TTFI = Average Minutes to Locate Needed Information
```
