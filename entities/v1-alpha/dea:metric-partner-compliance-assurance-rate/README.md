## 2. Definition

Partner Compliance Assurance Rate (PCAR) captures exposure to adverse outcomes for organizations tracking alliances & partnerships management. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Partners Passing Annual Compliance Review / Partners in Scope; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how well partners meet contractual, regulatory, and brand obligations. Partner non-compliance is enforced under your brand name. Assurance by attestation rather than sampling and verification is thin. Read with partner data sharing compliance and channel program compliance rate.

## Formula

```
Partners Passing Annual Compliance Review / Partners in Scope
```
