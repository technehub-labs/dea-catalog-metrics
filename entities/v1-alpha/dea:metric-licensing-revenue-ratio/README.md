## 2. Definition

Licensing Revenue Ratio (LRR-CV) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Corporate Venturing & Partnerships domain. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Licensing and Royalty Revenue / Total Revenue, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of revenue from licensing intellectual property - patents, brands, content, technology - rather than products or services. A rising ratio signals monetizable IP strength and asset-light growth, often with exceptional margins. Watch for one-off deals creating permanent-sounding trends, and for licensing that cannibalizes product sales or trains future competitors. Read with royalty yield on R&D spend and renewal rates of licensed rights.

## Formula

```
LRR-CV = Licensing and Royalty Revenue / Total Revenue
```
