## 2. Definition

Trial to Membership Conversion (T2M) measures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome in the Fitness & Wellness domain. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Trials Converting to Membership / Trials, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Fitness & wellness practice.

## 3. Context

Trial to membership conversion measures the share of prospects who try the club and join, and it is the fitness industry's sales-funnel effectiveness metric - trials are the standard acquisition mechanism (free weeks, guest passes, class packs), and the trial experience is effectively the product demonstration. Conversion rates carry diagnostic depth when segmented: trial-to-join conversion varies enormously by trial type (paid trial converts far higher than free), by whether the prospect was onboarded with a first-workout appointment (human connection is the strongest known conversion factor), and by usage during trial (the number of visits during a trial predicts joining more than any demographic trait). Read with trial volume by source (some channels bring trialists who never intended to join), onboarding appointment completion rate, and cohort retention after conversion since churn-prone conversions are false wins.

## Formula

```
Trials Converting to Membership / Trials
```
