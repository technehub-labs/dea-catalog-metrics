## 2. Definition

Stay Interview Coverage (SIC-EE) addresses the retention of customers, users, or assets over time and is a fixture of employee engagement & culture (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Key Talent with Stay Interview in 12 Months / Key Talent, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
SIC-EE = Key Talent with Stay Interview in 12 Months / Key Talent
```
