## 2. Definition

A Retail Standard Measures indicator, Online Order Fulfilment Cost Ratio (OOFCR) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Pick-Pack-Deliver Cost / Online Revenue - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Retail & E-commerce sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures fulfilment cost relative to online revenue. Digital channel economics stand or fall on fulfilment cost. Costs excluding returns processing understate the true ratio substantially. Read with return rate (e-commerce) and cost to serve per customer (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Pick-Pack-Deliver Cost / Online Revenue
```
