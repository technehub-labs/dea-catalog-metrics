## 2. Definition

Within Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive), Freedom of Information Response Timeliness (FOIRT) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Requests Answered within Statutory Deadline / Requests, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Public sector practice.

## 3. Context

Measures whether FOI requests are answered within statutory deadlines. Timeliness is the legal floor of open government. Extensions obtained as routine rather than exceptional normalize delay. Read with regulatory retrieval success rate and records disposition timeliness.

## Formula

```
Requests Answered within Statutory Deadline / Requests
```
