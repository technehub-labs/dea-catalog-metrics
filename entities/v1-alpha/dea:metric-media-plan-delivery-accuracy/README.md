## 2. Definition

Media Plan Delivery Accuracy (MPDA) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of advertising & media buying reporting. It is computed as Delivered Impressions vs Plan within Tolerance / Placements, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Delivered Impressions vs Plan within Tolerance / Placements
```
