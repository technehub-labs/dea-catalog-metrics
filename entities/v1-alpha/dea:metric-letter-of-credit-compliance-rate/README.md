## 2. Definition

Within International Trade & Customs, Letter of Credit Compliance Rate (LCCR) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its calculation is Presentations without Discrepancies / Presentations, measured in percent (%). It provides the day-to-day signal that a process is running as designed. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Logistics & Trade practice.

## Formula

```
Presentations without Discrepancies / Presentations
```
