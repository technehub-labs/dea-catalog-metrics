## 2. Definition

A Human Resources indicator, Offer Acceptance Rate (OAR) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Offers Accepted / Offers Extended, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of extended offers that candidates accept. It matters because declined offers waste pipeline and signal compensation or brand misalignment. Teams can protect it by only extending offers they are certain will be accepted, narrowing the candidate pool. Read with Time to Hire and Offer Decline Reasons tracking to see whether the rate reflects brand strength or risk-averse extending.

## Formula

```
Offers Accepted / Offers Extended
```
