## 2. Definition

Customer Advocacy Rate (CAR) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of customer reporting. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Customers Providing Referrals or Public Advocacy / Total Customers, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of customers actively recommending or defending the brand. It matters because advocacy drives organic acquisition at near-zero cost and signals durable loyalty. Gaming risk includes incentivized referrals counted as organic advocacy or counting social mentions regardless of sentiment. Read with Net Promoter Score and Referral Rate to separate authentic advocacy from paid amplification.

## Formula

```
CAR = Customers Providing Referrals or Public Advocacy / Total Customers
```
