## 2. Definition

Critical Skill Coverage Ratio (CSCR-LD) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of learning & development effectiveness (deep dive) reporting. The formula is Roles with Successor-Ready Skills / Critical Roles - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether the organization holds the critical skills its strategy requires, at required depth. Strategic skills gaps are execution risk in disguise. Self-rated coverage overstates capability against tested assessment. Read with strategic competency coverage and critical talent retention rate (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Roles with Successor-Ready Skills / Critical Roles
```
