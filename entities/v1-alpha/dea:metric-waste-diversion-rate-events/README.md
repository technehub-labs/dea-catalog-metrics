## 2. Definition

A Events & Exhibitions indicator, Waste Diversion Rate (Events) (WDR-E) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. Its calculation is Waste Diverted from Landfill / Total Waste, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Waste Diverted from Landfill / Total Waste
```
