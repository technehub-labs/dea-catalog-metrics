## 2. Definition

Conversion Rate (CVR) addresses how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome and is a fixture of marketing reporting. It is computed as Conversions / Total Sessions or Visitors, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Conversions / Total Sessions or Visitors
```
