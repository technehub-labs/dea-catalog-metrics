## 2. Definition

Blast Fragmentation Index (BFI) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking metals & mining operations (deep dive). The formula is Fragment Size Distribution Match to Target - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is a standard measure in the Metals & Mining sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
