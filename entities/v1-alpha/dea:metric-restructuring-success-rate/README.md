## 2. Definition

A Credit & Collections Risk (Enterprise) indicator, Restructuring Success Rate (RSR-CC) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is defined as Restructured Accounts Remaining Current / Restructured; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how many restructured or cured loans perform sustainably. Restructuring that merely postpones default is failure with paperwork. Success measured to the first payment (not full cure) overstates. Read with non-performing loan ratio trend and collections effectiveness index.

## Formula

```
Restructured Accounts Remaining Current / Restructured
```
