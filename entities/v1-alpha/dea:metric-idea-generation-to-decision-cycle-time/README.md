## 2. Definition

Idea Generation to Decision Cycle Time (IGDCT) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Investment Research & Trading Operations domain. Its calculation is Days from Research Publication to Decision, measured in days. It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
IGDCT = Days from Research Publication to Decision
```
