## 2. Definition

Adviser Productivity - AUM per Adviser (APA-W) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking asset & wealth management (deep dive). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is AUM / Advisers, measured in currency per unit. Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Financial Services practice.

## 3. Context

Measures assets managed or advised per adviser. It is the leverage metric of wealth and asset management economics: adviser cost is the dominant expense, and productivity trends decide whether margins expand or the model breaks. Productivity raised by assigning orphan accounts to unprepared advisers is growth at quality risk. Read with client-to-adviser ratios and client satisfaction or complaint trends.

## Formula

```
AUM / Advisers
```
