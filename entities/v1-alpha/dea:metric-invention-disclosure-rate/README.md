## 2. Definition

Invention Disclosure Rate (IDR-IP) measures growth over time in the Intellectual Property & Licensing domain. Its calculation is Disclosures Filed per 1,000 R&D Staff per Year, measured in events per thousand or million (scaled). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures how often engineers and scientists formally disclose inventions. Disclosure rate is the earliest indicator of IP pipeline health. Rates sustained by trivial disclosures create prosecution waste. Read with patent portfolio growth rate and innovation pipeline conversion rate.

## Formula

```
Disclosures Filed per 1,000 R&D Staff per Year
```
