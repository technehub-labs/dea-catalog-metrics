## 2. Definition

Customer Complaint Rate (Transit Deep Dive) (CCR-T) addresses the quality of outputs and outcomes and is a fixture of public transit (deep dive) reporting. Its calculation is Complaints per 100,000 Journeys, measured in events per thousand or million (scaled). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. It originates from and is most used in Public Transit practice.

## Formula

```
Complaints per 100,000 Journeys
```
