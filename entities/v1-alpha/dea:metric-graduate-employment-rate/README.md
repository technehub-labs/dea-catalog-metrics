## 2. Definition

A Education & Training Outcome Standard Measures indicator, Graduate Employment Rate (GER) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Graduates Employed within Study Field Window / Graduates - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Education sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of graduates employed (ideally in field) within a defined window. Employment is the outcome students enroll for. Rates measured on respondents to alumni surveys are survivorship-biased. Read with employer satisfaction with graduates and time-to-degree (Median).

## Formula

```
Graduates Employed within Study Field Window / Graduates
```
