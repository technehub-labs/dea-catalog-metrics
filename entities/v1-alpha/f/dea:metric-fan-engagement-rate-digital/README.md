## 2. Definition

A Sports & Events indicator, Fan Engagement Rate (Digital) (FER-D) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its calculation is Interactions on Club Channels / Followers, measured in percent (%). It originates from and is most used in Sports & events practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures fan interactions relative to fan base across digital channels. It matters because engagement drives media value, ticketing, and merchandising revenue. Engagement can be bought through contests and giveaways that do not build durable fandom. Read with Ticket Renewal Rate and Merchandise Revenue per Attendee to confirm engagement converts to revenue.

## Formula

```
FER-D = Interactions on Club Channels / Followers
```
