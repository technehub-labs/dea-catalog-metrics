## 2. Definition

Feedback Conversation Frequency (FCF) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of culture & employee experience reporting. It is computed as Documented Feedback Conversations per Employee per Period, expressed in ratio (x:1 or x). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
FCF = Documented Feedback Conversations per Employee per Period
```
