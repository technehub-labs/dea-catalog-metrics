## 2. Definition

Free Trade Agreement Utilization (FTAU) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of international trade & customs reporting. It is defined as Preferential Entries Claimed / Eligible Entries; the result is stated in percent (%). In Logistics & Trade, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of eligible trade flows using preferential FTA rates rather than standard duty. Unclaimed preferences are pure margin leakage, and utilization gaps reveal both documentation friction and ignorance of eligibility. Rules-of-origin compliance is the risk twin: claimed preferences that fail post-clearance audit convert savings into penalties. Read with post-clearance audit adjustment rate and origin documentation quality.

## Formula

```
FTAU = Preferential Entries Claimed / Eligible Entries
```
