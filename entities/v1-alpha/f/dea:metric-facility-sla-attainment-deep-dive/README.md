## 2. Definition

A Facility Management & Workplace (Deep Dive) indicator, Facility SLA Attainment (Deep Dive) (FSLA-D) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is SLAs Met / SLAs Measured, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
FSLA-D = SLAs Met / SLAs Measured
```
