## 2. Definition

Material Weakness Count (MWC) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of internal controls & financial reporting integrity reporting. The formula is Material Weaknesses Identified in Period - reported in count (items per period). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the number of material weaknesses identified in internal control over financial reporting. Material weaknesses are the serious end of the control-failure spectrum, with direct disclosure consequences. Counts rise with honest detection before they fall with remediation, so trend needs context. Read with significant deficiency closure rate and control environment maturity score.
