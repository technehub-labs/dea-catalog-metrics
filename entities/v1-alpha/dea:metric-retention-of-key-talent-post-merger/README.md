## 2. Definition

Retention of Key Talent (Post-Merger) (RKTP) addresses the retention of customers, users, or assets over time and is a fixture of mergers & acquisitions reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Key Personnel Retained at 12 Months / Key Personnel, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures retention of named critical people through and after integration. Key talent flight is the most common way deal theses die quietly: acquired capabilities walk out wearing lanyards. Track against retention packages honestly - paid retention that expires followed by exit is deferred, not solved, attrition. Read with cultural integration survey score and successor coverage in acquired units.

## Formula

```
Key Personnel Retained at 12 Months / Key Personnel
```
