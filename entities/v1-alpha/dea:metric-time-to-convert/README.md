## 2. Definition

Time to Convert (TTC) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of marketing reporting. Its calculation is Average Days from First Touch to Conversion, measured in days. It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures the average time from first touch to conversion. It matters because shorter journeys reduce drop-off and raise campaign ROI. It can be gamed by attributing only late-stage touchpoints, making journeys look shorter than they are. Read with Lead Conversion Rate and Sales Cycle Length to check acceleration is real across the whole journey.

## Formula

```
Average Days from First Touch to Conversion
```
