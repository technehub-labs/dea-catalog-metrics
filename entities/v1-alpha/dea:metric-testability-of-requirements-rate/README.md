## 2. Definition

A Requirements Engineering & Product Discovery indicator, Testability of Requirements Rate (TORR-RE) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is Requirements Written as Testable Statements / Requirements - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Requirements Written as Testable Statements / Requirements
```
