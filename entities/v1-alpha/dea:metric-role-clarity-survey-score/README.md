## 2. Definition

Role Clarity Survey Score (RCSS-OM) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Operating Model Monitoring & Design Effectiveness domain. It is computed as Employee-Reported Role and Decision Clarity, expressed in score (points). It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.
