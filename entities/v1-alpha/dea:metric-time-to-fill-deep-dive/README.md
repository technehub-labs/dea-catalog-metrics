## 2. Definition

Time to Fill (Deep Dive) (TTFDD) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of talent acquisition deep dive reporting. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It is computed as Days from Requisition Open to Accepted Offer, expressed in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

Days from Requisition Open to Accepted Offer
