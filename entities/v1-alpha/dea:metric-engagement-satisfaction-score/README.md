## 2. Definition

A Professional Services indicator, Engagement Satisfaction Score (ESS) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Satisfied Client Survey Responses / Responses - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Professional services sector.

## 3. Context

Measures client satisfaction with professional services engagements. It matters because services businesses live or die on renewals and referrals driven by perceived value. Scores can be gamed by surveying mid-engagement before the difficult delivery phases. Read with Project Margin and Client Renewal Rate to confirm satisfaction converts into commercial outcomes.

## Formula

```
Satisfied Client Survey Responses / Responses
```
