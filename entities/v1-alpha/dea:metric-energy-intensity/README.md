## 2. Definition

Energy Intensity (EI) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking sustainability & esg. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Energy Consumed / Unit of Output or Revenue - reported in ratio (x:1 or x). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures energy consumed per unit of output or revenue. It matters because it decouples efficiency from production volume and tracks both cost and emissions. It can be gamed by outsourcing energy-heavy steps so the metric improves while total footprint shifts upstream. Read with Energy Cost per Unit and Scope 1 and 2 emissions data to confirm intensity gains are real, not boundary tricks.

## Formula

```
Energy Consumed / Unit of Output or Revenue
```
