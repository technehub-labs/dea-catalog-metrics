## 2. Definition

Landfill Gas Capture Rate (LGCR) captures environmental and social sustainability performance for organizations tracking waste & environmental services. The formula is Captured Landfill Gas / Generated Landfill Gas - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Environmental services sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Landfill gas capture rate measures the share of generated methane collected by the gas system rather than emitted, and it is the metric that determines whether a landfill is a climate liability or an energy asset - methane has roughly 80 times the warming potential of CO2 over 20 years, making uncaptured gas a serious emissions problem and captured gas a revenue stream for electricity or RNG projects. Capture performance is technically bounded: standard wells achieve 60-90 percent depending on cover quality and system design, so 100 percent claims deserve scrutiny. Capture varies over the landfill life cycle, peaking during active operation and declining post-closure. Read with surface emissions monitoring (which measures what actually escapes), well-field vacuum optimization, and cover integrity inspection results.

## Formula

```
Captured Landfill Gas / Generated Landfill Gas
```
