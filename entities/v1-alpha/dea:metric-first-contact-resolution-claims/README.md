## 2. Definition

First Contact Resolution (Claims) (FCR-C) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking insurance claims deep dive. It is computed as Claims Resolved at First Contact / Claims, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Insurance. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Claims Resolved at First Contact / Claims
```
