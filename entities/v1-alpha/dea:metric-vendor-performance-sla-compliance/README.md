## 2. Definition

A Third-Party Risk Management indicator, Vendor Performance SLA Compliance (VPSC) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. The formula is SLAs Met by Vendors / SLAs Measured - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
SLAs Met by Vendors / SLAs Measured
```
