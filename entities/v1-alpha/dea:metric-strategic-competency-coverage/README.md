## 2. Definition

Strategic Competency Coverage (SCC-BSC) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of balanced scorecard - learning & growth perspective reporting. The formula is Roles with Required Competencies Filled / Strategic Roles - reported in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures how well the workforce holds the competencies the strategy requires. Strategy execution fails quietly when the required capabilities are missing or thin. Self-assessed competency grids inflate coverage. Read with skills gap analysis coverage and digital skill proficiency index.

## Formula

```
Roles with Required Competencies Filled / Strategic Roles
```
