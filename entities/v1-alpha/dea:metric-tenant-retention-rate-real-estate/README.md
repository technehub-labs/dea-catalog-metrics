## 2. Definition

A Real Estate & Mortgage Standard Measures indicator, Tenant Retention Rate (Real Estate) (TRR-RE) quantifies the retention of customers, users, or assets over time. The formula is Leases Renewed / Leases Expiring - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It is a standard measure in the Real estate sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of expiring leases renewed. Retention is far cheaper than vacancy plus fit-out and downtime. Retention bought through aggressive concessions can be value-negative. Read with weighted average lease term and net effective rent ratio.

## Formula

```
Leases Renewed / Leases Expiring
```
