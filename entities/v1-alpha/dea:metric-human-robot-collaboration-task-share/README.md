## 2. Definition

Human-Robot Collaboration Task Share (HRTS) measures capability maturity or development in the Industrial Automation & Robotics domain. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Tasks Performed Collaboratively / Automatable Tasks, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Manufacturing practice.

## 3. Context

Measures the proportion of tasks performed jointly by humans and robots. It matters because collaborative share signals flexibility and workforce integration maturity. Task allocation can be counted as collaborative when it is merely adjacent. Read with Workplace Safety incidents in shared cells and productivity per station to confirm collaboration is real and safe.

## Formula

```
Tasks Performed Collaboratively / Automatable Tasks
```
