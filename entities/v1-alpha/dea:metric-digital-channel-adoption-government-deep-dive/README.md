## 2. Definition

A Public Sector & Citizen Service Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Digital Channel Adoption (Government) (Deep Dive) (DCADD) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Digital Service Transactions / Total Transactions; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. In Public sector, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of citizen transactions completed through digital channels. Digital shift is the cost and accessibility lever of citizen services. Adoption counted on transactions citizens were forced into digital (no assisted fallback) is not preference. Read with assisted digital completion rate and service accessibility standard compliance (Public).

## Formula

```
DCADD = Digital Service Transactions / Total Transactions
```
