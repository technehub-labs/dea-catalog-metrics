## 2. Definition

Meeting-Free Focus Time Ratio (MFFTR) addresses how efficiently resources are converted into outputs and is a fixture of workforce planning & productivity reporting. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It is computed as Deep Work Hours / Paid Hours, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Deep Work Hours / Paid Hours
```
