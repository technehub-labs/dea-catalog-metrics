## 2. Definition

Contracted Sell-Through Attainment (CSTA) addresses the economic value created or preserved and is a fixture of channel & distribution management reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Partner Sell-Through vs Commitment / Commitment, expressed in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures whether partners meet their committed purchase or sales targets. Commitments are the accountability spine of channel programs. Attainment measured on purchases rather than end-customer sales counts channel stuffing. Read with channel partner productivity and inventory in channel (sell-in vs. sell-out).

## Formula

```
CSTA = Partner Sell-Through vs Commitment / Commitment
```
