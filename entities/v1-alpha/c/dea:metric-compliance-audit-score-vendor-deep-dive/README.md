## 2. Definition

Compliance Audit Score - Vendor (Deep Dive) (CASV-D) measures conformance to standards, plans, or obligations in the Textile & Apparel (Deep Dive) domain. It is defined as Social and Quality Audit Composite Score; the result is stated in score (points). In Textiles & Apparel, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures supplier facility audit results against social, environmental, and safety compliance standards. In textile supply chains, vendor audits are the control layer for risks the brand owns reputationally - labor practices, safety conditions, unauthorized subcontracting - and audit integrity determines whether the control is real. Audit-quality issues (unannounced versus scheduled, auditor competence) matter more than scores. Read with corrective-action closure and unannounced-audit findings divergence.

## Formula

CASV-D = Social and Quality Audit Composite Score
