## 2. Definition

Within Operating Model Monitoring & Design Effectiveness, Centralization versus Autonomy Balance Index (CVABI) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. The formula is Balance Score of Central and Local Mandates - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures how design authority and execution autonomy are balanced against the intended operating philosophy. Neither pole is virtuous: the question is fit with strategy and scale. Index scoring embeds ideology as much as evidence. Read with decision rights clarity score and shared service adoption ratio.

## Formula

CVABI = Balance Score of Central and Local Mandates
