## 2. Definition

Catalyst Lifetime Efficiency (CLE) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Chemicals & Specialty Materials domain. Its calculation is Output per Catalyst Load vs Design, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It originates from and is most used in Chemicals practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
CLE = Output per Catalyst Load vs Design
```
