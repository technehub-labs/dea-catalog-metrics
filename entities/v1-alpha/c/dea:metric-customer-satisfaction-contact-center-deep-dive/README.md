## 2. Definition

A Contact Center Standard Measures (Deep Dive) indicator, Customer Satisfaction (Contact Center) (Deep Dive) (CSATDD) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. The formula is Post-Contact Satisfaction Rating - reported in score (points). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

CSATDD = Post-Contact Satisfaction Rating
