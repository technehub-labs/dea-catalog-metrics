## 2. Definition

A Digital Transformation indicator, Customer Digital Preference Share (CDPS) quantifies growth over time. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Customers Choosing Digital Channel for Key Journey / Customers; the result is stated in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of customer interactions or transactions customers choose to conduct through digital channels, as opposed to channels firms push them into. It is the demand-side truth check on digital investment: if customers will not come, the front door was never the problem. Segment carefully, since preference varies sharply by age and task complexity, and verify that digital share is not rising only because assisted channels degraded. Read with journey completion rate and digital self-care adoption patterns.

## Formula

```
CDPS = Customers Choosing Digital Channel for Key Journey / Customers
```
