## 2. Definition

Co-Innovation Output Rate (CIOR) addresses growth over time and is a fixture of alliances & partnerships management reporting. Its calculation is Jointly Launched Innovations per Year, measured in count (items per period). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures how much jointly developed product or capability reaches market from alliances. Co-innovation is the premium value tier of partnership. Output counted as prototypes rather than shipped capability is potential. Read with new product revenue share (Deep Dive) and supplier innovation contribution rate.

## Formula

```
CIOR = Jointly Launched Innovations per Year
```
