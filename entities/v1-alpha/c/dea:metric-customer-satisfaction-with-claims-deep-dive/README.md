## 2. Definition

Within Insurance Claims Deep Dive, Customer Satisfaction with Claims (Deep Dive) (CSAT-C) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is defined as Average Claimant Satisfaction Score; the result is stated in score (points). In Insurance, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures claimant satisfaction through the claims journey, usually at settlement and at key touchpoints. Claims is the moment of truth where the insurance promise is tested, and satisfaction there predicts renewal far better than sales experience. Timing matters: settlement-time surveys capture relief bias, while touchpoint surveys expose process pain. Read with cycle time and reopen rates, since slow-and-painful books rarely score well for long.

## Formula

```
CSAT-C = Average Claimant Satisfaction Score
```
