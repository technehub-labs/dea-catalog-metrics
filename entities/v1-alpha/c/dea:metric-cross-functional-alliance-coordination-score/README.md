## 2. Definition

A Alliances & Partnerships Management indicator, Cross-Functional Alliance Coordination Score (CAACS) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Internal Team Alignment Survey per Alliance, expressed in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

CAACS = Internal Team Alignment Survey per Alliance
