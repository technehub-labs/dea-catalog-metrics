## 2. Definition

A Education & Training (Deep Dive) indicator, Course Completion Rate (Deep Dive) (CCRT-D) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. It is defined as Completers / Enrolments; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. In Education, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of enrolled learners completing courses. It is the persistence metric of education delivery, and it is stratified by modality, cohort, and life circumstance - completion for working adults differs structurally from full-time students. Completion improvements achieved by diluting assessment standards are degree inflation, not success. Read with assessment pass rate (Deep Dive) and time-to-completion distributions.

## Formula

```
CCRT-D = Completers / Enrolments
```
