## 2. Definition

End-to-End Service Accountability Coverage (EESAC) captures governance for organizations tracking operating model monitoring & design effectiveness. The formula is Services with Single Accountable Executive / Services - reported in percent (%). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of services with a single accountable owner across all contributing functions. Siloed accountability is why customers experience the seams. Coverage defined as escalation-path existence rather than genuine authority is weak. Read with process ownership coverage and service level objective attainment.

## Formula

```
Services with Single Accountable Executive / Services
```
