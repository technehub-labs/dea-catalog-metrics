## 2. Definition

Property Viewing to Offer Rate (V2O) captures how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome for organizations tracking real estate brokerage. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. The formula is Offers Made / Viewings Conducted - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Real estate sector.

## Formula

```
Offers Made / Viewings Conducted
```
