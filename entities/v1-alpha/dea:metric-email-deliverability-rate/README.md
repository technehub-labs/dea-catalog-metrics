## 2. Definition

Email Deliverability Rate (EDR) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking marketing. The formula is Emails Delivered / Emails Sent - reported in percent (%). It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Emails Delivered / Emails Sent
```
