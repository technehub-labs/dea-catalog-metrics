## 2. Definition

A Channel & Distribution Management indicator, Direct-to-Channel Sales Ratio (DCSR) quantifies strategy. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It is defined as Direct Sales / Channel Sales; the result is stated in ratio (x:1 or x). The objective is neither maximization nor minimization but staying within an agreed operating band. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the balance of direct versus partner-sourced revenue. The ratio is a strategic statement about cost-to-serve and market coverage. Channel conflict from poor ratio management poisons the whole program. Read with channel revenue mix balance and cost of sale by channel.

## Formula

```
Direct Sales / Channel Sales
```
