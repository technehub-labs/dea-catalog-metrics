## 2. Definition

Within Insurance Distribution - Direct & Digital, Pay-How-You-Drive Telematics Uptake (PHYD) is used to gauge growth over time. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Telematics Policies / Motor Policies, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It originates from and is most used in Insurance practice.

## Formula

