## 2. Definition

Within Balanced Scorecard - Internal Process Perspective, Regulatory Process Compliance Rate (Deep Dive) (RPCR-D) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its calculation is Process Audits Passed / Audits, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures adherence of core processes to applicable regulatory requirements. Regulatory process failures convert into fines and license risk, not just rework. Compliance measured by attestation rather than control testing is thin evidence. Read with regulatory finding closure rate and assurance map coverage.

## Formula

```
Process Audits Passed / Audits
```
