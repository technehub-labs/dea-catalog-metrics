## 2. Definition

Within Digital Transformation, Digital Talent Coverage (DTC) is used to gauge capability maturity or development. It is defined as Roles Filled with Digital Skills / Roles Requiring Digital Skills; the result is stated in percent (%). Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of critical digital roles - product management, data science, modern engineering - that are filled with adequate capability versus demand. It is the binding constraint on most transformation roadmaps: plans without talent are fiction. Watch for role inflation (everyone becomes a product manager) and coverage measured against a job-description census rather than the actual skill depth of the portfolio. Read with time to digital experiment and attrition in digital teams.

## Formula

```
Roles Filled with Digital Skills / Roles Requiring Digital Skills
```
