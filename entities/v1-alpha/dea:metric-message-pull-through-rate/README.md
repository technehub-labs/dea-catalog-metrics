## 2. Definition

A Corporate Communications & PR indicator, Message Pull-Through Rate (MPTR) quantifies how effectively a process or entity achieves its intended outcome. It is computed as Coverage Containing Key Messages / Total Coverage, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Message pull-through measures the share of earned coverage that contains the intended message or language, and it is the bridge between communications activity and actual narrative influence - coverage volume without pull-through is visibility without persuasion. It is measured through content analysis of headlines, quotes, and key-phrase presence, and it exposes the common failure mode of high-volume campaigns that generate mentions while communicating nothing. Pull-through varies by message type: financial messages pull through business press more readily than product messages. Read with share of voice (pull-through times share of relevant coverage gives true narrative presence), sentiment among pulled-through items, and spokesperson quote adoption.

## Formula

```
Coverage Containing Key Messages / Total Coverage
```
