## 2. Definition

A Facility Management & Workplace (Deep Dive) indicator, Waste Diversion Rate (Facilities Deep Dive) (WDR-D) quantifies environmental and social sustainability performance. Its calculation is Diverted Waste / Total Waste, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Diverted Waste / Total Waste
```
