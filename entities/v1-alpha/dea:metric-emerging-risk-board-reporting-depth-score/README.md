## 2. Definition

Emerging Risk Board Reporting Depth Score (ERBRD) measures governance in the Emerging Risk & Horizon Scanning domain. It is defined as Rubric of Board Discussion Quality on Emerging Risks; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether board-level emerging risk reporting carries sufficient insight to enable governance. Reporting depth is the difference between informing and equipping the board. Deep reports that arrive post-event are archives. Read with executive threat briefing cadence and risk governance board engagement.

ERBRD = Rubric of Board Discussion Quality on Emerging Risks
