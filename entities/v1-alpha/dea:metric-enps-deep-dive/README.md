## 2. Definition

A Employee Engagement & Culture (Deep Dive) indicator, eNPS (Deep Dive) (ENPSDD) quantifies the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. Its calculation is Employee Promoters minus Detractors, measured in score (points). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures employees' willingness to recommend the organization as a place to work. It is the engagement question reduced to one honest number. eNPS moves faster than satisfaction scores and reads direction first. Read with employee engagement score (Deep Dive) and Glassdoor rating trend.

## Formula

```
Employee Promoters minus Detractors
```
