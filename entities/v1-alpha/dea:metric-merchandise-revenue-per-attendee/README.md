## 2. Definition

Merchandise Revenue per Attendee (MRPA) measures the economic value created or preserved in the Sports & Events domain. It is computed as Merchandise Revenue / Attendance, expressed in currency per unit. Its use is concentrated in Sports & events. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures merchandise spend per event attendee. It matters because merchandising is a high-margin revenue stream that scales with fan passion, not just attendance. Discounting can lift per-capita spend while destroying margin. Read with Merchandise Gross Margin and Fan Engagement Rate (Digital) to confirm growth is both high and profitable.

## Formula

```
Merchandise Revenue / Attendance
```
