## 2. Definition

Operating Expense Ratio (Property) (OER-P) captures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs for organizations tracking real estate portfolio & property management. It is defined as Operating Expenses / Effective Gross Income; the result is stated in percent (%). In Real Estate, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## 3. Context

Measures property operating costs against revenue. It is the efficiency and recoverability gauge of asset management: high ratios erode net operating income and, in leases with expense stops, shift costs to tenants that then churn. Recoverability matters as much as level, since unrecoverable opex is the true economic drag. Read with net operating income trends and capex deferral evidence.

## Formula

```
Operating Expenses / Effective Gross Income
```
