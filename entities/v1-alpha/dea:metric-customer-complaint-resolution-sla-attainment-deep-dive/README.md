## 2. Definition

A Balanced Scorecard - Customer Perspective indicator, Customer Complaint Resolution SLA Attainment (Deep Dive) (CCRS-D) quantifies the quality of outputs and outcomes. Its calculation is Complaints Resolved within SLA / Complaints, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
CCRS-D = Complaints Resolved within SLA / Complaints
```
