## 2. Definition

IP Litigation Cost Exposure Ratio (ILCER) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of intellectual property & licensing reporting. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Litigation Spend and Reserves / Revenue, expressed in percent (%). It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures potential IP litigation cost relative to capacity to absorb it. Litigation exposure is the risk number that makes IP strategy real. Exposure computed on asserted patents only ignores the demand-letter tail. Read with IP enforcement action success rate and legal cost as percent of revenue.

## Formula

```
Litigation Spend and Reserves / Revenue
```
