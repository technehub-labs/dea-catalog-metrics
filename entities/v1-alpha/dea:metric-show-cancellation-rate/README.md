## 2. Definition

Show Cancellation Rate (SCR-V) addresses exposure to adverse outcomes and is a fixture of entertainment venues & attractions reporting. It is computed as Shows Cancelled / Shows Scheduled, expressed in percent (%). Its use is concentrated in Media & Entertainment. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Shows Cancelled / Shows Scheduled
```
