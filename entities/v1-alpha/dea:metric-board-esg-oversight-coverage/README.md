## 2. Definition

Board ESG Oversight Coverage (BEOC) measures governance in the Environmental, Social & Governance Reporting domain. It is defined as ESG Topics Reviewed by Board / Required Topics; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether board committees have defined oversight of material ESG risks, with documented cadence and competence. Governance is the connective tissue between ESG risk and enterprise risk management: regulators and investors test whether oversight is structured and informed, and coverage metrics make the informal formal. Coverage without escalation and decision evidence is decorative. Read with board minutes trail on material ESG decisions and director training completion.

## Formula

```
ESG Topics Reviewed by Board / Required Topics
```
