## 2. Definition

A Public Sector & Government indicator, Transparency Score (Open Data) (TSOD) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Datasets Published in Open Format / Datasets Identified - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Public sector sector.

## 3. Context

Measures completeness and quality of open data publication against standards. It matters because transparency drives civic trust, economic reuse, and accountability. Scores can be inflated by publishing data portals that are technically open but stale or unusable. Read with Open Data Usage statistics and data quality complaints to confirm publication meets real consumption needs.

## Formula

```
TSOD = Datasets Published in Open Format / Datasets Identified
```
