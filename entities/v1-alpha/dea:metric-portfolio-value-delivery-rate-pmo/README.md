## 2. Definition

Portfolio Value Delivery Rate (PMO) (PVDR) measures the economic value created or preserved in the PMO & Portfolio Governance domain. It is defined as Benefits Realized vs Business Case / Business Case; the result is stated in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of portfolio spend converted into realized business value, PMO-governed. It is the core justification for portfolio governance itself. Value scoring dominated by project self-assessment inflates the rate. Read with benefits realization rate and portfolio outcome review cadence compliance.

## Formula

```
Benefits Realized vs Business Case / Business Case
```
