## 2. Definition

Within Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring, Strategic Initiative Decision Latency (SIDL) is used to gauge how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It is computed as Days Strategic Decisions Wait beyond Target, expressed in days. It should be driven down, since lower readings signal healthier performance. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures how long strategic initiatives wait for the decisions they need from leadership. Latency is pure drag on execution speed. Self-reported latency from initiative managers may skew low to protect relationships. Read with decision cycle time - strategic decisions and demand intake to funding cycle time.

## Formula

```
Days Strategic Decisions Wait beyond Target
```
