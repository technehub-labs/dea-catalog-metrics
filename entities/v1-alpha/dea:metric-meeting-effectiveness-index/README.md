## 2. Definition

Meeting Effectiveness Index (MEI-GOV) measures how efficiently resources are converted into outputs in the Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring domain. The formula is Composite Meeting Value Survey Score - reported in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.
