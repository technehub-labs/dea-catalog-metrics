## 2. Definition

A Workforce Planning & Productivity indicator, Contingent Workforce Ratio (Deep Dive) (CWR-WF) quantifies the utilization or availability of capacity. Its calculation is Contingent Workers / Total Workforce, measured in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of the workforce that is non-permanent. Contingent labor converts fixed cost to variable, but high ratios concentrate capability and knowledge outside. Ratios that exclude SOW consultants understate the reality. Read with revenue per FTE (Deep Dive) and independent contractor compliance rate.

## Formula

```
Contingent Workers / Total Workforce
```
