## 2. Definition

A Elder & Social Care indicator, Time Spent on Direct Care Ratio (TSDC) quantifies the utilization or availability of capacity. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. The formula is Direct Care Hours / Paid Hours - reported in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. It is a standard measure in the Social care sector.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of carer time spent with care recipients versus administration. It matters because administrative overload directly steals care hours in a stretched sector. Documentation shortcuts to improve the ratio can compromise care records and compliance. Read with Care Plan Review Compliance and staff turnover to confirm the balance is healthy.

## Formula

```
Direct Care Hours / Paid Hours
```
