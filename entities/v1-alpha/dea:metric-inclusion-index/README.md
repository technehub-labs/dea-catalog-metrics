## 2. Definition

Within Culture & Employee Experience, Inclusion Index (INCI) is used to gauge the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved. It is computed as Weighted Index of Belonging and Fairness Survey Items, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures employees' felt sense of belonging, voice, and fair treatment, complementing representation metrics that only count heads. It matters because diverse headcount without inclusion yields no performance dividend and drives diverse attrition. The index is survey-based and vulnerable to fear-driven overstatement in low-trust environments, so trend by anonymity assurances. Read with workforce diversity representation by level and retention parity ratio.

## Formula

```
Weighted Index of Belonging and Fairness Survey Items
```
