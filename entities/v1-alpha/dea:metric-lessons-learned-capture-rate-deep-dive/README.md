## 2. Definition

Lessons Learned Capture Rate (Deep Dive) (LLCR-PM) addresses capability maturity or development and is a fixture of project management (deep dive) reporting. Its calculation is Projects Documenting Lessons Learned / Closed Projects, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the share of completed projects documenting lessons into a reusable repository. Capture is the necessary first step of organizational learning. Lessons recorded but unindexed and unread change nothing downstream. Read with lessons learned reuse rate and estimation bias calibration index.

## Formula

```
Projects Documenting Lessons Learned / Closed Projects
```
