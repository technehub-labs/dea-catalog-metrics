## 2. Definition

A Threat Intelligence & Red Team indicator, Executive Threat Briefing Cadence (ETBC) quantifies governance. The formula is Threat Briefings Delivered to Leadership per Quarter - reported in count (items per period). It carries weight in boardroom and executive reviews, where it anchors cross-organization trade-offs. It is managed toward a defined target band rather than an extreme - both overshoot and undershoot can signal problems. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether leadership receives threat and risk briefings on the committed schedule. Informed executives make risk-acceptance decisions; uninformed ones make discoveries later. Briefings without decision-forcing content are briefings as theater. Read with security governance reporting compliance and tabletop exercise completion rate.

## Formula

Threat Briefings Delivered to Leadership per Quarter
