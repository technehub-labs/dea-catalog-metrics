## 2. Definition

Cross-Sell Attachment Rate (Insurance) (CSAR-I) captures growth over time for organizations tracking insurance distribution & agency management. The formula is Policies with Additional Coverages / Policies - reported in percent (%). It is a standard measure in the Insurance sector. It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Policies with Additional Coverages / Policies
```
