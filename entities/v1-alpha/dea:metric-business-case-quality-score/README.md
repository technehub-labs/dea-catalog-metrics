## 2. Definition

Business Case Quality Score (BCQS) captures the quality of outputs and outcomes for organizations tracking pmo & portfolio governance. Its calculation is Rubric-Based Completeness and Rigor of Business Cases, measured in score (points). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures whether business cases meet evidence standards: defined benefits, credible baselines, and risk transparency. Business case quality predicts downstream benefits realization better than any execution metric. Quality scored by the case author's peers is gentle. Read with benefits realization rate and estimate accuracy - cost.

## Formula

```
Rubric-Based Completeness and Rigor of Business Cases
```
