## 2. Definition

Decision Quality Audit Score (DQAS-GOV) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Enterprise Decision Effectiveness & Governance Monitoring domain. It is defined as Composite Evidence, Options, and Outcome Score; the result is stated in score (points). It is applied across virtually every sector. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures whether decisions followed a quality process: framed, alternatives explored, information fit for purpose. Process quality predicts decision outcomes far better than single outcomes do. Audits scored by the decision owners themselves defeat the purpose. Read with decision reversal rate and management information freshness score.

## Formula

Composite Evidence, Options, and Outcome Score
