## 2. Definition

Within Market Structure & Economics Standard Measures, Four-Firm Concentration Ratio (FFCR) is used to gauge exposure to adverse outcomes. Its calculation is Combined Share of Top Four Competitors, measured in percent (%). It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Lower values are better; each reduction is an improvement. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## 3. Context

Measures the combined market share of the top four players. It summarizes industry structure and competitive intensity. Ratios computed on national aggregates mislead in regional markets. Read with relative market share and market share (Deep Dive).

## Formula

```
Combined Share of Top Four Competitors
```
