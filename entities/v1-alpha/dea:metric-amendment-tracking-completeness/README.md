## 2. Definition

Within Contract Lifecycle Management, Amendment Tracking Completeness (ATC-CL) is used to gauge the quality of outputs and outcomes. It is computed as Amendments Linked to Master Contract / Amendments, expressed in percent (%). Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
ATC-CL = Amendments Linked to Master Contract / Amendments
```
