## 2. Definition

A Pharma R&D & Clinical Trials indicator, Patent Filing Success Rate (Pharma) (PFSR) quantifies the economic value created or preserved. It is defined as Granted Patents / Filed Patents; the result is stated in percent (%). In Pharmaceuticals, it is a recognized element of the standard performance toolkit. Its trend is watched by senior leadership as an early signal of structural performance shifts. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## 3. Context

Measures grant rates on patent filings protecting molecules, formulations, and indications. It matters because commercial life post-genericization depends on the strength and breadth of the patent estate, and grant outcomes shape litigation risk for the asset. Be careful about scope erosion: granted-but-narrow patents are successes that protect little. Read with time-to-generic estimates, Orange Book listing strategy, and litigation outcomes.

## Formula

```
PFSR = Granted Patents / Filed Patents
```
