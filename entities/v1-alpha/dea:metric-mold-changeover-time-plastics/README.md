## 2. Definition

A Packaging & Plastics indicator, Mold Changeover Time (Plastics) (MCT-P) quantifies how efficiently resources are converted into outputs. The formula is Minutes from Last Good Part to Next Good Part - reported in minutes. It is a standard measure in the Manufacturing sector. It is monitored close to the work: teams act on it within a shift, sprint, or daily review. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
MCT-P = Minutes from Last Good Part to Next Good Part
```
