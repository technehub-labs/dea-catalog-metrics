## 2. Definition

Internal Network Connectivity Index (INCI) addresses the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved and is a fixture of employee engagement & culture (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Breadth of Cross-Functional Collaborative Ties per Employee, expressed in index (0-100 or normalized). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is reviewed at cadence by planning owners to correct course before issues become strategic. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Breadth of Cross-Functional Collaborative Ties per Employee
```
