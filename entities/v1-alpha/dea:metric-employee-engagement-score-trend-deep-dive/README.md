## 2. Definition

Employee Engagement Score Trend (Deep Dive) (EEST-D) measures the engagement or satisfaction of the people involved in the Balanced Scorecard - Learning & Growth Perspective domain. It informs long-horizon decisions and is typically tracked on enterprise dashboards rather than team boards. Its calculation is Change in Engagement Score between Periods, measured in score (points). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## 3. Context

Measures the direction of engagement over time. Trend matters: declining engagement predicts attrition and quality erosion quarters ahead. Survey fatigue and response bias can flatten the trend artificially. Read with critical talent retention rate and team happiness index.

Change in Engagement Score between Periods
