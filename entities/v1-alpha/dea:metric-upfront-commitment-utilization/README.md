## 2. Definition

Upfront Commitment Utilization (UCU-A) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of advertising & media buying reporting. Its calculation is Upfront Inventory Delivered / Committed, measured in percent (%). Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. It originates from and is most used in Media & Entertainment practice.

## Formula

```
Upfront Inventory Delivered / Committed
```
