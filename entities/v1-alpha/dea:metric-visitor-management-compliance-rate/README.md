## 2. Definition

Visitor Management Compliance Rate (VMCR) addresses conformance to standards, plans, or obligations and is a fixture of facility management & workplace (deep dive) reporting. It is computed as Visitors Processed per Policy / Visitors, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Visitors Processed per Policy / Visitors
```
