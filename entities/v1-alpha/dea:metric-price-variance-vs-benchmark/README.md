## 2. Definition

Within Strategic Sourcing & Supplier Performance (Deep Dive), Price Variance vs Benchmark (PVB-SS) is used to gauge the economic value created or preserved. It is computed as Negotiated Price vs Market Benchmark / Benchmark, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. It is a headline indicator: movement in it prompts leadership questions about strategy and resourcing. Values outside the intended band, on either side, warrant investigation.

## 3. Context

Measures the gap between prices paid and market benchmarks for comparable goods. Variance exposes where negotiating performance is below market. Benchmarks must adjust for specification, volume, and geography to be fair. Read with savings realization rate (Sourcing) and price realization rate (Market).

## Formula

```
Negotiated Price vs Market Benchmark / Benchmark
```
