## 2. Definition

Within Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery, Vital Records Protection Coverage (VRPC) is used to gauge conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Its calculation is Vital Records Stored per Retention and Protection Standard / Vital Records, measured in percent (%). Improvement means raising this figure toward best-in-class levels. Practitioners in nearly any industry can adopt it directly.

## Formula

```
Vital Records Stored per Retention and Protection Standard / Vital Records
```
