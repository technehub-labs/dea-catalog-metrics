## 2. Definition

A Regulatory Affairs & Compliance Operations indicator, Regulatory Findings Closure Rate (RFCR) quantifies conformance to standards, plans, or obligations. The formula is Findings Closed by Deadline / Findings Issued - reported in percent (%). It guides mid-horizon planning: portfolio, program, and function owners act on it between review cycles. Higher values are better; gains are read as improvement and losses as deterioration. Its use is not sector-specific; it appears in cross-industry performance frameworks.

## Formula

```
Findings Closed by Deadline / Findings Issued
```
