## 2. Definition

Hazardous Waste per Tonne Output (HWTO) measures environmental and social sustainability performance in the Chemicals & Specialty Materials domain. Its calculation is Hazardous Waste Generated / Production Tonnes, measured in ratio (x:1 or x). It originates from and is most used in Chemicals practice. Its value drives prioritization and resource reallocation among competing initiatives. Reduction is the goal, and sustained decreases indicate successful intervention.

## Formula

```
Hazardous Waste Generated / Production Tonnes
```
