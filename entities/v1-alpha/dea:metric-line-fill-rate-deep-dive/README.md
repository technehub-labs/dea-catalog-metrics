## 2. Definition

Line Fill Rate (Deep Dive) (LFRDD) measures the quality of outputs and outcomes in the Supply Chain Standard Measures (Deep Dive) domain. It is computed as Order Lines Filled Completely / Lines Ordered, expressed in percent (%). It is applied across virtually every sector. Front-line and delivery teams watch it in real time, as small deviations demand immediate correction. It should be maximized within the limits of cost and quality trade-offs.

## Formula

```
Order Lines Filled Completely / Lines Ordered
```
